# Competitor research loop for the Minecraft animation story niche (OWNER rules, 2026-11-03)

> **2026-11-14 (OWNER):** the same A–D loop now covers the five locked lanes listed in `config/research/locked_lanes.json`, using the general rules in `config/research/lane_research_rules.md`. This file and `minecraft_story_watchlist.txt` remain the history of the earlier Bin/Bo/Zu animation-story lane.

Applies to the Bin & Bo & Zu channel (US market, long-form). Run it every day and append the new rows to `data/research/raw/<date>_minecraft_story.json`. The watchlist is `config/research/minecraft_story_watchlist.txt`.

**Aesthetic standard.** Judge pictures, lighting, camera work, thumbnails and pacing against outlier videos from competitors, not against personal taste.

## A. Niche leaders (on the watchlist)
- Study the most popular videos, the newest videos, and the outliers.
- A video counts as an outlier if any of these hold:
  - it is ≤ 1 month old and has far more views than the channel's other videos (≥ 3× the channel median);
  - its views are many times its subscriber count;
  - it is exploding in its first 5 days.

## B. New challengers
- Channels created 0–30 days ago. Check the creation date on the About page ("Joined"); if the page doesn't show it, label the date INFERRED.
- Requirement: ≥ 2 long videos with ≥ 1,000,000 views. Study those videos and add the channel to the watchlist.
- Once a channel is on the list, it follows the old-competitor rules:
  - views many times its subscriber count, or
  - a video ≤ 15 days old with far more views than its other videos, or
  - exploding in its first 5 days.

## C. Exploding videos
- Use many keywords that match the format.
- Prioritise videos 0–15 days old with ≥ 1M views, and check their growth speed (views/day).
- The higher views ÷ subscribers is (several, tens, or hundreds of times), the stronger the trend signal, especially on small channels.

## D. Every day
- Add new competitors every day.
- Turn what you learn into principles, kept apart as:
  - **long-term principles** (stable, used repeatedly);
  - **short-term principles / trends** (may change; each one records its start date, evidence, and when it was last seen).
- Goal: make videos with breakout views, ideally ahead of the trend.
- Principles live in `reports/research/minecraft_story_principles.md`.

## Limits
- Public metadata only. Browse at a human pace or use the official YouTube Data API.
- Do not download videos.
- Never copy characters, names, scripts, music or footage. Learn the formula; the content must be our own.
- Label every number OBSERVED (with the date it was seen) or INFERRED.
