## Non-negotiable style and QA
1. **Visual:** vivid, glossy, very high-quality original 3D CGI suitable for a kids' yoga animation series, with vivid eyes, lively faces, carefully designed hair, headwear/accessories and trendy garments. Modern, varied fashion — not all skirts, not all joggers, not just recolors.
2. **Outfit policy:** EACH character has **Yoga / Casual / Street / Sport / Chill**, coherent identity and distinct fabrics, layers, cuts, patterns, color palettes and personal icons. One single character per final deliverable; multi-character collage is not a valid substitute for individual portraits.
3. **Yoga footwear:** **NO SNEAKERS / SHOES / BOOTS on ANY yoga mat**, across hero poses and small thumbnails alike. Use the character's own themed **non-slip yoga grip socks**, snugly fitted, safe decorative elements, visible rubberized traction pattern on underside. Casual/street/stage images off the mat may show shoes.
4. **Reference consistency:** face proportions, hair strands and silhouette, eye color where known, hairstyle, hair clips, accessories, color story and motif all checked across angles, outfits and future episodes.
5. **Character sheet required:** independent full-resolution head-to-toe hero, front, side, back, 3/4, expressions, hair/accessory macros, five outfit variants, yoga pose panels, sock top/sole closeups. Do not trust tiny autogenerated captions/labels without manual verification.
6. **Safety:** comfortable age-appropriate athletic clothing and body proportions; no adult body styling, unsafe contortion or implausible limbs. Exercise poses reviewed by a qualified movement/yoga instructor before use as instructional content. Platform child-directed rules need separate review.
7. **Approval:** New outputs start `CONCEPT_ONLY`. No promotion to `APPROVED_CANON` without verified actual full-res file, file path and checksum, a specific owner approval and passing independent image QC. Do not invent assets or approvals.
