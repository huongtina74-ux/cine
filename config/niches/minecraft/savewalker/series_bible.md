## 1. Logline
Something lives in the moment a Minecraft world saves. It walks between autosaves, and everything you did since the
last save is something it can take back.

## 2. Why it can stand out (INFERRED from the research)
- It is built on a real game mechanic every player knows (Java autosaves every 6,000 ticks ≈ 5 min, "Saving world"),
  not on a borrowed monster. Viewers can test the "rules" in their own worlds → comments, theories, re-watches.
- The rules give a natural escalation ladder for a season and a clear series identity, while every episode and
  case file has a different premise, mechanic and twist (inauthentic-content policy: no template stories).
- Horror-lite: dread, absence, things undone. No gore, no killing of characters on screen.

## 3. The rules of SAVEWALKER (canon — never break silently)
1. **It moves only during a save.** Between saves it is frozen wherever it last stood. (Tension clock: the autosave countdown.)
2. **It can undo anything you changed since the last save** — blocks placed or broken, items moved, doors opened.
3. **It cannot undo writing.** Signs and books stay. Players talk to it through signs; it "answers" by undoing.
4. **Every undo leaves one wrong block** — a single block that was never there (gravel in a wall, a cobweb in a chest
   room). Viewers learn to hunt for it. (Clue device; one per episode, sometimes hidden in thumbnails.)
5. **It cannot enter chunks that have never been saved.** New land is safe — until episode 3, when it learns to follow.
6. **The deepest undo is the player.** Its endgame is to restore the world to the moment before you joined.

Escalation per season: blocks → items → whole days → the player.

## 4. Characters (all original)
- **Wren** (username `wren_0419`): solo survival player, practical, tests things (tripwires, item counts, signs).
  Mostly silent; speaks through an on-screen journal and chat. Skin: our own 64×64 design (to draw).
- **The Archivist**: unseen narrator who receives corrupted world files and "opens" them for the viewer (case files,
  recaps in FULL MOVIE bridges). The only regular voice → one consistent narrator voice (Kokoro English or a human VO;
  owner decision).
- **SAVEWALKER**: tall, thin figure of grey "unloaded-chunk" texture; face is a missing texture tile. Never fully lit.
  Original model (Blockbench), animated with BBS. No resemblance to Herobrine, Verity, Leviathan or any creator's entity.

## 6. Case files (stand-alone, ~13 min, Archivist-led)
Each = one world file, one rule, one hidden link to SAVEWALKER (a file named `*.bak`, a wrong block, a sign).
- **Case 01 — This World Is Only 16 Blocks Wide**: the border sits 8 blocks from spawn; a figure stands at the edge.
- **Case 02 — The Seed That Has No Night**: the sun never moves; mobs spawn anyway, facing the player.
- **Case 03 — He Never Logged Out of World 88**: the player list still shows `kestrel_88`, ten years later.
- **Case 04 — The Village That Rebuilds Itself**: every house you break is whole again at the next save.
