# Bin, Bo & Zu — series bible v1 (2026-11-11, working titles; owner approval pending)

Owner decision (2026-11-10, OWNER): Minecraft niche for huge, sustainable traffic. One channel, an original character universe telling survival and adventure stories (Maizen model). Tone: all ages (families watch together), with real suspense; not aimed at young kids.

## Core engine
- **Cast:**
  - Bin (older brother, planner, brave)
  - Bo (younger brother, joker, builder)
  - Zu, a baby zombie the brothers secretly adopted. Zu is the only zombie who can think and feel. Design direction: "cute but eerie".
- **Season-1 question:** where did the zombies come from, and why is Zu different? (Zu's lost memories → the "Hollow" source.)
- **Every episode** has: a new place, a new threat, a build or protect goal, a cost, and one reveal about Zu.
- **Distinctness rule** (YouTube inauthentic-content policy): place, antagonist, goal and ending must differ from the previous 3 episodes. Log each in the continuity ledger.

## Production rules
- Titles and thumbnails show what is really in the episode. Never say "players" for NPCs.
- Thumbnails and the first 7 s: no blood or gore; zombies stay cartoonish.
- Music and SFX are made by the studio. No Minecraft soundtrack.
- Description carries the Mojang disclaimer. "Minecraft" is never the main word in the channel or series name.
- Nobody can guarantee views (INFERRED). Each episode is judged on 28-day data: CTR, average view duration, audience age, traffic source.
