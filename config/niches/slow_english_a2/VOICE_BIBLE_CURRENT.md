1. Preserve original voice registry aliases (`bo`, `me`, `juno`, `Lia`, `bubu`, `bix`, `ongngoai`, `bangoai`, `ongnoi`, `banoi`) in runtime for backward compatibility. Map Arthur/Rose/Elara only at display/casting level.

3. Store provider, model version, real voice ID or authorized source WAV, source SHA256, approved timestamp, authorized reviewer. Until accepted, do not fill or pretend non-null voice identifiers.
4. Use identical voice source for EVERY shot including the 18-shot trailer, whether speech is generated in Muse or post-synced. Never trust labels like VOICE_MARA to force a Muse model speaker identity.
5. For masked identity: audio post should use original actor's master voice with one consistent EQ/mask FX and retain provenance; private reveal linkage does not belong in public story notes.
6. QC timing, en-US intelligibility, A2 phrasing, emotion differentiation, and word-level lip-sync; rejected voices should not be labeled LOCKED.
