## D: Learning opportunities (hypotheses vs durable mechanisms)

*Likely reusable long-horizon creative MECHANICS, pending multiple independent full AV studies:*
1. A concrete challenge clearly explained at opening, a finite or measurable goal, escalating difficulty, solution with causality and visual payoff (100 days expanding world, one block, noob/pro building).
2. An impossible/unusual space to build or hide in (older Maizen bed-based hit; new JJ & Mikey Adventures toilet video): repeated cross-channel click promise, but should be original in locations/characters/story.
3. Serialized character relations and consistent visual identity: adventure, tension, transformations, plot question; animation-first production can exploit reusable rigs, sets and animations.
4. Concrete contrast in packaging: NOOB vs PRO, POOR vs RICH, GIANT vs TINY, vs monsters; more useful when genuinely grounded in video story rather than clickbait.

*Volatile 2026 seasonal/topic signals, not proven permanent:*
- Verity/Curiosity, monster transformation / sad origin, web superhero crossover, brand-associated "JJ sister" drama, secret home inside weird objects. Duration of appeal and freshness differ; reassess via next weekly/monthly cohorts, independent competitors and decay in video-level view snapshots.

*Risk and business controls:*
- JJ & Mikey and other Maizen character likenesses, Pokémon and Spider-Man require separate rights analysis. Do not auto-copy thumbnails, character skins, footage, music or voices. Prefer original owned characters and original concept variants.
- External RPM, CTR, retention, revenue, traffic source, channel organic purity are UNAVAILABLE from these sources; views/sub is a discovery ranking signal only.
- Do not move a format to production approval without full native video/audio scrutiny of winners, originality checks, cost-per-accepted-minute experiments and owner gate.
