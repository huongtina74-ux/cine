# 1 — THE EMOTIONAL PROMISE
Orin is not primarily searching for three family members; as Veylora's Keeper he is responsible for **finding and helping ALL dispersed people**, one real contact at a time. Mara searches for mother and brothers as one intensely personal part of the humanitarian mission. On Earth she builds a human family and a sanctuary. The question is both: "How can everyone find a safe home?" and "What actually makes magic strong?"

**Core theme:** Magic is strongest when life can renew itself; trying to OWN or EXTRACT all its power destroys the very world that sustains it.
**English A2 series promise:** "Our old world is losing its magic. Then the bells break, and our people disappear. We find a new home on Earth. Now every new door can help us bring someone home."

# 2 — MAGIC ECOLOGY: WHY VEYLORA WAS DYING EVEN BEFORE THE DISASTER
**Proposed physical world rule:** Veylora's magical energy, the **Living Current** (Vietnamese: Mạch Sống), circulates between the land, roots, air, water, living creatures, the witches' spells and Gate Bells. Ordinary living cycles USED TO replenish the Current. Generations of increased portal traffic, centralized power conversion and destructive collection take more than the landscape regenerates. Symptoms appear gradually: healing weaker, once-glowing plants fading, long-bell delays, intermittent strange clouds, creatures changing behavior, some children unable to use simple familiar charms.
- **Visible clues:** older trees are losing one luminous ring every decade, old castle lamps flicker, a flower blooms fully near a stream but withers in the drained Tower area. Orin can measure ring stability; Elara noticed life-cycle changes before he did; Rowan and Cael hold distinct records of evacuation.
- **Scientific story question:** Is magic running out everywhere, or is Veylora's way of USING magic broken? Initial citizen theories vary. This is not answered before the disaster.
- **Exploration is organized and justifiable:** Orin and other bell experts establish PUBLIC gate stations in worlds with useful ecosystems/resources and send competent teams to study foreign magic, environmental regeneration, safe materials and community knowledge. Some expeditions establish peaceful exchanges; some worlds deny entry. A world is NOT solely a place to strip of raw energy.
- **Plot pressure:** The Council fears irreversible decline and argues over centralized fixes, quotas, public transparency and risky new gate experiments. The emergency research program creates the very technical access the saboteur later abuses.

# 3 — THE CATASTROPHE: CAUSE, DIFFERENT DISPLACEMENT MODES & EMERGENCY SEAL
**Known truth to the family:** During a scheduled investigation of the weakening Living Current, a central Bell Tower experiment/operation destabilizes world resonance; earthquakes of light and multiple doorways appear. Mass emergency orders go out. The Great Tower's bells ring out of sequence and a large rupture opens.
**Showrunner confidential direction (not culprit's name in PUBLIC document):** There is DELIBERATE MISUSE/SABOTAGE of a central resonance/route system as a cause of the acute failure, distinct from generations of decline. One figure wanted to centralize extraction/control as a dangerous purported solution. The culprit and exact role of any manipulated technician are kept in a private writers' file; do not blame a random accident once this evidence is established.

**DISPLACEMENT MODE K — KNOWN WORLD DOORS:** People near ordinary explored gate stations are sucked through already INSTALLED doorways as their bells ring uncontrollably; land in a recognized world. Their route back is destroyed/unstable and their Gate Bell may be inaccessible. Not all know the world code or how to repair a station.
**DISPLACEMENT MODE U — UNKNOWN-WORLD FRACTURES:** New uncharted rifts appear outside stations; people are sucked into worlds NEVER connected by official bells, often without any return station at the destination. Neither ordinary gate spells nor charts can return them.
**VEY LORA SEAL:** Elara triggers a ONE-TIME self-maintaining emergency isolation mechanism to stop cascading ruptures and the destruction of linked worlds. **She is then swept into an UNKNOWN world in the evacuation.** She is not holding the barrier on Veylora for years. Her fate is genuinely uncertain at beginning; one writer proposal places her in an uncharted island world. Orin's elder sons split: eldest Rowan via a mapped coastal gate cut off after disaster, second Cael via an unknown forest rift; working names/destinations, require approval.
**ORIN + MARA EARTH LANDING:** A desperate last protective spell from Orin toward Mara creates one linked resonance across their separated rupture trajectories. They land at TWO DIFFERENT places on the SAME unknown world, Earth, and find each other after struggle. This avoids a statistically arbitrary coincidence and doesn't require a secret preexisting Earth gate, targeted Earth exile or magic family GPS. The spell only influences their final shared direction amid a unique surge; it is NOT a repeatable route or a global family locator.
**AFTER:** The source Veylora gateway remains unreachable; no one knows whether the homeland has collapsed utterly or what people remained there. The big fissures quickly close; they are NOT permanent two-way corridors available for easy recovery.

# 4 — EARTH'S MAGIC RENEWAL & ITS RISK
Earth was not on the original map. Humans live apparently without wizard society, but Earth's living ecosystems have a pattern of **natural renewal** rather than continuous concentrated extraction. For witches with damaged Living Current, being near balanced life gradually restores their innate magical stamina, plant companions and weaker spells. The environment is not an infinite "magic battery," humans need not see neon magic, and not all Earth cities are equally healthy.
**Why Orin/Mara remain:**
- Earth restores an essential part of their depleted ability, strongly motivating scientific research for Veylora's cure.
- Orin lacks the route home and can still explore reachable worlds piecemeal, seeking scattered people and credible proof of what the homeland needs.
- Mara chooses a home with her Earth spouse Evan and children Juno/Lia. She maintains a secret workshop near a stable natural resonance place, and balances Earth life, research, rescue and unknown-world adventures. Her magic is more stable on Earth, but her responsibilities are not erased.
- Witches brought to Earth voluntarily may recover and form safe communities. **NEW ESTABLISHED LOCATION DIRECTION:** an evolving protected **Earth sanctuary zone** close enough to logistics in Bellfern but separate from Mara's crowded family home. Start very small (one safe room/house and garden), gradually expand only with housing, water, food, medical care, guardian arrangements, safety, human noninterference and local consent. The sanctuary is not an entire hidden kingdom that appeared overnight. Later Earth/human-world political exposure is a real story conflict; no consequence-free mass influx.
- Survivors who don't want Earth, or whose worlds cannot reach it safely, deserve aid, contact and options. Some retain homes in host worlds. Underleaf/Bellhaven, if established later, is an optional auxiliary partner, NOT primary base.

# 5 — TWO WAYS OF CROSSING: NORMAL GATES AND BUBU'S LIVING PORTALS
## A. Ordinary established Gate Stations (before & after)
At an existing mapped world's fixed Gate Bell, a permitted traveler **says a short destination spell and RINGS THAT GATE'S FIXED BELL**. The destination station must exist, be recognized and sufficiently responsive; a passage opens for people to walk through when operating. No one has to carry a personal bell. Orin may use an expert's handbell as diagnostic/calibration gear, not a mandatory passenger key; Evan and children can walk through any legally opened safe gate.
**Before catastrophe:** widely connected gate directory; ordinary travel easy and reliable.  
**After catastrophe:** old stations and route registry damaged/severed; Orin repairs safe pairs, proves return and negotiates permissions. A standing bell at a station is NOT enough if the far end is silent or has collapsed.

## B. Bubu's living door (rare, story-defining exception)
Bubu is a living bell spirit with ONE physical golden collar bell. His special formation is a **PROPOSED long-arc mystery**: he resonated from a surviving fragment/last echo of Veylora's damaged Great Tower, revived and made stable by Earth's restorative Living Current. His exact origin must be earned, not declared a fact in EP001. He becomes Lia's inseparable purple sparkling cat friend.
- **HOME NOTE:** Bubu has imprinted the unique living resonance of **Mara's Earth home anchor** (a small physical tuned listening-place she and Orin built in Bellfern, NOT an ancient fixed Earth Gate Bell). When Bubu is present, awake and willing, ringing his collar bell can open a **small short-lived return doorway** to that actual Earth anchor. This works from an uncharted world even when no ordinary World Gate Bell exists at the source; it is the living rift's exceptional return mode.
- **WHO RETURNS:** only people physically gathered beside Bubu in the active short-lived doorway, with manageable load and safe receiving room at Earth. Cannot teleport remote relatives just by their name or transport an unlimited crowd. A regular group/family can return, and a few rescued people can join if capacity fits.
- **RING RULE:** One distinct home-call rhythm; Bubu's conscious response is required. He is not an involuntary object anyone may abuse indefinitely. When the Earth anchor lacks charge, is obstructed, disrupted or Bubu exhausted/incapacitated, the return is **blocked until restored**; the show must establish these vulnerabilities early, not invent exceptions after failure.
- **UNKNOWN-WORLD NOTE:** Sometimes Bubu hears an uncharted resonance left by the catastrophe or a clue/object from an unknown world. His collar bell can resonate with that **specific echo** and reveal an unpredictable TEMPORARY portal to that world. Destination is "random to the people" because the original mapped coordinates are unknown, but NEVER random to screenplay logic: each opening requires a previously planted physical echo, place, object, weather pattern or audible signal. He cannot dial any desired unknown destination, open Veylora's sealed heart or find Elara/Rowan/Cael by blood alone.
- **EXPLORATION SAFETY:** Mara probes the opening, uses a small nonliving object, visual/light/sound checks and gate-side protection; minors do NOT blindly jump into unverified lethal worlds. When local peril/urgent rescue forces risk, the story must own the consequence.
- **COST:** The destination portal consumes concentrated Bubu stamina, lasts a limited time, and is harder to call than the practiced Earth home-note. The home door normally works reliably for family emergencies but not if Bubu is separated or overwhelmed. Bubu cannot keep multiple world doors open simultaneously or serve as industrial mass transit.
- **WHY THIS IS DIFFERENT FROM AN ORDINARY PORTAL:** Gate BELL = engineered, persistent, station-to-station path via known address. BUBU = living, portable, echo-driven temporary path + privileged bonded return to Earth. Two different technologies based on related musical resonance physics.
- **SERIES BALANCE:** The more unknown worlds Bubu reveals, the more *potential* leads/rescue cases the family discovers; it does NOT automatically link those worlds to Orin's permanent gate network. A found world still needs proper repair/permission if thousands later must travel.

## C. How Orin can travel and return without carrying Bubu
Orin explores the **few mapped worlds** accessible through repaired old GATE stations. He physically visits, meets witnesses, searches old evacuation routes and occasionally locates stranded groups. His expert equipment can measure old-world bell address and restore safe links. As Mara and Bubu prove Earth's return-note, Orin gradually learns to tune selected recovered gate pairs toward **the new Earth home anchor** (a negotiated new receiving station, not old forgotten Earth gate). This allows limited ordinary EARTH↔MAPPED WORLD travel where actually repaired; he still cannot instantly reach EARTH from every remote planet. On unreachable missions, he stays away until safe route or family-supported meeting, creating real separation and dramatic stakes.
**Family calls:** Bubu can receive Orin's spoken messages when there is an existing or weak usable harmonic link/relay from where Orin is; zero signal = no call. His familial voice relay does not open routes or find hidden relatives everywhere.

## D. How to bring dispersed people to Earth WITHOUT unlimited evacuation
**Small group:** Mara and Bubu find a person in unknown world, assess safety, consent, whether they actually want Earth; Bubu opens short Earth home-return for family + limited survivors if the anchor has prepared room. 
**Large community:** cannot pour hundreds through the kitten's collar bell. Orin and recovered trained bellsmiths must establish a verified normal high-capacity permanent passage from that world's actual safe station (or forge/repair one with rare material, local consent and an already accessible work site) to an authorized Earth reception station. Until then: local aid, evidence and evacuation in carefully planned bounded groups.
**Unknown world with Bubu absent:** cannot simply follow a name; Orin must search via old gates, known neighbors or trusted local allies. 
**Earth refuge:** expanding civilian shelter under pressure of secrecy, capacity and ecology; some rescued people move to other welcoming worlds voluntarily, or eventually Veylora if safe.
**Ethics:** no compulsory Earth transfer; a community with a new home may stay where it is.
