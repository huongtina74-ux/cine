## SHOWRUNNER WORLD ASSIGNMENT V4.7 — USER-DIRECTED PLOT RULE
**Date:** 2026-11-06
**Status:** Owner-defined **STORY DIRECTION on the development branch**, not yet merged/Canon-Locked to active `main`. This document SUPERSEDES the geographic split and "Mara regularly explores BOTH old and new worlds" language of v4.5 and the provisional earlier old-world-heavy episode ratios in v4.4. Other magic, family, character and 40–50-minute quality constraints remain unless actually inconsistent.

### 1. Primary dramatic tendencies — with rare v4.8 exceptions and genuine surprises
- **ORIN is primarily, BUT NOT EXCLUSIVELY, the OLD-WORLD explorer**: travels already mapped pre-catastrophe worlds through surviving/repaired FIXED Gate Bell stations; looks for ALL lost Veylora civilians, searches lost wife and two sons, repairs old routes, examines false evacuation records and rare bell technology, tries to locate route back to Veylora. He works independently and is MOSTLY OFF SCREEN; rare in-person appearance must matter. Messages to family via Bubu require a surviving signal path; no universal calling into disconnected worlds.
- **MARA, EVAN, JUNO, LIA, BIX AND BUBU are the lead cast, primarily for NEW/UNKNOWN-WORLD adventures**: Bubu sometimes detects a *specific rift echo*, opens a limited temporary doorway to an uncharted realm whose destination is unpredictable to the characters (but has pre-planted causal sound evidence for writers); Mara leads safe exploration, encounters fantastical cultures, local crises, discoveries related to magical renewal or lost Veylora refugees, and returns to their EARTH anchor through Bubu subject to stamina, physical proximity and receiving-anchor availability.
- **Occasional EXCEPTIONS: Mara and family enter OLD WORLDS ONLY because of a specific motivated reason**: (a) a rare Orin assignment with justified urgency and why he cannot personally do it; (b) an unexpected event/call/survivor or a returning ally they care about; (c) a dangerous discovered clue directly connected to the family, Earth regenerative ecology or a previously rescued person; (d) a major reunion/special. A "need to fill an old-world episode quota" is NOT a legitimate reason. A fixed known gate is only usable when actually repaired, two-way tested and permitted. The family can choose to decline.
- **EARTH HOME** remains a full adventure engine: family, Evan's post, school, Mara's magic ecology research, protected voluntary sanctuary and surprising local magical incidents. These are NOT filler; occasional episodes happen entirely on Earth.
- **ORIN can SOMETIMES contact or unexpectedly fall into a NEW WORLD** while repairing an OLD Gate, if an actual residual rift/trace creates an unstable, plot-planted anomaly. An unknown answering signal does not by itself transport him; physical crossing needs a rupture, and getting home needs an earned working path or a later specifically matched Bubu rescue. Orin can remain stranded and unavailable for an honest interval. This exception is a welcome source of surprises, not unlimited Orin travel.
- **ORIN never becomes a second parallel protagonist television show**: audience learns his discoveries through actual-path Bubu calls, verified letters and recovered records; occasionally a brief ORIN glimpse creates a specific real escalation. His role in sending missions is periodic, not the start of every episode.
- **The bad actor / Ash Bellkeeper** has active reach primarily in the surviving OLD fixed-gate network. He cannot materialize in Bubu-only uncharted realms or Earth before he earns an actual path. This physical separation is a FEATURE of the mystery: rare old-world assignments are especially risky, while old traces, refugees and consequences can intersect Mara's uncharted exploration without the live villain appearing there.

### 2. Preserve the unique world-physics correctly
- EARTH was uncharted when Orin/Mara arrived through catastrophe UNKNOWN rifts, not the formerly known/banned Earth of v3. No ancient Earth fixed gate predating their arrival.
- A fixed Earth workshop Gate Station was built/tuned later from salvaged materials, paired with a FEW repaired old-world stations; it is NOT permission to tour all old worlds whenever bored. At an OLD FIXED gate the traveler rings the gate's own bell after invocation; no mandatory personal bell for every traveler.
- BUBU has ONE golden collar bell and an ever-sparkling purple spiral tail; is a living character and family friend, has a consent/stamina-limited unique return-note to prepared Earth home anchor and sometimes temporary NEW-world openings linked to genuine echoes. New discovery is NOT arbitrary world-selection, instant mass travel, a universally safe return or a new permanent mapped gate.
- Orin's old mapped world contacts cannot call Bubu if no real relay/path exists. If no current contact, family still acts independently; Orin's absence has emotional significance.
- Lost people from the WHOLE Veylora civilization matter; family reunions are part, not the total objective. Some located refugees want safety where found, not compulsory relocation to Earth. Magic restoration is sustainable ecology, not strip-mining every realm.

### 3. Writer's-room testing ratio: WHERE a family episode takes place
**This is a NONBINDING creative trial ratio, NOT verified YouTube audience preference. Orin's rare unknown-world accidents are allowed and mostly tracked as linked beats, rather than forbidden by this family-location mix:**
- **65% — NEW/previously unknown world spaces:** including FIRST discoveries via Bubu and REVISITS to worlds he originally discovered, new towns and changing residents. IMPORTANT: 65% of episodes are NOT 65% brand-new planets. Aim pilot slate ~4–6 true first-world discoveries across 20 episodes when story/asset feasibility allows; others are deeper revisits. Adjust later by quality and real analytics.
- **20% — EARTH Bellfern:** surprising family events, ecological research, magical refugee story and secrets.
- **10% — OLD mapped world visits by Mara family:** strictly special assignments or credible independent urgent events; Orin is chiefly responsible for OLD-world search. A rarely used old route must have a working tested paired gateway.
- **5% — MULTI-location exceptional story:** deliberate cross-world rescue or climax; not a quota device.
This replaces v4.5's earlier 35% OLD, 30% NEW, 25% EARTH, 10% mixed. Other ratios concerning **story SUBJECT** (lost people, mystery, romance/family, villain, exploration) are a separate overlapping classification and do NOT alter this primary geography rule.

### 4. Why Old-World Orin can sustain big narrative without stealing screen
Orin's report can contain: a surprising living witness; one missing name confirmed; coordinates/physical artifacts with uncharted rift echoes Bubu can interpret later; newly repaired gateway and old world alert; a false evacuation ledger or villain sabotage of a concrete old station; a request requiring Mara's specialist Earth/Bubu capabilities. **His new information is not itself an episode plot** unless Mara family must make a meaningful new choice. Messages can be brief, broken, outdated or missing; Bubu cannot read the entire old world from afar.
Example: Orin repairs old-world ocean station; a survivor describes a vanished child and gives a song fragment. Mara hears an echo at home days later; Bubu opens an unknown forest where a DIFFERENT local crisis must be solved before the family can even investigate the song. Orin's Old-world intelligence is one causal bridge, not a dispatch at minute 2 every time.

### 6. 40–50 minute long-form story standard
One compelling 42–48min test story includes a first 30–45s visually specific HOOK; relatable family goal; active unknown world/problem; 1–2 earned reversals, including earlier planted evidence; moral choice with real cost; a community/local resolution and changed relationship or world; optionally a proportional wider clue. Do NOT pad by traveling through 10 empty scenes, endless camera moves, repeated false alarms or NPC information dumps. Families must ask, choose, fail, learn and act.
Rotate family lead (Mara default, Juno+Bix investigation, Lia+Bubu friendship, Evan home/post dilemmas once secrets handled), genres (comedy, mystery, fantasy wonder, action, emotions, ecological ethics, lost-person stories), world ecology and twist patterns. Orin's absence is a variable, not obligatory episodic plot explanation.
**World re-entry rule:** After an UNKNOWN world has been discovered once, Bubu may return using a *known retained echo* if plot-established and physically viable; repeated short visits consume stamina and remain temporary. Long-term world connection requires independent Gate Bell installation/paired test and consent (not automatic).
**Gate safety:** Family with children can inspect portal destination, use Bix nonliving probes, verify Bubu's Earth home anchor, avoid leap into unknown mortal hazard without dramatized extraordinary reason.
**Audio/visual identity:** nine original cast references remain unchanged; Bubu one gold collar bell, always-sparkly spiral purple tail; Bix compact and bell-free; no fabricated unapproved new faces, CGI rigs or voices.
**Data honesty:** 1000 complete scripts/episodes and A2 45-minute timed QC not complete by creating this guide; no YouTube retention data gathered for Bell & Leaf to validate these ratios.

### 7. Hard FAIL list for episode QC
1. Giving Mara a majority of OLD worlds while Orin is a rare figure, contrary to owner's explicit intent.
2. Orin claiming to choose any NEW world with a standard repaired Gate Bell without a demonstrated rupture, resonance leak or verified temporary path; rare spontaneous rift entrapment is explicitly allowed, and a true reunion with Bubu needs an earned matching route.
3. Forcing family to visit old worlds without a mission, rare unexpected event or personal reason.
4. Reusing "Orin calls → task → portal" as every episode opening.
5. Bubu picks any world by name, instantly teleports thousands, or makes permanent old Gate Stations by ringing the little collar bell.
6. Every new world has a refugee/villain clue; many independent locals and mysteries must stand on their own.
7. Main antagonist magically appears inside wholly unknown Bubu-only realm.
8. A supposed "new discovery" resets the inhabitants' history upon repeat visit.
9. An Old world allows instant Earth crossing despite no verified working paired Gate Bell.
10. Fake 45-minute runtime through repetitive attempts and empty beauty shots.
11. Orin/Elara/Rowan/Cael mission displaces ongoing search for unrelated lost people.
12. Writing new faces/voices for original locked nine-character master without the user's reference approval.

**SERIES RULE:** "Orin follows the old bells. Mara and Bubu follow the unknown song. The family stays at the heart of every story."
