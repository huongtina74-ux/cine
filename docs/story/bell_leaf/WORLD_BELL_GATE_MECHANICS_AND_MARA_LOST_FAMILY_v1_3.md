## A. The public explanation to use IN THE SHOW (3 devices and 4 steps)

### 1. ONE WORLD = ONE GATE BELL
Every discovered mapped world has **ONE main fixed Gate Bell** (\`WORLD_GATE_BELL\`), placed inside a protected **Gate Station** (\`BELL_GATE_STATION\`) when its route was first established, and one unique \`WORLD_NOTE\` / bell-song address. A world may have thousands of buildings but one registered World Gate Bell. The gate itself is the door. This bell can be physically present but inactive. Founding bellsmiths originally installed gates across worlds long before present-day Orin. The first arrival mechanism of ancient pioneers remains a separate prehistory question, not an invented convenience of the present family.

### 2. A PERSONAL TRAVEL BELL IS A KEY, NOT A SECOND WORLD GATE
Witches ordinarily carry a small **TRAVEL_BELL** issued with a finite list of permitted world notes. It is like a travel key with a few authorized destination settings. They don't each need a different world-sized bell in their pocket and don't need to know how to build any bell. Each character uses the same travel bell for their approved routes, says the destination spell and rings ONCE at a safe Gate Station.
- \`"Bell of Underleaf, open the door."\` (simple English A2 draft)
- The existing station sends a request to the destination World Gate Bell.
- The distant bell must answer with its correct note (**TWO BELLS ANSWER**).
- If authenticated and both directions are stable, one temporary gate opens between the stations. A normal traveler steps through and enters a protected arrival pad in the other world.
- To return, traveler visits the authorized station in that world and calls home using a pre-approved note. The receiving Earth bell answers. If no return confirmation, nobody sends Mara/children on that trip.
- Ordinary humans including Evan can STEP through a portal Mara safely opens; no random magical powers needed.

**Child-friendly in-show rule:** \`"One world has one big bell. Our little bell is the key. Both big bells must answer before the door opens."\`

### 3. THE ROUTE MAP IS A DIRECTORY + PERMISSION TABLE
The **Great Tower at Veylora** once coordinated bell addresses, route authentication, emergency gates and permission. It was the main switchboard, not a magic source giving everyone unlimited power. A few small **PRE-PAIRED DIRECT ROUTES** between safe stations existed as fallback paths. These are the only reason any travel can resume before Veylora itself is restored. A name on a map cannot automatically summon a working gate; the remote bell must physically respond and return must pass a test.

### 4. WHY THE NETWORK BROKE
Sabotage deliberately scrambled the central address/permission data while overstressing the gates, causing unwanted cross-world openings and a dangerous rupture over Veylora. Most Gate Bells physically survived in their worlds but their route keys/authentication were lost or their infrastructure broken. Some planets still have functioning gates but no paired line to Earth; others have smashed bells. Many stranded people lost their travel bells during emergency and are NOT bellmakers. No collective instant homecoming.

## B. Four conditions for an ordinary crossing
1. The destination has a known verified unique note, and the travel bell has permission for it.
2. A source and a destination station are linked by a surviving/repaired path; destination Gate Bell answers.
3. Destination's physical arrival pad is safe and local access is allowed.
4. The reverse path is tested and Earth Gate Bell/return reserve answers. **NO VERIFIED RETURN = NO CHILDREN CROSSING.**

Examples:
- **Earth → Underleaf**: BOTH Earth and Underleaf respond; Mara speaks/rings, protected gate opens; family arrives Underleaf; after local adventure they ring Earth and return to the hidden Earth station.
- **Earth → Unknown Mistland**: a whispered world name or grandma rumor is NOT enough; if Mistland's gate cannot answer, NOTHING OPENS. At most, Orin sends a weak diagnostic test from a nearby known station and logs INACCESSIBLE. Family cannot teleport because of urgency.
- **Underleaf → Previously verified Sky Port**: a real already-tested direct pair may allow crossing via the intermediate world; no bypass from Earth to all worlds by vague "resonance."

## C. How Orin restored the FIRST Earth route despite Earth being FORBIDDEN
The old Veylora expeditions had once established **a single hidden Gate Bell on Earth**, but the old expedition failed to report a dependable return signal; Veylora law formally sealed/removed the public route. The Gate Bell remained PHYSICALLY on Earth, inactive and forgotten. Thus forbidden = a legal quarantine plus broken/disconnected route; NOT the nonexistence of any bell or all magic.

**During the disaster** attacker-corrupted emergency gates on VEYLORA used massive SOURCE-SIDE energy to eject refugees through temporary **one-way tears**. A full two-bell response was BYPASSED because the evacuation mechanism was corrupted. Mara and Orin landed at different places on Earth. Such one-way emergency dumping cannot be recreated routinely with ordinary personal bells, and did not restore a way back.

**Years of effort:**
1. Orin's damaged travel bell preserves a weak signature from the anomalous one-way arrival. He searches for the source of that note on Earth; first he cannot even be certain the old Earth Gate Station exists.
2. He traces the ancient hidden bell, finds it physically corroded/sealed, and moves/restores the existing parts and matching receiving station to the hidden Bellfern workshop location. **He did not make a second World Gate Bell**; it is the same single Earth key, restored/relocated, with new nonmagical casings and extremely rare salvaged resonant parts.
3. One personal bell of Orin holds a previously verified limited direct-route note from another world whose Gate Bell still answers (fallback bypass predating sabotage). Orin sends diagnostic sounds only. He tries small objects both ways and measures an actual stable return. This takes YEARS, not one story afternoon.
4. When safe, he alone crosses first; returns; expands to a FEW safe worlds by repairing additional known lines, finding intact intermediate stations, obtaining rare materials and coordinating with living bellsmiths. Earth remains a PRIVATE, limited, illegal or ethically disputed refuge, not a public network or legal OPEN world.
5. The choice to settle by the ancient Earth station explains why Mara's protected workshop and home are at Bellfern; it is not a lucky accident or second Earth bell.

**Engineering continuity:** source-side forced arrival bypassing checks is unique to disaster. Normal travel requires TWO-way station handshake. If destination bell is physically smashed and there is no local bellsmith or alternative safe access, Orin CANNOT remotely repair it. Characters must locate a helper there or an independently existing neighboring safe line. Veylora's central gate remains blocked by the protective isolation and dangerous damage; the Earth fallback does not automatically reconnect it.
