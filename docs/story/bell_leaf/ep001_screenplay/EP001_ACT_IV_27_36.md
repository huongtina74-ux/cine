### SC-021 — The Branch and the Nest | 30:00–31:30

**S187 [31:00–31:10]** Bubu climbs halfway up the trunk, his spiral tail still sparkling. He steadies a hanging strap with his paw, not a portal.

### SC-022 — Aila's Choice | 31:30–33:00
*Location:* Covered garden worktable / case suspended by soft ropes · *Cast:* MARA, AILA (asset hold), JUNO, LIA, BIX, BUBU · *Ethical beat:* consent, proportionate repair, protection of original keepsakes and Earth wildlife.
