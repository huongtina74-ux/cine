# BELL & LEAF — WHERE THE PEOPLE LIVE
## Bellhaven in Underleaf — settlement decision v3.3, creative proposal
**Date:** 2026-11-06
**Status:** Proposed showrunner location decision on development branch; NOT active Canon Lock, not designed/approved location image sheet. This responds to the owner's question on where Orin's found Veylora survivors actually settle.

## The clear three-home geography
1. **EARTH / BELLFERN:** The everyday PRIMARY HOME of Mara, her nonmagical spouse Evan, Juno, Lia, compact Bix and purple cat spirit Bubu. Evan delivers post, children attend ordinary school, ordinary paternal grandparents live nearby. Mara's concealed workshop protects the private Earth gate. Earth remains a forbidden/silent public destination and is NOT open to mass refugee resettlement.
2. **UNDERLEAF / BELLHAVEN:** The central proposed **refugee reception and voluntary settlement community** of displaced Veylora residents. Bellhaven is NOT an independent newly discovered planet, nor already a massive city. It starts as a small permitted district/town in the existing GREEN-tested world Underleaf after residents/authorities agree to land, safe shelter, water, food, healthcare, schools and transport capacity. Gradually grows through survivor cooperation and host participation. Orin can run his *search, rescue, family reunification and bell repair liaison* from Bellhaven while travelling frequently to known safe worlds; he does not move all refugee families into Mara's house.
3. **VEY LORA / VEYLORA:** Original homeland with inaccessible/blocked public Bell Gate. The ultimate long-term restoration goal; not a current destination for refugees. Once confirmed safe and rebuilt, people may voluntarily return, visit, remain in Bellhaven or other host worlds, with genuine choice.

## Practical routes and limits
- Mara's family may travel EARTH ↔ UNDERLEAF only along a preverified GREEN safe two-way bell route, with a protected return anchor; normal travel needs two responding main World Gate Bells and the prepared Travel Bell key.
- Bubu's one collar bell relays Orin's voice ONLY across known active signal paths; it cannot move people or talk into disconnected worlds. Do not invent universal refugee teleportation.
- The Earth private station has scarce capacity, restricted legal status, and a mixed human family at home; sending displaced people en masse to Earth would break technical, ethical and emotional logic.
- Underleaf residents retain sovereignty, must consent to a **specific planned site and capacity**, and need to see material improvements and enforceable resource-sharing; absence of consent = NO refuge settlement yet.
- Gradual chronology: *initially no Bellhaven* → first Green-world local support case / negotiation → permitted modest reception house and garden → staffed medical, school and practical trades → a small neighborhood → multiple satellite safe communities on other verified worlds. Do not assert overnight large-scale construction.
- ORIN's family missing grandmother Elara and elder sons Rowan/Cael (names not locked) can eventually meet him at Bellhaven IF safe routes and their own wishes permit; they may stay temporarily in their adopted host communities to protect other refugees. Finding them never ends Orin's all-Veylora rescue duty.
- Some survivors CHOOSE to remain where they were found (safe local settlement); others reunite at Bellhaven or a different approved host world. The network must never force all refugees to one capital or displace Underleaf inhabitants.
- Veylora endpoint becomes reachable only through earned repairs, local verification, and explicit safe opening; no last-minute mass shortcut.
- Local-world story variety: Bellhaven's neighborhood dramas, children learning new routines, food/water construction dilemmas, welcoming hosts, honest political disagreements, Orin's limited travel, Mara's own home life in Bellfern. The two homes create recurring characters and evolving emotional stakes across 1,000 episodes.

## Screen-friendly scene to ESTABLISH the place
After a dramatic, verified rescue of several Veylora refugees, Orin and Mara have to decide where to take them. The private Earth workshop cannot house or legally receive them; original Veylora gate is sealed. They visit Underleaf via an established GREEN path. The local community initially refuses a large refugee camp due to water and land scarcity. Mara and locals cooperate to repair a shared irrigation supply, consult elders, and negotiate a modest reception house near the existing station. The first guests enter by choice; residents name it **BELLHAVEN** as a hope rather than a replacement for Veylora. Orin works there when available and delegates management to a consented mixed local/refugee council rather than owning the district. This is an EARNED local payoff and new recurring location, not a magically built city.

## Distinguish the THREE dramatic homes
- **Bellfern Earth** = family emotional home, ordinary human life.
- **Bellhaven Underleaf** = collective Veylora survivor aid / temporary-to-permanent chosen new home.
- **Veylora** = ancestral home and restoration of freedom to travel, not the only valid final home.
