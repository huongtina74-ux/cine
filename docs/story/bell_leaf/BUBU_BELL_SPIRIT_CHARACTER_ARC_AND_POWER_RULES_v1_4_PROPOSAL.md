### 1. Distinct job — a living magical witness, not a key
> **ORIN makes and repairs the bell; MARA uses its power to take the family safely through a gate; BIX measures the metal and vibration; BUBU senses when the bell's magical memory or voice is wrong.**

Bubu is a living **bell-resonance spirit** (proposed origin class). Each Gate Bell has a **unique living echo** on top of the mechanical world-address tone. A forger can copy a destination note or paperwork but cannot reproduce every faint echo easily. Bubu feels the difference up close; his feelings are a **warning**, not proof. The collar bell is an extension of his spirit-heart; it cannot carry passengers to another world.

### 2. Three powers — clearly distinct, limited and plot-usable
**P1: Hear dissonance.** Near a real/accessible Gate Bell or its authentic fragment, Bubu can hear whether the magical echo feels familiar, distressed, interrupted or inconsistent. The physical note could sound correct to a machine while echo doesn't. It flags possible tampering; it cannot identify WHO tampered, WHEN, or WHY, and altered/damaged/very old bells may have ambiguous signal. A skilled saboteur could disguise clues or intentionally plant a confusing echo.

**P2: Catch one memory-fragment.** A bell which rang during a significant event may retain a brief residual auditory shard: a cut-off warning, several notes, one voice, a partial rhythm. Bubu must be physically NEAR an authentic bell/part, focus and listen in silence, and may receive **nothing** if residue is absent/destroyed/corrupted. One fragment is incomplete; it is NOT a literal scene recording, a mind-reading device, universal time machine or infallible testimony. The audio can be misinterpreted; needs independent support (archive, witnesses, technical timestamp). Repeated attempts tire him and do not reveal new facts from an already-exhausted residue.

**P3: Brief stabilizing counter-tone, NOT portal creation.** When an ALREADY-OPEN verified Gate is temporarily wobbling, Bubu may match a sound to warn/assist Mara and hold the harmless edge of the resonance for **seconds** while the trained witch/technician stabilizes the real station. This costs him stamina; he may lose his voice/sparkle briefly and must recover. He can never open a route, repair a destroyed Root Bell, override legal lock, re-power a shut Veylora route or guarantee safe return. Without two real answering Gate Bells, P3 has no effect and must never be used to let a child travel through a forbidden unknown world.

**Best on-screen visual signaling:** tail sparkles form tiny pulses timed to the bell; purple ears angle toward a sound others don't hear; collar bell makes a faint DIFFERENT ring. Maintain actual approved appearance exactly. No extra props or overall redesign.

### 3. Core vulnerability and emotional arc
- **WANT:** be useful and loved as a person rather than a disposable living detector.
- **NEED:** tell the difference between wanting to help and claiming certainty; learn to say "I don't know", honor Lia's agency and accept care.
- **FEAR:** the family will eventually prefer a machine or an ordinary travel bell to him. This is the user's precise concern turned into a character conflict.
- **FLAW:** dramatizes his hunches, jokes instead of admitting uncertainty, gets jealous of Bix/Evan's practical problem solving, and sometimes insists a half-heard chime is a complete answer.
- **CHOICE:** in a major incident he chooses to tell Orin and Mara that he cannot identify an echo, preventing a dangerous false rescue; or chooses to exhaust his short stabilizing counter-tone to buy the family a genuine few seconds, not magically win a crisis.
- **Relation to Lia:** she cares for him independent of powers; they learn respectful listening together. Unlike Lia's empathy with CONSENTING LIVING ANIMALS, Bubu hears the residue/feel of **MAGICAL BELLS**. They never have duplicate abilities.
- **Relation to Bix:** Bix says "The note is correct." Bubu says "It sounds wrong." They need each other's evidence; comic rivalry grows into trust.
- **Relation to Evan:** Evan's postmark/date and Bubu's sound-fragment form two independent evidence streams. Human proof vs magical hint, not competitive utility.
- **Relation to Orin:** Orin can repair a circuit but cannot hear spiritual echo; Bubu knows a strange feeling but cannot name route damage. Orin learns to listen rather than dismiss children's companion.
- **Relation to Mara:** she makes the decision; the cat does not replace parental protection.

### 4. One subtle LONG-ARC backstory proposal (not yet Canon or proof)
**Working mystery:** Bubu's tiny collar bell holds a SINGLE **untampered resonance fragment from a Veylora evacuation bell**. Because the family did not know how it was preserved, Bubu himself doesn't know its true significance. This is NOT a master key, world map, address or grandma tracking device.
**Origin options must be resolved before a definitive screenplay:**
- (A) Bubu was already with Mara in the disaster, and his bell recorded a rhythm without his understanding; requires carefully defining his age/location and earlier show timeline.
- (B) After Orin recovered one old bell fragment, Bubu formed or bonded to it and later befriended Lia on Earth; this protects ambiguity about where Bubu was during the crisis. **Preferred draft:** B. His special memory is an OBJECT'S residue, not direct omniscience.
- (C) He was rescued by a stranger from an already opened safe world and his collar bell was a gift; proof of previous owner must be earned without inventing grandmother's image.
Choose only one after showrunner review. Do not casually assert grandmother intentionally imprinted her location inside his bell.

**Fair-play twist ladder for Bubu:**
- Early eps: Bubu notices a copied bell note in an Orin mission and is dismissed; later practical evidence corroborates an authentic fake. Introduces his P1, not an all-seeing ability.
- ~EP060: a vivid but incomplete residual "two notes/one pause" helps isolate a particular craft method, not the maker. Bix confirms vibration; Juno finds a physical mark.
- ~EP200: Bubu makes a false accusation due to confusing one honest safety-station repair with a sabotage mark. He apologizes; audience sees limits.
- ~EP350: the incomplete collar echo fits a VERIFIED archived note from the evacuation; it changes the chronology of a specific gate, not the whole mystery.
- ~EP550: a true recorded fragment could clear the alleged bellmaker Nera only in combination with independent dated records. Do NOT claim guilt solely from sound.
- ~EP700: after evidence establishes the perpetrator, Bubu helps test that the repaired worlds are genuinely connected under new ethical rules; he does not solve court/political accountability alone.
- Finale: he has a family even if the bell network is repaired and his diagnostic talent is less necessary. That is his emotional payoff.

### 5. Sample episode twist — "THE BELL THAT LIED"
Orin assigns a safe mission: bring a genuine tuned bell plate from an Underleaf repair station. Bix measures it; the frequency and signature match Orin's known notes. Bubu says it *sounds frightened*, but refuses to claim a villain. Juno scoffs that measurements don't lie; Lia asks Bubu what he *actually heard*. A local resident reveals two repairers independently copied the exact same plate; their machines sound identical, but one plate retains the softer ring of an earlier protective emergency.
**First apparent twist:** Orin's verified bell part looks legitimate but may have been copied.
**Second earned twist:** the "frightened" note is not caused by the current merchant, who was protecting displaced families, but by the original incident years before. The family saves local workshops from a false accusation. A technician independently dates a legitimate repair and preserves provenance; Bubu's echo gives a useful clue but not a culprit.
Local resolution: the repair workshop is cleared, a cracked bridge is mended together, a route record is corrected. A2 short dialogue:
BIX: "The notes are the same."
BUBU: "They sound the same. But they do not feel the same."
LIA: "Can you tell us who changed the bell?"
BUBU: "No. I only heard a little sound."
ORIN: "Then we need more proof."
JUNO: "I can check the old metal."
BUBU: "Thank you. That is a better plan."
