# I. THE SERIES HEART

> "Every world has a door. Every door has a bell. What happens when somebody steals the way home?"

Veylora is not merely a city of wand-waving wizards. It is the shared HOME of a dispersed magical clan, a place of family kitchens, schools, festivals, living gardens, ordinary joys — and the **Great Bell Tower**, which once enabled safe travel between thousands of worlds. Orin was both the **Head of the Witch Clan** and **Keeper of the Bells**, responsible for routes and the rules that protect both travelers and destination worlds.

The story is about restoring the right to come home while discovering who exploited the system to seize control of travel and exile the entire clan. Belonging, informed consent and family love matter more than one all-powerful spell.

**Mystery questions, nested:** (1) What exactly happened to Veylora that made evacuation essential? (2) Who sabotaged the network and who triggered the dispersal? (3) Why was forbidden Earth suddenly reachable? (4) Who continues attacking repaired routes? (5) Where is Orin's wife? (6) What was the saboteur truly trying to protect, steal or conceal?

# II. THE BELL SYSTEM — SIMPLE TO WATCH, STRONG ENOUGH TO WRITE 1,000 EPISODES

### Rule B-01: ONE WORLD, ONE ROOT BELL, ONE GATE ADDRESS
Every known world has its own **Root Bell** (`World Bell`) and corresponding **Gate Address**. They are paired: the bell's unique note *is that world's key*. An approved traveler can take a tuned **travel bell** that carries this destination's verified note; they speak a simple destination spell and ring it. A gate to the selected world opens where a safe anchor is registered.

This solves both user requirements: **a world has a distinct bell/door** and ordinary authorized witches can simply **say the spell and ring**. Carrying a travel bell is not synonymous with knowing how to build a new world bell.

**Spoken incantation (story proposal; English A2):** `"Bell of [world name], open the way. Take me to the safe gate."` Ring once. The spell does not work if the travel bell lacks that world's live verified tune and safe destination anchor.

### Rule B-02: THE FORGE IS RARE; THE TRAVEL SKILL WAS COMMON
Before sabotage, many witches could *use* a prepared gate; Orin and only a small group of senior trained bellmakers could *make* a new world Root Bell, copy/verify destination notes, align the return route and repair a broken line. Crafts require rare **Resonant Metal** found scarcely, if at all, on Earth. Some aides know only one discipline (metal, tuning, map, anchor); cooperation matters.

Aila's existing personal bell is a limited pre-tuned travel bell entrusted to her after rescue in the existing EP001 candidate; she is not an expert and does not suddenly learn unlimited new-world access. Orin and Mara have functioning but limited approved travel bells. Bubu's collar chime is not a universal key.

### Rule B-03: A RETURN IS A SEPARATE PROMISE
Arrival does not guarantee return. A safe round trip requires **verified bell tune + destination receiving anchor + permission + tested return anchor**. A damaged map/remote voice is merely a signal, NOT a working door. Some people stranded with a permitted bell still cannot leave because that world's anchor failed. Many have no bell at all. Sending untested bells risks a second disappearance.

### Rule B-04: WORLDS HAVE ACCESS CATEGORIES
- **OPEN WORLDS:** travel, limited everyday magic and protection permitted by local agreement.
- **LIMITED WORLDS:** authorized emergency, diplomacy and research only; some powers prohibited.
- **CLOSED WORLDS:** no routine travel or public magic. Some are closed to protect local people; others because a route cannot guarantee return. Earth belongs here. A closed world is not necessarily empty, evil or physically unreachable forever.

### Rule B-05: EARTH IS A SILENT WORLD (THE FORBIDDEN GATE) — NEW STORY PROPOSAL
Earth was recorded in ancient restricted archives as `THE SILENT WORLD`. It is populated by ordinary humans who did not consent to magical contact. Moreover, its bell signature is unusually weak: it is very hard to register an exit anchor and **very easy for an incoming signal to lose its return address**. Long ago a test party failed to send a confirmed return signal; the world was formally quarantined, its Root Bell sealed and its address removed from public maps.

**Avoid the implausible claim "no witch ever arrived on Earth":** the credible formulation is **no officially recognized successful round trip had been recorded for generations**; young witches were taught it was a world nobody returned from. Some forbidden worlds still contain old traces or hidden stories. Only rare archives held by the clan Head mention its old address. This is a ban grounded in safety, ethics and politics, not "magic absolutely cannot work on Earth".

Small hidden magic can still work on Earth, but loud portal spells are dangerous: they may expose the family, weaken an exit anchor, or be detected by an unknown watcher (PROPOSED, not currently proven). Earth is not the witches' new convenient free-for-all playground. Mara's workshop uses protected, rare hardware; Juno and Lia attend an ordinary school.

# III. THE CATASTROPHE — WHAT MADE VEYLORA UNLIVABLE THAT NIGHT? (PROPOSED)

**Working name:** `THE NIGHT THE BELLS BROKE` / `Đêm Những Chiếc Chuông Vỡ`.

Someone with privileged access maliciously interfered with Veylora's **Great Bell Tower route synchronizer**. The attack deliberately made incompatible World Bell notes answer at once. It is sabotage, not an accidental or well-meant unauthorized experiment. The same synchronizer maintained the safe **Veil** protecting the city from the raw crossings between worlds.

A chain reaction unfolded in stages:
1. **Unheard double tone.** Hundreds of bells rang without a human hand; gate-map lights moved to wrong addresses. Workers noticed the system was being overridden.
2. **The sky broke into doors.** Uncontrolled, incomplete gates opened in midair — in streets, stairs, markets, under roofs. Fragments of alien storms, water and gravity crossed into Veylora. Timepieces ran at different speeds; bridges between city districts became unsafe. Spectacle must remain family-appropriate, not graphic.
3. **The Veil weakened.** Beneath the sky appeared a widening pale crack called `THE HOLLOW RING`, a destructive pressure tear between route spaces. It pulled living beings toward gates. Staying was worse than fleeing. Orin ordered evacuation as his duty as clan Head.
4. **Emergency scatter.** The old public evacuation law activated surviving gates into nearby **one-way safety exits**. The enemy's corrupted routing caused people near different gates to be drawn into *different destinations*, often without a bell. Two people a step apart could be swept to different worlds. This explains why so many escaped alive but cannot return.
5. **The last isolation.** A protective safety action sealed the Great Bell Tower/Veylora return links to prevent the rupture from spreading to other worlds. Who activated this seal and at what moment is a DIFFERENT mystery from who instigated the malicious attack. Veylora is now unverified, **not established as physically destroyed**; the watchers see an abandoned gate, storms and silence, but know neither the city's ultimate survival nor its inhabitants.

**Important causal separation:** The attack endangered Veylora, and the defense/evacuation isolated travelers. It must NEVER be later rewritten as merely a "compassionate accident"; moral dilemmas concern the emergency rescue response, not whether sabotage was intentional.

### Why Orin and Mara reached Earth — the plot's first impossible fact
During the evacuation the forbidden Earth Root Bell — sealed for generations — **rang on its own**. Two separate fragments of the emergency gate swept Orin and Mara away from different locations; both fragments unexpectedly delivered them to different places on the SAME forbidden planet, Earth. Their reunion on Earth required effort, not a casual immediate plot jump. Grandmother was seen heading to protect other civilians; the gate between her and them closed; **her destination and survival remain UNKNOWN**.

Orin cannot explain why the sealed bell rang. Was it a safety fallback discovered by chance, a hidden route from long ago, or did an insider **deliberately aim two specific members of the clan at Earth**? For the first long arc, all three remain live hypotheses; no one pretends to know.

After years of searching for exotic materials in Earth's scarce supply, Orin repairs an Earth return anchor and a FEW previously known safe routes using the damaged personal travel bell and partial archives. No worldwide instant map or mass evacuation rescue follows. Mara establishes her concealed workshop, meets ordinary Evan later, has Juno and Lia; the kids grow up with magic kept out of public ordinary life.

# IV. THE REVEAL CONTRACT — THE MYSTERY MUST PAY THE AUDIENCE BACK

**Do not reveal culprits through ominous silhouettes alone.** A clue must have independent meaning and a later test. Build three layers:
1. **The SABOTEUR:** initiated deliberate corruption with privileged knowledge. Identity and motives UNRESOLVED.
2. **The EMERGENCY ISOLATOR:** may have sealed the spread to save lives; possibly connected with grandmother, but NO proof yet.
3. **The PRESENT-DAY INTERFERER:** may sabotage restored routes now. Could be same person, follower, opportunist or a protective official; no premature equivalence.

**Proposed suspect network** (not locked and not necessarily guilty):
- A former senior bellmaker with partial route-map access who disappeared at the collapse.
- An official responsible for restricted-world registries, who could know Earth was forbidden.
- A survivor claiming the sealed routes must never reopen.
- A supposed ally whose letters arrive with impossible dates. The story must exonerate some suspects by credible evidence rather than endless red herrings.

**Proposed recurring motifs:** A missing final chime in an old recording; a world address scraped out of archive pages; a mechanical tool mark that can be replicated by legitimate repairs; an unopened letter bearing Earth's forbidden gate seal. None is decisive alone.

**One progressive mystery promise per 20–50 episode segment:** At least one real, consequential answer or rescue, not just another anonymous laugh behind a mask. Reveal (a) Earth Root Bell existed (early), (b) someone unsealed it (later), (c) different actors attacked vs closed routes (middle arcs), (d) identities and evidence (final arcs). Do not force every episode to end on the same three-note teaser.
