## SUBNICHE CANON — Gia đình phù thủy / Magical Witch Family Stories

**Taxonomy:** Slow English → Slow English A2 → Magical Witch Family Stories → Bell & Leaf. This is exactly one **magical-family** micro-niche, not the whole Slow English niche. Each adventure must remain a captivating original family fantasy/mystery story first, with clear accessible A2 spoken English; a vocabulary lesson alone is not an episode. Retain humans and non-witches in the family: Evan and paternal grandparents are normal Earth relatives unaware of magic. Juno is an inventor; Lia has animal-empathy magic; Mara is a witch; Orin is a wizard. This is one recurring family and Veylora narrative, not an anthology of unrelated witches.

The Bell & Leaf family lives on ordinary Earth and returns home daily from extraordinary worlds. Evan (bo) is a postman, Mara (me) runs a concealed magical workshop, Juno (juno) is 13 and builds the turtle-pangolin robot Bix, Lia (Lia) is 11 and has animal-empathy magic, and Bubu (bubu) is her purple cat bell spirit. Orin (ongngoai), Mara's father, is the leading wizard with a separate travel bell. The paternal grandparents (ongnoi, banoi) are ordinary nearby relatives unaware of magic; bangoai is a conditional Veylora mystery character, not a default cast member.

A deliberate sabotage scattered Veylora's bell network and the family of witches across worlds. Each episode requires (a) a truly fresh adventure or unexplained event, (b) genuine surprise, (c) a local satisfying payoff, (d) a meaningful clue or emotional advance toward the network sabotage/reunion mystery, (e) relatable humor and emotion, (f) comprehensible natural A2 spoken English. 30–50 minutes per episode; 1000+ episode ambition. No narrator. No empty padding and no formula copied from competitor stories.

Original user-provided visual character sheets are binding identity sources, not the generated redesign sheets. Bubu must keep visible little glittering magical sparks in his spiraling purple tail, even for neutral studio angles; Bix must NOT have a bell (ignore erroneous concept props). Fixed location topology, true reverse angles, D55 color, no global yellow/orange cast, same age and CGI facial proportions across episodes.
