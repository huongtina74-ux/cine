# 1. Core promise
**When the bells of Veylora are deliberately broken, people from an entire witch homeland scatter across many worlds. Orin—the clan chief—and Mara's family dedicate themselves to accounting for and helping ALL displaced Veylora people: reconnecting separated families, rescuing communities, restoring safe communications and routes, and finding why the catastrophe happened. The missing grandmother and two elder brothers matter deeply, but are three stories inside a far wider civilization-wide search. Every episode must deliver a genuine local emotional outcome, not just a clue to the three relatives.**

The audience gets three intertwined worlds of emotion:
- **HOME:** Mara, Evan, Juno (13), Lia (11), small robot Bix, purple spirit cat Bubu, nearby ordinary paternal grandparents; school, post office, marriage, promises and humor matter. Family usually returns to Earth by choice, not magical curfew.
- **MISSIONS:** Orin is clan chief and Keeper of Bells, away investigating, restoring routes or pursuing time-critical leads. He delegates appropriate tasks to Mara's team, not omniscient commands. Allies in verified worlds may ask for help; Earth can have surprises; family makes independent choices.
- **LONG MYSTERY:** sabotage, forced diaspora, address manipulation, the ancient Earth taboo, the separate protective sealing of Veylora, missing relatives in different unreachable worlds, later hostile interference and ethical rebuilding of the route network.

## 1A. THE ULTIMATE RESCUE PROMISE — ALL PEOPLE, NOT ONLY ORIN'S FAMILY
**Orin's pledge:** "I will find our people. I will not leave them behind."
- **Orin's family** is only one of many scattered by the malicious catastrophe. The search includes unrelated witch parents, children, elders, workers, teachers, healers, bellsmiths, entire refuge camps and people possibly remaining within sealed Veylora. Each has independent identity and agency.
- **Scope:** establish contact, account for status where evidence allows, send aid, safely reconnect loved ones, and restore the RIGHT to choose whether to return, settle elsewhere, or stay in adopted communities. Do NOT equate rescue with forcibly moving everyone to Earth or back to Veylora. Do NOT assert everybody is alive, the entire population evacuated, exact total survivors or instant full census.
- **Method:** surviving partial Veylora evacuation ledgers + physical testimony + verified bell relay signals + real local humanitarian partners + returned signed letters. Maintain an evolving Book of Lost Bells with UNKNOWN/REPORTED/CONFIRMED CONTACT/NEEDS HELP/SAFE IN PLACE/RETURN CHOSEN HOME states and evidence sources. No omniscient location map or unverifiable total success stats.
- **Scale:** Orin orchestrates a growing cooperative network of survivors, skilled repaired-route bellkeepers and consenting host-world communities. Mara's Bell family is the trusted *forward team*, not the only workers or an army with unlimited reach. The new partner groups independently help people in their own worlds.
- **Causality:** recover one real person/group → get firsthand leads to other refugee camps → locate another responding Gate Bell via a known verified relay → test GREEN two-way route → deliver aid/contact → expand safe response. Some leads fail and must be reported as UNKNOWN. One world may contain MANY separated Veylora people; not one new world per person.
- **Role separation:** Bubu provides voice link only on GREEN/AMBER real signal paths, not universal family telepathy; Evan resolves letters/identity after approved knowledge reveal; Juno/Bix measure/record, Lia empathizes/helps locals; Mara decides safe travel and safeguarding of dependents. If the route is GRAY, nobody crosses because of a desperate child or beloved relative.
- **Ethical boundary:** children's safety, consent of displaced people and local communities, identity verification and refuge capacity matter. Earth's private Gate Bell is neither public nor capable of resettling the whole Veylora population.
- **Moral continuity:** even after grandmother, Rowan and Cael are recovered or contacted, **Orin and Mara remain committed to everyone else**. Finding one's own family is not the ending; restoring connection and hope to the whole dispersed community is.

# 2. Fixed world chronology (no arbitrary time travel)
## BEFORE catastrophe
- Veylora is a populated homeland with schools, villages, markets and many witches who USE bells but cannot forge them.
- Fixed World Gate Bells at stations across thousands of worlds respond to their unique musical addresses; the Great Tower coordinated a registry/permissions/evacuation. A few verified point-to-point backup paths predate the sabotage.
- Different people held technical/registry/safety responsibilities. Orin led and supervised without personally reading each restricted archive page every day.
- Earth was THE SILENT WORLD, sealed after an earlier expedition's verified return channel failed; officials additionally prohibited public magic among nonmagical humans. Ancient Earth Gate Bell is physically present but corroded/disconnected; magic in Earth is not universally impossible.

## THE NIGHT OF BROKEN BELLS
- An actor DELIBERATELY corrupted the Great Tower's active route assignment/permission control; resulting unsafe gates open toward wrong physical destinations. Dangerous cross-world forces rupture Veylora's central area. People must evacuate or be pulled through unstable gates.
- Orin orders legitimate emergency rescue. Some of the destination assignments are modified AFTER the emergency order; travelers scatter through uncontrolled unsafe one-way exits, often losing their portable keys.
- Orin and Mara are separately ejected toward forbidden Earth, arriving at different places there. Grandma activates ONE-TIME automatically sustained emergency isolation of the central Veylora gateway, then an unstable evac gate sweeps HER to a different world with refugees.
- Mara's eldest brother (working name ROWAN) is cast to a sea world; second elder brother (working name CAEL) to another forest world. The attacker and any protective lock operator must NOT be assumed same person.
- Veylora's central access is now sealed for safety. Its complete physical fate remains unknown to the family and audience, rather than incorrectly claiming it vanished.

## YEARS OF EXILE & RESTORATION
- Orin and Mara find one another on Earth through work, not instant telepathy. Orin's burnt travel bell preserves a faint forbidden-world signal; they find the ancient hidden physical Earth Gate Bell in the Bellfern area. Mara's eventual concealed workshop protects this recovered original station.
- Orin spends years repairing the station, salvaging scarce material, testing a remaining limited **prepaired backup** route to a known responsive gate, first by sound, then inert object round-trips, then supervised crossing. Very few safe routes are restored; Earth public quarantine remains active.
- Mara marries ordinary Evan, Juno and Lia grow up on Earth; Evan's parents live nearby; school/work keep the family grounded. Orin continues looking for his wife, sons and missing clan.
- Present EP001 onward: Orin has few GREEN routes, many unreachable worlds, gives specific justified missions; Bell family undertakes safe adventures and returns home. There is ongoing evidence of interference in some reopened routes.

# 3. Bell operating rules (simple and inescapable)
**The audience-facing sentence:** "Every world has one big bell. Our little bell is the key. Both big bells must answer before the door opens."

**World Gate Bell:** ONE fixed main Gate Bell / registered World Address per world, with a safe station.  
**Travel Bell:** small portable pre-enchanted key with LIMITED permitted world addresses; magic is in bell, not necessarily traveler. Evan may cross Mara's portal; later can use one Orin-issued old prepared key only after proper on-screen family-disclosure and safety training.  
**Bell Directory:** registry of signatures, local permission, route links, safe arrival pads and confirmed return.  
**Normal departure:** Source station + destination verified station authenticate; both bells answer; an actual safe doorway opens on the station pad; people walk through. **Return separately checked** before children go.  
**Relays:** The Great Tower used to route most connections, and a few old point-to-point paths remain; no direct all-to-all magic. Orin repairs one verified pair at a time.  
**Forbidden:** a route can be technically possible but legally prohibited; this remains a story problem.  
**Forced one-way:** emergency exit failure used extraordinary source-side energy to THROW refugees outward even though destination had no safe return. Never used as a normal rescue shortcut.

**Five route statuses:**
- GREEN = independently VERIFIED, permissions, two-way travel safe;
- AMBER = weak communication only (speech/brief signal via a real surviving path, NO person/object crossing);
- BLUE = testing with inert objects, NOT family safe;
- GRAY = disconnected/unknown, NO voice and NO travel;
- RED = legally prohibited, physically unsafe or tampered, NO family travel.

**Remote repair limits:** One repaired route may reach an already functioning nearby station. A completely destroyed receiving bell needs a local expert, spare parts or another separately verified path, not Orin's remote imagination. Rare metal, skilled bellsmith time, ethical local consent and return proofs constrain all expansion.

# 4. Bubu family communication — distinct from travel
- Orin calls from his prepared Travel Bell **within signal reach of a known station or authorized relay** using a private family call rhythm. Bubu's one little collar bell receives and voices Orin's words for people near him, and sends their voluntary replies along that existing path. Bubu is not a universal transmitter and cannot hear Orin in an isolated dead zone.
- GREEN supports live short two-way speech; AMBER carries a short broken voice/echo but CANNOT transport bodies; GRAY gives nothing; RED forged calls require independent identity verification. A familiar voice alone is not proof of who/when.
- Bubu hears disturbed magical residue and occasionally a **short, uncertain sound fragment** from a physical real bell part near him. He needs documentation, Bix's measurements, Evan's dates and witnesses for conclusions. He cannot locate missing grandma/brothers until real paths are restored, see all history or conjure a gate. Brief resonance support of an ALREADY OPEN gate is a limited last resort, never a travel substitute.
- He remains Lia's beloved funny companion with his own desire, errors and growth; never reduce him to a mobile router. His approval-locked purple spiral tail always keeps sparkles and exactly one collar bell.

# 6. What makes a legitimate Orin mission?
Every Orin task has (1) a genuine or independently checked sender, (2) concrete local problem, (3) reason Mara's team is appropriate while he is elsewhere, (4) GREEN travel/return verified or else ONLY remote diagnosis, (5) consent/safety plan, (6) meaningful choice and cost, (7) proof or honest inconclusive report and (8) durable change to people/world map/mystery.
**Varied starts:** an Orin assignment, ally distress, event in Earth workshop from a known safe route, an Earth-side letter, a friend/child's promise, village revisit or an Orin side adventure. Family may refuse/change an unethical mission. Ordinary local people always have independent lives, not just give the family a clue.
**Family returns home** for Evan's post work, children’s school and friends, grandparents, equipment/route logs and the private safer Earth home station. Longer weekend stays are legitimate if planned. Earth secrecy is valuable yet may be compromised by overuse.

# 7. Mystery architecture (NO definitive culprit names in public)

(iii) Who actually signed evacuation and who changed destinations afterwards? (iv) Who triggered the protective lock and why did that operator end up in another world? (v) Why was ancient Earth closed and why did it open during catastrophe? (vi) Why does active interference continue in today's repaired paths? (vii) Where are the actual lost relatives and what do they now WANT?
**Fair-play evidence:** genuine original signed evacuation order, later route-address revisions, technician tool provenance, witness statements, time-stamped old maps, local documents, physical gate damage, Bubu's fragment (NEVER sufficient alone), brother's independent records, grandma's firsthand account. These must be planted BEFORE the reveal and cross-checked.
**No cheap twists:** no last-minute new powers, memory wipes, fake deaths, infinite teleport, magical innocence by feeling, or revealing a new unknown main villain at episode 999.
