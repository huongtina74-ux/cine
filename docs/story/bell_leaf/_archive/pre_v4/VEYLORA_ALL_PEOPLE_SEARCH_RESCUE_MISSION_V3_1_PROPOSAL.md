# 1. New binding premise for writers
**ENGLISH A2 LOGLINE:** "When the magic bells break, people from Veylora are lost across many worlds. Grandpa Orin and the Bell family promise to find them, help them, and bring families together. Each new door saves someone—and reveals another part of the truth."
**MISSION VOW (Orin):** "I will find our people. I will not leave them behind."
**Daily series question:** "Who needs help today, and what must the family risk or give up to help?"
**Long series question:** "How can a broken civilization restore contact and freedom across many worlds without repeating the abuse that broke the network?"
**Emotional intimacy:** missing grandma/Rowan/Cael remain a personal heartbreak, but the plot constantly features many families with equally real emotions, grief, culture and goals. Mara chooses to help even when the lost person is unrelated to her.

# 2. Map of the scattered population — distinguish physical outcome, not only family
**During catastrophe:** some residents were pulled through unstable emergency one-way gates to entirely different worlds; some reached the SAME worlds in groups; some wandered far from the local station and lost their bells; some may have remained on Veylora when its central route was sealed. Not all destinations are known. Do NOT assume every resident evacuated, every resident lives, or the same world houses each cohort.

**The recognizable story groups:**
A. Lone displaced person, no portable key and no registered location.
B. Separated families, each relative landing in a different world (parents, siblings, children).
C. Large refugee camp/community sharing an arrival world; no bell specialist, variable local welcome.
D. The vulnerable: children, injured people, elders and caretakers; protection and consent priority, age-appropriate depiction.
E. Trained workers dispersed: bellsmiths, route recorders, healers, teachers, craftspeople, couriers, protectors and ordinary people with actual expertise.
F. Groups integrated into their new host communities; they may not WANT to leave, and deserve choice.
G. Distress signals/survivors near physically damaged or CLOSED worlds; contact ≠ transport.
H. People whose status remains genuinely unknown; avoid fictional promises of guaranteed life/rescue.

**World community realism:** A rescue concerns refugees AND existing locals, languages/customs, safety limits and reciprocity; the Bell family cannot commandeer another world's food, homes or permission without consent.

# 3. Rescue network, not one family teleporting to everyone
**Orin is responsible as clan head, not a one-man omniscient superhero.** He oversees an emerging **Veylora Search and Reconnection Network** (working title only; not an already constituted organization from episode 1).
- **Orin — Strategic coordination:** keeps original but incomplete pre-disaster census, final evacuation rolls, surviving route and station records; trains/coordinates discovered specialists; personally repairs only the toughest gates; investigates current sabotage and looks for missing wife and sons too.
- **Mara's Bell family — Forward Rescue Team:** enters only GREEN already verified worlds, listens to residents, searches for dispersed people, delivers contact, brings material/evidence, helps allies and resolves local problems. Mara leads and can refuse dangerous orders.
- **Bubu — Signal/voice liaison:** Orin can call family via existing GREEN or weak AMBER link; a found refugee may speak to relatives on other connected safe routes. Bubu cannot call unknown worlds with absolutely zero relay, auto-track blood relatives or open a door.
- **Evan — Postal/identity liaison:** after approved on-screen secret reveal, verifies personal names, letters, addresses, family matching and forged messages. Before reveal, he contributes naturally to Earth family life without magic knowledge.
- **Juno/Bix — Tools and ledger:** build low-risk scanners and document logs, record signal state, repair nonmagical infrastructure, never conjure rare bell metal or bypass permissions.
- **Lia — Empathy and children/animals:** hears consenting local animals, notices frightened children and survivors, upholds dignity, cannot magically sense all dispersed people.
- **Restored world partners:** consenting local stationkeepers, rescued bellsmiths, volunteers and community leaders can conduct separate support/search actions in their OWN safe jurisdictions. They report genuine evidence; they do not all become the Bell family's followers.
- **Recovered survivor-led search groups:** as the network grows, adult refugees independently map addresses and distribute aid. This enables scaling from one small family to a civilization-wide response without breaking geography or the main cast's agency.

# 4. Missing person & world census — the durable change after each meaningful event
Build the **Book of Lost Bells / The Names We Keep** (working screen-friendly name) at the concealed Earth workshop, with offsite copies on other verified worlds, not a universal magical GPS.
**Entry fields:** PERSON_ID/ALIAS; old Veylora home/community; last verifiable seen place/date; suspected/current WORLD_ID; fate/status UNKNOWN, REPORTED, CONTACTED_UNVERIFIED, CONTACTED_CONFIRMED, NEEDS_HELP, SAFE_IN_PLACE, TRANSFER_REQUESTED, RETURNED_TO_CHOSEN_HOME, DECLINED_TRANSFER, DECEASED_VERIFIED (only with strong ethical evidence), DUPLICATE/UNRESOLVED; source/custody; permissions/privacy; dependents/relatives sought; assistance requested; actual available return route; scene/episode and last fact verified.
**Data sources:** remaining public registry, multiple evacuation witnesses, certified gate stations, personal letters, physical keepsakes, old maps, aid lists, tested local signal relays. Some records may be stolen/forged. Two witnesses who copied ONE original error are not independent proof.
**Screen experience:** children can understand a named person moving from "MISSING" to "HEARD FROM" to "SAFE", or being told "we still don't know." Do not overfocus numerical dashboards or bureaucratic exposition. Orin's ledger is a moral reminder, not merely a prop.
**No fabricated counts:** show a growing wall of real names without inventing total population, specific percentage rescued, or asserting a complete original census.

# 5. The causal search protocol, from silence to reunion
1. A known person/community, Orin's damaged ledger, or a local resident provides a **TRACE** of a Veylora refugee, possibly a world name or item.
2. Independent **VERIFY**: compare dates, identities, world Gate Bell note, location; forged or mistaken references are plausible but not an excuse for endless filler.
3. Decide the ACTUAL NEED: immediate medical/shelter aid, message to family, reunification in place, physical extraction, root gate repair, or patient listening and safety.
4. **Route check**: GREEN = Mara may visit with tested 2-way return and local permission. AMBER = Bubu and Orin relay short fragmented speech, no living travelers. GRAY = NO call or travel; Orin can only work from neighboring known relays/local allies/evidence. RED = prohibit travel. BLUE = inert tests only.
5. **Local story**: the team cooperates with host community, faces a conflict that can be resolved honestly TODAY. The Veylora person has agency, not a passive MacGuffin.
6. **Transfer only if asked, feasible and safe.** Some refugees prefer to live in host world and only receive contact; not every reunited family wants to move to Earth/Veylora. No rescue means kidnapping.
7. **Record outcomes**, update lives/contact routes/real resources, then a new trace may open several OTHER people/communities. Every substantial search episode changes measurable facts, social trust or route capacity.
**Proof of progress:** new survivor verified, safe contact maintained, children/family reunited, actual rescue delivery accepted, or a specific obstacle removed. A false trace with an ethical/local payoff can count as story but cannot fake progress in search statistics.

# 6. Why all people cannot simply return to Earth or Veylora now
- Earth is restricted and the private repaired Earth station has finite capacity/permissions; it is not a refugee resettlement solution for all of Veylora, and Mara's human home cannot house huge crowds.
- Veylora's own Gate Bell is not safely accessible; even if an ancient address is known, its two-way station fails; forced one-way disaster is not an ethical method of rescue.
- Many displaced witch travelers have lost travel bells, were sent to worlds with damaged Gate Bells, lack spell metal or specialists, or live too far from the current safe station.
- Some safe communities are allied but can't carry unlimited evacuees overnight; real shelter, food and transport capacity are constraints, making coordination necessary.
- Rescue is different from **choosing home**: the eventual rebuilt decentralized network should restore SAFE ACCESS and family contact so people can make their own choices.

# 7. What the family does BETWEEN grand reunions
**Small daily drama** → locate a Veylora child in a safe-market village, find a missing sibling, clear false identity, save a local harvest before asking for supplies, repair a bridge, deliver a letter, comfort an elder, arrange a doctor and local guide, help a person integrate.
**Mid-sized arcs** → find/refugee camp, establish witness/contact registry, repair station with old bellsmith and locals, restore a two-way family reunion, set up offsite support point in a world no longer isolated.
**Big sagas** → multiple worlds collaborate, former cast survivors now run their own search teams; expose forged evacuation orders; access Veylora/resolve original attack and create fair new bell governance.
**Orin direct orders** alternate with refugees' requests, Earth supernatural events, personal promises and local-world mysteries. Avoid repetitive "new missing person + rescue + clue to Isel" formula. Mysteries can exist without new people found, and many rescue episodes need no villain clue.

# 8. Three sample engines (not episode outline commitments)
**A. 'The School Without Parents'** — Orin asks Mara to verify whether a Veylora refugee child reported in the SAFE cloud town is related to a missing caravan. Local teacher refuses to hand child over to strangers, correctly demanding proof. Evan, if already aware, verifies competing family letters; Lia earns child's trust; Juno discovers a forged arrival date. Twist: child's true relatives are still missing, but a different refuge camp found safe contact by joining the address records. Payoff: child is safely cared for and contacts a relative through a tested relay; no forced adoption/transfer.
**B. 'A Town of Lost Bells'** — a known GREEN world has an enclave of dozens of Veylora residents; Orin needs a specific bellsmith but the community suffers from water shortages. Mara chooses to repair local supply with their help before seeking parts. Twist: the bellsmith refuses evacuation because they have become the town's doctor and will not abandon residents. They agree to aid route repair remotely with a locally secured specialist. Payoff: community stabilizes, wider clan contact improves, new practical route repair becomes possible.
**C. 'The Voice of a Thousand Names'** — Bubu receives a short broken AMBER message listing names. Family first thinks it is a current call from living refugees. Bix/Evan's timestamps reveal it was a recorded evacuation log, not a live message. Crucially it provides verified names and a lead to a SAFE relay, but NO automatic portal to the blocked origin. They save a present-day local family, protect the data and honestly tell Orin who is confirmed vs unknown.
No plot may assert a missing person is alive merely because their name appears on an old list.

# 9. 1,000-episode macro structure — collective rescue now leads
**001–100:** Orin formally gives the family the FIRST rescue and reconnection mandates, tests ordinary safe routes, rescues/contacts several *unrelated* Veylora families, begins trustworthy Lost Bells register; Aila EP001 pilot remains consistent; first real lead about Mara's sibling emerges.
**101–200:** identify first refugee enclave, achieve a meaningful group support milestone, verify/contact Rowan only through earned relay/safe trip. The found people contribute sources for other missing groups.
**201–350:** rescued survivors run regional search contacts; reconnect families in 2–3 existing worlds, find Cael and uncover conflicting historic address logs. No magic network-wide census.
**351–500:** major separated-family reunion cases; discover lost grandmother in OTHER world, establish an earned signal/route and a choice to protect local refugee children before evacuation.
**501–650:** multiple safe partner stations and search teams emerge; trace missing communities and contemporaneous forged messages; find evidence of deliberate registry tampering while expanding rescue.
**651–750:** culprit becomes identifiable via independently tested evidence; ACTIVE hostility threatens refugee routes and search teams. Protect people before confronting the conspiracy.
**751–850:** Veylora survivors and host communities organize decentralized safety/identity verifications, shelter and consent-based travel; protect existing connections and widen recovery.
**851–950:** earn entry to Veylora, clarify fate of any residents who stayed, restore genuinely safe two-way links and reunite widely dispersed communities as choices allow.
**951–1000:** conclude principal criminal responsibility, scalable ethical governance, reunions and the future of Veylora; not everyone must move physically. Resolution standard: no abandoned known survivors, credible plan for unknown cases and local-world autonomy. Do not promise statistical perfection or a magical instant sweep.
