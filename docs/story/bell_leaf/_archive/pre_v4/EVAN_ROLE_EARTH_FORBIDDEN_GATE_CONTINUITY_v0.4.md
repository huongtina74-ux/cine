## 1. What MUST remain true and what must be APPROVED as an evolving plot
**Current approved starting state:** Evan is a non-magical human postman and *unaware of magic*. Evan's parents (paternal grandparents) are ordinary and unaware. The family loves each other. Orin is head of witch clan, Keeper of Bells; Mara witch; Juno/Lia children; Bix/Bubu locked. No retcon that Evan has always known; no grant of magical powers, no replacement of him by a witch/fighter.

**PROPOSED STORY EVENT, needs owner/showrunner approval:** Evan discovers the actual truth near the beginning of the broadcast narrative, not years earlier. Suggested precise version: **EP002, 'Sunday with Grandma'**, as a real moral choice and a compelling episode climax, while ordinary grandparents remain unaware. From EP003 onward Evan can join selected journeys through an authorized bell, always with permission and a verified Earth-return anchor. If the showrunner prefers late reveal, the state must remain UNAWARE until that on-screen event and every episode must obey the earlier Canon.

### Why Mara withheld the truth without making her cruel
Earth was a forbidden world. Mara survived a disaster and sincerely believed revealing her clan's secret might put Evan and children at risk and expose a forbidden route. Her secrecy is a flawed protective decision with relational cost, **not automatically justified by love**. Evan has the right to feel excluded, ask difficult questions and require no more lies about danger to their children. Trust is restored through real actions over multiple episodes, not a single apology.

### A father's independent character engine
- **WANT:** Keep his family safe, remain a good father and be part of the truth.
- **NEED:** Trust without magical strength; learn that bringing care, patience and knowledge can save people.
- **FEAR:** Mara and the children are leading lives from which he is excluded; he might fail to bring them home.
- **FLAW:** Tries to solve too much alone and mistakes keeping family safe for preventing every risk.
- **CHOICE:** Join when capable, respect Mara's expertise, and refuse unsafe gates rather than acting recklessly.
- **UNIQUE NONMAGICAL ADVANTAGE:** Postal routes, postmarks, sorting, handwriting, return addresses, records, interviewing ordinary people and noticing a *wrong address* before anyone mistakes it for a lost relative. Orin knows magical routing; Evan understands human meaning of sending and receiving.
- **RELATIONSHIP:** With Mara, trust/agency; with Juno, inventive craft versus practical postal tools; with Lia, compassion, listening and steady emotional support; with Orin, initial distrust of an ordinary man, later respect based on evidence and courage; with grandparents, still authentic son and caregiver, without exposing them to magic.

### Why he cannot join EVERY trip
He has postal work, ordinary family duties, and does not have a magical body. He travels on weekends, holidays and occasional urgent weekday cases, while Mara and children may travel after school without him. Family dinners remain the common emotional rhythm. A single 45-minute adventure can feature Evan as the central protagonist; the next can center Juno or Lia. This improves novelty and prevents "father disappears" without destroying Bellfern everyday life.

## 2. Earth: two separate facts, not one undifferentiated 'impossible' statement
**POLITICAL/ETHICAL CLOSED:** Since long before the catastrophe the Veylora council forbade routine travel and public spellcasting on Earth, to avoid interfering with its nonmagical people. Orin as clan chief should know/accept the prohibition. The ban does NOT mean all magic is physically impossible; Orin and Mara carry limited magic with them.
**ROUTE TECHNICALLY SEALED:** The original known Earth world address was removed from public route maps and its Veylora Great Tower Root Bell branch was sealed. Ancient travelers might have been there; no verified official return for generations. Earth has a weak/noisy return bell response, making round trip unusually difficult. "Forbidden" therefore has law, history, safety and technical form, without falsely saying no witch ever reached Earth.

**Key mystery:** During deliberate network sabotage, the sealed Earth world key sounded and *ONE-WAY chaotic evacuation* pulled Orin and Mara through separate temporary emergency gates. A one-way violent evacuation is NOT a repaired safe route. Someone/thing bypassed an ancient world restriction. The audience's deep question persists: WHY did Earth's prohibited address become active? Orin may have been targeted/exiled or protected, but there is no early proof.

## 3. How Orin legally/technically re-established a PRIVATE RETURN without making Earth an OPEN world
**One world key remains:** No second independent 'Earth Root Bell' is invented. The Earth world key remains one unique signature/address. The original Great Tower *public Earth connection stays sealed*. Orin reconstructs a **small receiving end/return anchor of that SAME world address** at Mara's protected workshop — a privately paired terminal, not a new Root Bell and not a restored open public network.

**Before:** Earth existed only as an ancient quarantined address. Arrival from Veylora was a one-way accidental/emergency tear; Orin and Mara were stranded. 
**After YEARS of work, in steps:**
1. Orin studies the burnt travel bell carried out of Veylora. Its metal kept a weak physical trace of the Earth gate note from the night of evacuation; surviving notebooks explain the ancient restricted address in incomplete form. No clean universal map.
2. From the damaged bell and a tiny quantity of rare metal carried/salvaged from the evacuation, plus locally available NON-magical casings and stabilizers, Orin rebuilds an **Earth reception resonator** inside Mara's protected workshop. Several attempts fail; Earth scarcity is a real obstacle.
3. The damaged personal travel bell ALSO preserves one or two old verified reachable world-address notes (not an unlimited list). Orin can send a weak diagnostic ping to one known World Root Bell via a surviving **point-to-point bypass relay** that does NOT require the central Veylora tower to operate. This 'limited cross-world bypass' is a **new mechanism proposal**: it reconciles Veylora central collapse with any subsequent travel. It is a limited backup route, not a massive second network.
4. The remote verified World Bell replies; Orin first tests a small inert non-living object, then its return. Repeated tests take time; one signal is NOT a usable passage. Once two-way tests pass, the first Earth-to-known-world route is usable.
5. From that first world, Orin collects genuine rare resonant components/receives help from the resident bellkeeper and repairs stronger local Earth reception/return. He reaches other worlds through their VERIFIED relays one by one.
6. Mara's private bell and Orin's personal bell store the signed, tested **paired link** between Earth Home Anchor and selected safe world anchors. Only authorized paired travel bells can dial Earth; an ordinary witch in an unknown world still CANNOT open the private Earth destination by chanting its name.
7. Earth's original statutory ban is NOT automatically lifted. Orin's limited emergency exception is morally/legally contentious: he keeps a secret route to preserve his stranded family and rescue efforts. Revelation of this unauthorized secret can form a future political story, not handwaved as 'because he is head'.

**This is NOT a public network reactivation:** one private receiving terminal, restricted keys, scarce fuel/parts, limited verified paths, risk of instability, ongoing ban; sealed public Earth Root Bell branch intact. Nor does it let Orin go straight to Veylora: its central return remains unusable.

**The plot-worthy weakness:** Earth return anchor may lose a limited safety reserve or become detectable if overused. It can be temporarily suspended; ordinary family adventures require testing and a firm return plan. No daily arbitrary stranding. Private route registers and spare return components must be tracked by episode.

## 4. Travel policy after Evan is on-screen informed
- EP001 pilot: Evan unaware. He deals with genuine everyday postal/family responsibilities. The existing Aila rescue, limited bell, flying suitcase and grandparents' supper remain intact.
- EP002: house bell sound threatens to reveal magic to visiting grandma; Evan sees what cannot be explained (perhaps the hidden portal nearly catches Lia), and Mara CHOOSES to explain rather than concoct another excuse. Grandma does not see the portal. Evan is shocked, hurt at secrecy, but first protects family. **This is proposed deviation; NOT existing episode 2 canon.**
- EP003: after asking questions and learning emergency return procedure, Evan's **first crossing** to Underleaf happens for a specific human reason (e.g., a delivery/letter may help a resident). He can choose not to cross immediately if story needs a slower trust process; crossing no later than first arc's resolution only after evidence and family agreement.
- EP004–EP020: Evan becomes an active but selective travel participant. His human postal skills identify false addresses, letter trails and misidentified relatives. He is NEVER declared the inventor of magical bells.
- Ordinary paternal grandparents keep NOT KNOWING. Evan can now help Mara keep family safe without forcing children to lie to him.

**When mother and children travel without Evan:** He truly knows where they are after reveal, knows a safe return protocol and how long a trip should take, but does not micromanage Juno/Lia. He may keep a non-magical analog log of departure/return times while Mara checks the actual bell resonance.
**When Evan travels:** An authorized pre-tuned bell with Mara/Orin's explicit supervision and reliable two-way route only. Even an ordinary human may pass through a gate once opened: magical aptitude is required for forging/travel spell operation, NOT for walking through a safe doorway. A witch reads incantation/rings; Evan walks through along with family. In exceptional story moments, Evan might carry a registered bell and ring it to trigger pre-tuned fixed route only if world rules explicitly allow ordinary humans to do so; safest default is Mara opens and controls portals.

## 5. How the mystery links father + Earth paradox
- The first apparent 'Earth return address' may have been planted by a saboteur. Evan's postal instinct identifies one character of the ancient address that was **written differently in two independent records**. This is not a portal discovery by itself.
- Orin's official clan title doesn't guarantee he knew the restricted archive's full truth; council and bellmakers could have held parts separately. He must accept limits of his knowledge.
- A letter with a real Earth postal stamp but a pre-disaster date forces a question: was a witch already on Earth, or is the date forged? Don't assert magic time travel or grandfather-paradox without evidence.
- Evan's choice to keep people safe without power contrasts with any villain who seeks to control every route 'for safety'.
