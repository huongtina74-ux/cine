## VEYLORA LOST PEOPLE: SAFETY, NEW HOMES AND CHOSEN RETURN v3.2 — 2026-11-06
**Status:** PROPOSED SERIES BIBLE ADDENDUM on story branch only; not owner-approved Canon Lock; not a completed screenplay or implemented logistics system. Answers the showrunner question: "Vậy khi tìm được người thì sẽ để họ ở đâu?" 
**Supersedes underdeveloped assumptions:** Do NOT relocate all displaced Veylora people to Mara's Earth home, Orin's personal house, a magically infinite shelter, or Veylora while its gate remains sealed. Finding a person, establishing voice contact, getting them out, reuniting a family, and helping them choose a long-term home are FIVE different accomplishments.

## I. Central principle
**Rescue = SAFE + CONTACT + CHOICE + SUSTAINABLE LIVING.** Not every found refugee needs extraction. Orin promises to account for and help all displaced Veylora residents, including ordinary people, not simply to return his own relatives. Before relocating a person, verify their identity and wishes, host community permission, actual two-way safe route, food, water, shelter, protection, medical care, education for children, family links and longer-term livelihood.

## II. Five destination outcomes and what is possible
**A — SAFE WHERE FOUND / STAY BY CHOICE:** Resident has settled and locals accept them; restore family contact/records and offer continued help without taking them away. Very many people may prefer to stay.
**B — TEMPORARILY STUCK / HELP LOCALLY:** World lacks verified two-way return (AMBER signal-only or GRAY totally disconnected). MARA AND CHILDREN CANNOT CROSS. Orin sends only short messages via actual working relays if AMBER and arranges local allies, supplies from that side, technical repairs through a separate existing GREEN entrance. If GRAY, no live aid signal; a real found survivor at that world cannot be transported until a path is earned. No remote teleport or infinite rescue portal.
**C — VOLUNTARY SHORT-TERM TRANSFER:** An unsafe resident wants to leave; a green two-way permitted route and authorized capacity exist. Move to a PREPARED small reception centre on an established SAFE world that consents, with named hosts, medical triage, place for family, and plan to transfer/settle. Never default to Earth.
**D — FAMILY REUNIFICATION:** Verify family relationship and consent, identify which world both parties CHOOSE, confirm receiving-world permission and green path; then conduct accompanied transfer or restore messages if transfer not yet possible. For minors, protect identity and guardianship: a letter or surname alone is not proof.
**E — VEYLORA RETURN LATER:** After central gate is repaired, safe, and communities choose to return, help with rebuilding/visits/gradual repatriation. Not all survivors need to return; many may have married, worked or built homes in host worlds.

## III. Build an evolving distributed rescue/settlement network (NOT one magical refugee city)
**Phase 0 — EARLY EPISODES:** Initially Orin has only a FEW safe GREEN worlds. There is no established refugee capital. Family helps each discovered person primarily in place, through friends and established local safe places, while negotiating a small temporary reception room/house in one CONSENTING existing GREEN world. If one host refuses, that is a real obstacle, not proof of evil.
**Phase 1 — FIRST PERMITTED RECEPTION CENTRE:** Working title \`Bellhaven\` is a future possible COMMUNITY within an already established, verified GREEN world, not a new planet or instant connected gateway. Select geography and capacities only after story/host approval. Begin as a modest house/workshop/clinic with a local council; only limited people can enter. Capacity constrained by beds, food, staff and transport.
**Phase 2 — MULTIPLE SAFE HOST COMMUNITIES:** Displaced teachers, doctors, farmers, bellsmiths and craftspeople plus willing local people create small distributed centers, each with different economic strengths and needs, connected by actual individually repaired safe links. Local people retain sovereignty; refugees have voices in governance. No massive camp appears from a single magic spell.
**Phase 3 — CONSENTED SELF-GOVERNANCE:** Residents run placement, education, food, medical services and search letters; Orin remains technical/coordinating leader and does not own their lives. Earth keeps private restricted base used for coordination, equipment, Orin-family calls, not population housing or public immigration gateway.
**Phase 4 — ETHICAL HOMECOMING:** Once Veylora is verified safe, families may return slowly, stay in host worlds, or retain safe mutual travel. Rebuilding a homeland is an earned collective event, not a finale teleport of everyone.

### Carrying-capacity ledger: the nonmagical realism
For every reception community keep \`HOST_WORLD_ID\`, \`CONSENT_APPROVED\`, \`GREEN_TRAVEL_PAIR\`, \`BEDS_AVAILABLE\`, \`WATER_FOOD_FOR_30_DAYS\`, \`CLINIC_CAPACITY\`, \`CHILD_SAFE_GUARDIANS\`, \`JOBS_TRAINING\`, \`LANGUAGE_SUPPORT\`, \`RETURN_AND_TRANSIT_SAFETY\`, \`HOST_RELATIONS\`, \`CURRENT_RESIDENTS\`, \`APPROVAL_DATE\`. These are fictional dossier fields, not a real running database.
Never invent numbers unless established on-screen and logged. If capacity is zero, rescuers must find another permitted centre or arrange local shelter; DON'T stack hundreds of people in Mara's hidden workshop.

## IV. The five-stage displacement/rescue workflow
1. **VERIFY PERSON:** Name/old residence, independent witness, correct last known family ties, status alive or unknown. No fast sibling identification from a matching surname.
2. **ASSESS NEED:** danger, medical need, urgent separated children, emotional needs, desire to stay or go; ask the actual person.
3. **DECIDE PATH:** STAY LOCAL, SUPPLIES/CONTACT ONLY, EMERGENCY EXTRACTION, REUNIFY TO CHOSEN WORLD, WAIT FOR SAFE CORRIDOR or RETURN TO VEYLORA LATER. No destination by decree from Orin.
4. **VERIFY ROUTE/RECEPTION:** only GREEN carries bodies; AMBER voice cannot carry matter; source and destination Gate Bells answer, local receiving station & destination centre give consent; responsible adults accompany children. Earth private Gate Bell is not a public immigration centre.
5. **FOLLOW THROUGH:** record safe arrival, host contact, needs, relatives still missing, reunification chosen, local relationship changes; set next review. A rescue that loses the person into a new undocumented camp is not a success.

## V. Relationship and role arcs
- **ORIN:** as clan chief, must resist treating survivors as movable "assets" or counting rescues as success without consent. Learns to trust recovered survivors and local-world leaders to operate centres; balancing immediate family quest with entire scattered people makes ethical drama.
- **MARA:** accepts tasks, assesses vulnerable people in GREEN worlds; genuinely must return to Earth for Evan/Juno/Lia's school/home and workshop, not because she hoards all rescued people there. Has the courage to refuse an unethical mass transfer and to accept a survivor's chosen home.
- **EVAN:** after owner-approved on-screen magic disclosure, applies letters, addresses, record matching, practical food/material distribution, not a magical new power.
- **JUNO & BIX:** design nonmagical practical logistics, water filters, capacity measurements and safe route logs; Bix has NO bell and cannot synthesize magical metal.
- **LIA & BUBU:** help children feel seen; Lia has animal communication, not people-locator power; Bubu bridges short voice calls ONLY via existing signal paths, not a teleporter. Bubu's small collar bell is not a travel bell; purple spiral tail still sparkles.
- **ELARA/ROWAN/CAEL:** when found, may already lead local refuge efforts and choose temporary stay to protect others. Their reunions do NOT end Orin/Mara's obligation to unrelated families, and they are not automatically relocated to Earth.
- **LOCAL/REFUGEE LEADERS:** independent decision makers, not faceless thankful NPCs. Conflict about resources, customs, integration and receiving children can generate honest episodes.

## VI. A stand-alone dramatic case demonstrating why destination is an ACTUAL story
**Working story: "The Village That Said No".** Orin asks Mara to locate a particular Veylora bellsmith in an already GREEN sea world. On arrival, family discovers a whole Veylora neighborhood living beside the local harbor. A new reception centre elsewhere has asked to transfer them quickly, but the town refuses. Mara first mistakes the town for hostile. Local council proves their food and fresh water cannot sustain new refugees, and the bellsmith has become the only mechanic keeping the harbor pumps working; moving him would strand both refugees and local families. Family instead helps local villagers repair the pumps, negotiates limited aid, trains a local successor and establishes Bubu-bridged calls to faraway missing families over an actual signal path. Most residents choose to remain in the harbor; a few consent to move to a verified prepared reception site. Evidence about another evacuation convoy emerges from a saved registry. An ethical rescue ends with multiple chosen outcomes, one emotional family contact, a repaired water system and a genuine new lead. No unlimited Earth hosting, no false universal portal, no villain clue forced at the end.

**Twist:** the villagers' "NO" wasn't cruelty; it protected the safety and choices of the people living there. The family learns not to mistake transfer for rescue.

## VII. Narrative growth and 1,000-episode pacing
**001–100:** individualized cases, families reunited by LETTER or SIGNAL first; no fully built refugee capital. Earn the first tiny receiving community; establish meaningful small capacities.
**101–250:** first refugee community and its governance disputes; some local solutions, some voluntary resettlement; repaired routes permit a few meaningful reunions.
**251–500:** survivors become network operators; build and staff several separate reception sites; find Rowan/Cael/Elara but their own local responsibilities prevent automatic extraction.
**501–750:** misinformation / forged rescue missions and political struggle over who gets access, which corridor is actually safe, not just villain flashbacks. Help people during attacks on existing centers.
**751–900:** multiple communities participate in truly decentralized rescue and self-governance, preventing another one-person monopoly over routes without publishing any secret antagonist identity.
**901–1000:** after safe entry to Veylora becomes possible, support return for those who elect it, contact those who remain away, and account honestly for unrecovered/missing cases. Avoid guaranteed everyone alive/endless capacity.
