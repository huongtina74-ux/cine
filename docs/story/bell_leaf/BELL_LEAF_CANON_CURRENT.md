## 1. Genre, audience, and format
- Original family-friendly cinematic **STYLIZED 3D CGI** fantasy animated series, with genuine humor, adventure, emotion, suspense, mysteries, and emotionally meaningful character choices. Story first; not a vocabulary lesson, not a procedural repeat.
- Understandable **General American A2 English** dialogue, acting and lip-sync; consistent approved voice and face on every shot.
- 1,000+ episode long-term universe; individual full episodes 40–50 min (target ~45), but NEVER claim complete scripts exist merely because a treatment or prompt pack exists. Each video generation SHOT is ONE 10-second continuous take, 16:9, 24fps desired export, no subtitle/text on frames.
- Each episode starts with a new surprising world/event/magical occurrence or honest consequence, resolves a local goal fully, and advances real persistent consequences. Larger mysteries may be teased, but do not demand Isel cameo or new world in every episode.
- New viewers may watch an episode alone; loyal viewers benefit from character growth, changes to world societies, rescue progress, verified mystery evidence and recurring antagonists.

## 3. Veylora crisis and catastrophe (do not retcon)
Veylora's regenerative Living Current / Mạch Sống had declined gradually for generations from expanding gate traffic, energy concentration and extraction that outpaced ecological replenishment. The earlier decline is REAL and predates the villain's sabotage. Wizards built FIXED station bells in many already mapped worlds, hoping different regional technologies/magic could save their civilization. The antagonist's deliberate central resonance intervention and bypassing of safety limits caused the catastrophic failure: some Veylora people escaped through reachable KNOWN-world gates, others swept into UNKNOWN-world rifts. Orin and Mara were **accidentally** thrown onto UNMAPPED EARTH and later reunited. Elara, Rowan, Cael and many ordinary Veylorans are missing; searching for ALL people matters, not just the family.

## 4. Why Earth matters
Each world has **MAGIC AFFINITY** (particular technique/material/ecology) and separate **MAGIC RENEWAL** (capacity to regenerate weakened inner magic sustainably). New/old worlds may excel at one or several specialties but not heal a damaged overall regenerative loop. Earth's living cycles in water, soil, organisms, air and plants gradually restore depleted witch magic and Veylora plants, NOT instantly or infinitely. Earth was **never on the pre-disaster route map**, because weak balanced signal evaded surveys favoring strong peak magic. Its ecology must be protected; no right to extract without consent. Discoveries from OLD and NEW realms ultimately help rebuild Veylora's damaged circulation. Earth may be the first known broad-regenerative realm, not logically necessarily the only realm in infinite worlds.

## 5. TWO different portal mechanisms
**FIXED KNOWN-WORLD GATE BELL**: existing historical gate STATION at a mapped world; enter invocation and ring the STATION bell. No compulsory per-person travel bells, including Orin/Evan. Post-catastrophe some routes are broken, unsafe or one-way and need verified physical repair and permission. An EARTH workshop fixed station is BUILT ONLY AFTER accidental first arrival, paired over years to a SMALL number of genuinely repaired old-world gates. No hidden ancient Earth Gate.
**BUBU'S LIVING UNKNOWN-WORLD BELL**: Bubu's ONE gold neck bell hears SPECIFIC rupture echoes tied to an **uncharted** place; with enough stamina, safety and willingness can open a SHORT temporary passage. Family does NOT freely choose any planet, and an echo might be old, distorted or false. The independent prepared Earth HOME ANCHOR permits a limited return for a small nearby group when Bubu, the anchor and space are working; NO instant universal evacuation or fixed public access. He may convey Orin's voice ONLY when a real resonance path works. Discovery of unknown world does not automatically create a permanent mapped-world gate.

## 6. Character adventure split — flexible but FAMILY FIRST
**ORIN USUALLY explores OLD mapped worlds:** repairs gates, tracks missing refugees, gathers physical evidence and traces way home to Veylora. He is MOSTLY OFF-SCREEN and rarely appears directly. Mara family leads the show; never create a separate full-time Orin series.
**MARA + EVAN + JUNO + LIA + BIX + BUBU USUALLY explore NEW unknown worlds** led by Bubu's legitimate echoes, revisiting previously discovered unknown worlds for deep local stories. They occasionally go to an OLD world for a **specific** Orin request, distress from friends, missing-person discovery, urgent unexpected event, or crossover with real emotional cause. Earth family life, correspondence, school and ordinary grandparents can be the main location for entire surprising episodes.
**V4.8 EXCEPTION:** ORIN may RARELY and unexpectedly detect a NEW-world signal or be swept through a genuine unstable residual rift while working at an OLD gate. A signal alone CANNOT transport him. His return requires an earned functioning breach or later specifically matched Bubu link; don't let a stuck Orin phone home with no path. Surprise must remain physically causal.
**Trial—not quotas:** for Mara-family episodes roughly 65% NEW-world locations including revisits, 20% Earth, 10% exceptional OLD visits, 5% cross-world; Orin's rare uncharted events are allowed, not blocked by these planning ratios. Reassess from actual viewer metrics, not guessed psychology.

## 7. Persistent villain — publicly spoiler-safe synopsis
A respected Veylora rescue coordinator secretly uses an ASH-BELL masked persona on reachable OLD mapped-gate routes. He is a REAL active opponent who may save refugees genuinely and also control access, steal ledgers, manipulate permissions and damage local ecosystems. He believes centralized authority can save Veylora; his method threatens other worlds' autonomy. EARTH was unknown to him during the catastrophe and must be found later through clues/real routes; he cannot teleport to Bubu-only unknown realms. Some episodes involve totally independent local conflict. The villain's civilian identity, reveal chronology and exact final solution belong to a **LOCAL showrunner-only secret**, never public video/prompts or GitHub spoiler-safe notes.
Narrative strategy: viewers may learn masked identity earlier than public proof, allowing suspense when Mara trusts someone. The eventual public case needs hardware, time stamps and independent witness testimony. Never rely solely on "Bubu senses evil."

## 8. Longform episode engine: 40–50 minutes
