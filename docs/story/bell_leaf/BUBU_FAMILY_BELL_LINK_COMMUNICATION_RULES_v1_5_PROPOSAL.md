# BELL & LEAF — BUBU, THE FAMILY BELL LINK
## Interworld Family Communication Story Mechanic v1.5 — 2026-11-06
**Status:** Showrunner creative proposal, NOT Canon Lock, not part of active main series bible. New family-communication role was explicitly requested by user: "bubu có thể kết nối orin với mọi người trong gia đình". Must integrate with restricted-world travel / missing family mysteries and Bubu's existing visual identity. No complete screenplay/timed A2 review is claimed.

### Canon facts that must not change
1. BUBU remains the original user-approved small purple cat bell spirit, glittering sparkling spiraling purple tail and **ONE** small gold collar bell, affectionate companion of Lia. His collar bell is NOT a Travel Bell and cannot open a world gateway or create a new route. No redesign, substitute cat images, new face, or extra bells.
2. World travel still requires existing World Gate Bell in each world, verified address, two-way safety handshake, permitted prepared Travel Bell and return route. The family cannot travel to an unknown lost planet because someone hears a clue.
3. Orin is clan head and Keeper of Bells, increasingly dispersed exploring/restoring routes; Mara, Juno, Lia, Bix and sometimes nonmagical Evan take verified missions and return to Earth family home.
4. Mara's mother and TWO elder brothers are dispersed in distinct, initially unreachable worlds per updated showrunner direction, not secretly physically trapped inside Veylora. Their proposed supporting names/looks require separate approval; no automatic rescue or location reveal.

### The core role: BUBU is the living FAMILY BELL LINK
Bubu's ONE collar bell acts as a **living receiver, conversational interpreter and consent-based relay** for the family's private communication signature. Orin's own Travel Bell can ring a short family-call pattern through *an existing connected or partially responsive relay.* Bubu responds via the magic of the collar bell and shares Orin's speech aloud for anyone beside Bubu to hear. Those people can speak back; Bubu sends their voices along the same small signal line.

**Simple on-screen rule:** "Orin calls. Bubu hears. The family can answer." Bubu is **not** a radio tower broadcasting to every world at once. The physical relay path remains necessary even for a weak signal.

**Family ID vs magical physical path:** Orin's family-signature identifies who is attempting contact, but it does not create a physical route between cut-off worlds. Analogy: the contact is saved, but no network signal means no call. The magical conversation energy is held/translated by Bubu's collar bell, not naturally stored in Evan's human body or requiring every family member to learn magic. Evan can talk to Orin alongside Mara/Juno/Lia as soon as the family's magical secret is disclosed in an owner-approved on-screen event; at earlier scenes he is unaware and must not overhear implausibly.

### Four communication levels (no unearned omnipotence)
- **GREEN — SAFE_CONNECTED:** Orin's current world and Bubu's location are on verified safe paired route(s) with a functioning voice signal. LIVE two-way family voice, short calls. A mission can be assigned, checked, and approved. Other people at either end may join only when caller consents.
- **AMBER — SIGNAL_ONLY:** damaged but recognizable relay returns a small weak echo; Bubu can receive a fragmented short message/short distinct sound. NO BODY TRANSPORT; return path untested. A phone call cannot establish someone's full coordinates. An intermittent fragment cannot be treated as authenticated without independent checks.
- **GRAY — DISCONNECTED/UNKNOWN:** no active relay reaches that world. Bubu hears NOTHING. This explicitly includes grandma and both lost brothers before any signal path is discovered. Bubu cannot locate them automatically by blood, love, thoughts, or wishing.
- **RED — TAMPERED/BLOCKED:** identity signal appears forged, or bell itself seems distressed. Bubu warns and stops forwarding sensitive mission details. Sound identity is an indicator, not legal proof; check separate return record or known private phrase. The enemy may steal codes, mimic voices, or introduce a misleading echo, requiring Orin/Mara to cross-check.

### Five mechanical limits
1. **Existing path:** Both ends need reachable genuine bell-connected resonance (a weak signal-only path is allowed, but not zero path). If Orin goes to a dead zone, he cannot call until returning to a reachable relay or a local ally's intact bell. No cross-world telepathy by blood.
2. **Short range from Bubu to listeners:** Bubu must be physically near listeners or a previously prepared, approved room receiver in the Earth workshop; do not give Evan arbitrary always-on remote communication across the planet.
3. **Conscious choice/privacy:** Bubu must answer/accept a family call; no spying on Orin's private thoughts or on unavailable family members. No secret unconditional mass-mind link.
4. **Limited duration/recovery:** continuous intense communication tires Bubu; after a long call his collar chime becomes faint and tail sparkles temporarily reduce. Tail glitter must remain visible as a fixed character attribute in approved visual master; only momentary in-story animation variation if approved by asset continuity QC, not a changed model.
5. **No gate unlock:** voice link is not a World Gate Bell, cannot grant permission, repair a destroyed gate, move matter, transmit a full visual archive, or transform all worlds to connected. A route may carry a 3-second voice fragment yet be completely unsafe for travel.

### How Orin delivers missions with Bubu
1. Orin reaches a currently signal-connected safe station during his separate repair/search expedition and rings his own personal Travel Bell in a **family-call pattern** (ordinary travel ring is a distinct one-ring destination action).
2. Bubu's collar bell jingles differently; one spiral tail pulse tells Lia someone is calling. He asks if the family wants to answer.
3. Mara authorizes the call; Orin identifies himself using his known voice and a privately agreed question that can be independently verified; don't confuse familiarity with cryptographic proof.
4. Orin gives concrete assignment: SAFE destination, person to contact, what to collect, why Orin cannot go now, known danger, tested return.
5. Juno/Bix/Mara record facts and note which claims are unverified; Evan contributes nonmagical address, letter and chronology skills AFTER his on-screen knowledge reveal.
6. On arrival, Bubu can help a time-limited progress call if relay available; family makes its own moral decisions rather than following remote orders blindly.
7. They return to Earth, discuss outcome and establish new verified intel. An unanswered call can itself be a story trigger but must not automatically imply Orin is dead.

### Story development: truly lost relatives
- Before the family discovers any accessible relay to grandma's unknown world, Bubu cannot call her; likewise each brother. There is **no universally preexisting long-range family heart-bond** overriding network collapse.
- A **genuine dramatic milestone** in a later saga: Bubu picks up a tiny unfamiliar rhythm through a newly repaired AMBER relay. It resembles an old family lullaby, but MANY people might know the tune. Mara suspects grandmother, Orin refuses to conclude until independent identity proof.
- For a brother, a short voice could be authenticated through childhood shared knowledge and an old record BUT without a safe return path the family can only reassure/contact him, not instantly fetch him. He may reply "Please help the others first." Each reunion is earned through distinct local actions and route restoration.
- Once relatives are CONTACTED and consent to a family signature pairing through a safe relay, they can participate in group calls with Orin and Bell family. Being a relative alone does NOT install a signal.

### Critical relationship differentiation
- Orin: repairs gates, leads clan, assigns mission and uses travel bell to call.
- Mara: opens approved portals, protects/decides the family's risk, confirms mission.
- Evan: ordinary human letter logistics, recorded messages, address and date checks, emotional father connection; no powers granted.
- Juno: improves small mechanical communication amplifier / simple audio recorders; these do NOT replace Bubu's spirit sensing/relaying.
- Lia: Bubu's closest friend and advocate, helps him with limits and feelings; her empathy with living willing animals is distinct.
- Bix: measures signal clarity and stores accurate time marks; cannot hear living magical nuance.
- Bubu: uniquely acts as living ring/voice relay AND listens for magical dissonance/short residual fragments, but does not know all magic or predict every true sender. He sometimes gets it wrong and admits it.

### Mini plot 'The Last Call'
Orin requests family to check a signpost in a verified sea-town relay. While they solve a local flood and help residents retrieve letters, Bubu receives an AMBER call whose family cadence is correct but wording is unfamiliar. First interpretation: the missing elder brother is alive. Evan observes that the message mentions a street renamed after the disaster; Bix detects message playback latency; together the family proves someone REPEATED an old call rather than made a live connection. The family nonetheless gains an independent archived date and the local village is safe. Bubu's concern was correct but his preferred identity conclusion wrong.
Local ending: family comes home safely with a truthful timeline clue, not an invented new portal.
