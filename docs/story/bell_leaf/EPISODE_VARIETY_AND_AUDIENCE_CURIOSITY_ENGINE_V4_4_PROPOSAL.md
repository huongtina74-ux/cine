## SHOWRUNNER EDITORIAL PROPOSAL v4.4 — 2026-11-06

# 1. Main showrunner decision
**DO NOT MAKE EVERY EPISODE INTRODUCE ONE NEW WORLD.** Make an **episodic adventure with a cumulative serial mystery**. A new viewer must be able to understand a clear episode objective, local resolution and family relationships without watching 60 prerequisites; a loyal viewer is rewarded with accumulated clues, permanent world/community developments and fair serial twists.
**The invariant owner premise:** EVERY episode must have either a fresh, genuinely surprising world/adventure/event, OR a surprising magical incursion/event at Bellfern/Earth; no pure talk/exposition-only or repeated routine for 30–50 minutes. Familiar world ≠ familiar story.

# 2. Viewers need four rewards
**A. Novelty:** world/environment/culture/creature/quest surprise; can occur in an existing world in a new district or a magical event at home.
**B. Attachment:** enduring Orin/Mara/Evan/Juno/Lia/Bix/Bubu decisions, humor, relatable family bonds, friendships with locals/recovered residents.
**C. Curiosity & suspense:** meaningful mysteries, visible Ash Bellkeeper danger, possible false signals, scattered survivors, restoration of Veylora. The villain CANNOT inexplicably appear in newly unmapped worlds; first contact Earth remains special.
**D. Closure:** one honest local goal resolved by each episode, even if the larger Veylora story stays open. Repeat "nothing solved, come back next time" is prohibited.

The writer always asks: "If viewers skip the following episode, was today's story still worth watching?" If NO, redesign payoff. "If loyal fans watch ten episodes, did facts/relationships/route states truly change?" If NO, redesign arc.

# 3. Revised episode geography vs topic (supersedes older provisional mix)
**GEOGRAPHY v4.7 owner direction:** 65% NEW worlds including revisits; 20% EARTH; 10% OLD-world Mara exceptional visit; 5% multi. ORIN normally researches OLD worlds offscreen. The family pursues Bubu's new-world echoes; the first discoveries must have planted reasons and safe return, not gratuitous random teleport. These are editorial STARTING HYPOTHESES, not validated YouTube metrics. 
**TOPIC is SEPARATE:** a new-world story can concern a missing witch, local comedy, an ecology test or a mystery; a special old-world episode can include visible Ash Bellkeeper only when his real old route permits. Themes can overlap, don't use category quotas to force travel.

# 5. 45-minute narrative rhythm — flexible, NOT a minute-by-minute quota
- **0:00–0:30:** a clear surprising visual/audio HOOK. It must match the title/thumbnail premise; a home mystery also qualifies.
- **0:30–06:00:** immediate LOCAL stakes, why our main character cares, clear simple English A2; avoid magic lecture and 5-minute recap.
- **06:00–18:00:** active attempts and world/environment discovery with one tangible surprise; a world need not be new for novelty.
- **18:00–30:00:** earned complication, mistake, direct challenge or false lead; heroine must make a decision, not receive all exposition.
- **30:00–41:00:** solve local problem with cost and local agency; show actual human/creature consequences, no instant Bubu magic shortcut.
- **41:00–45:00:** earned home/relationship consequence and one proportionate next-story curiosity tag. Some episodes finish warmly without a hard cliffhanger.
Actual timing is illustrative; approved 30–50 min episodes require proper screenplay/read-aloud QC. Each 10-second Muse shot belongs only to downstream visual planning, NOT an excuse to pad 270 shots.

# 6. Vary different mystery engines and guarantee payoffs
**World wonder** (new scenery), **person wonder** (a lost person may still be alive), **culprit identity** (who altered an old gate), **relationship dilemma** (does Mara trust Evan with dangerous truth), **science mystery** (why Earth grows new roots), **mechanics mystery** (Bubu echo is sound fragment or live signal), **villain duel**, **redemption/false suspect**.
For every question planted record: \`Q_ID\`, \`FIRST_CLUE\`, \`CREDIBLE_ALTERNATIVES\`, \`EARLIEST_PROOF\`, \`TARGET_PAYOFF_WINDOW\`, \`ACTUAL_PAYOFF\`, \`CHARACTER_COST\`, \`FUTURE_COMPLICATION\`. Do not defer EVERY mystery for hundreds of episodes.

**Information budget rhythm (editorial hypothesis):** each 3–5-episode run answers at least one meaningful small question; each 12–20 episodes makes a major route/community/family/villain discovery; each 25–40 episodes changes a lasting arc condition. New questions may emerge BECAUSE of an earned answer, rather than as random late retcon. Villain's mask does not need to be the *only* puzzle until EP600: reveal tools, accomplices, motives and past actions at intermediate milestones. Re-evaluate reveal cadence with audience signals.

**Cliffhangers:** warm closure on most regular cases, a strong unresolved tag when there is real next-step urgency, and an occasional two- or three-episode multi-part event. Don't force a hard cliffhanger every week: it trains an expectation of no payoff.

# 7. Story team and production constraints
- Fixed gateway worlds = gate bell at the station; no universal per-person travel bell. Unknown rift worlds = Bubu's ONE golden collar bell/spiral purple sparkling tail, limited safe return to actual Earth home anchor.
- Orin's known-world route and villain's movements have provable geography. If antagonist on-screen appears somewhere, show gate path, local agent or actual earlier access.
- Every episode need one local value: help a person/community, understand an ecosystem, fulfill a relationship promise, repair a genuine gate, or honest truth that changes behavior. Every adventure needs a fresh magical event or surprise.
- New World Atlas v4.3 landscapes have 5-level registry, culture and lasting consequences; don't generate hundreds of empty places merely to fill the schedule. Revisit world after consequences and time, not reset world to default.
- Each new supporting person's look/voice/environment/location requires owner approval and source refs before image/video. The script plan is not approved video readiness.
- Avoid talking about English practice in-world: story first; 45m understandable A2 sentences, no narrator and no subtitles on shots.
