# CineFactory v213 — Mandatory competitor-first niche & technology policy

**Applies to:** every niche, subniche, project pod, all 26 departments, all 96 baseline registry entries, and future technology candidates.

## A. No niche is decided from a channel title, thumbnail, or one viral video

1. **Channel evidence:** verify stable IDs, creation-age provenance (no guessed ages), date-stamped catalogue, 8+ minute long-form results, new entrants, mature leaders, fresh million-view signals, and weaker comparable control videos.
2. **Whole-video research:** inspect lawfully supplied/authorized media, visuals **and** sound; create timestamped evidence across opening, middle, climax, ending and credit/CTA. Sampling frames alone is partial evidence.
3. **Production forensics:** document characters, rigs and material consistency; shot/cut lengths; camera blocking; animation/expression; physics; environments and lighting; voice, foley, score and mix; edits, pacing and thumbnail/packaging.
4. **Separate observation from inference:** analytics from public view counts are not CTR, retention, ad RPM or verified production software. Competitor tool stacks are *hypotheses* unless disclosed or independently verified. Never copy proprietary content.
5. **Format specificity:** distinguish animated Minecraft films, Minecraft gameplay capture/live and machinima, Roblox R6, long-slices, ASMR recorded sound, rain ambience, audiobook narration, fishing field footage and other formats. Reclassify before choosing software.

## B. Select a pipeline per niche AND per difficult shot

6. Hold a **production conference** on the actual competitor evidence; list viable lanes and exact missing capabilities, not a universal model.
7. Scout **existing 96 technologies** and research discoveries from official projects, GitHub, Hugging Face, verified vendor documentation, Claude Code/Agent SDK, MCP Registry, and official software releases.
8. Treat new source references as *R&D candidates*, never automatically approved executables, model weights, licenses, or workflows.
9. Verify commercial rights including model weights/output, assets and training dependencies; respect RTX 3060 12 GB VRAM, local installation and free/paid API boundaries.
10. Compare representative difficult pilot scenes on the **same** scope with verifiable 720p+ original outputs, stability, character identity, storytelling, sound and reviewer QC; benchmark elapsed time, human rework and finished accepted seconds/hour.
11. **Quality threshold first; speed second.** An unqualified faster option cannot beat a slower qualified option. Choose a primary and tested fallback, not one model for the entire episode.
12. Keep all promotions/manual deployment behind production, legal, rights, runtime, QC and owner approval. Fail closed for unknown evidence or errors.

## C. Always-current research without silent production changes

- Existing official-source watch: `python -m cinefactory.cli technology-watch --public-sources`. Each run rotates 4 approved official Claude/MCP/AI/SaaS sources, records immutable evidence; it does not install or upgrade anything.
- Niche-aware research: `python -m cinefactory.cli technology-discover --minor-niche minecraft_story_adventure --public-sources`. GitHub/Hugging Face is bounded and outputs pending research, not a production-ready backend.
- Niche-wide strategy: `python -m cinefactory.cli niche-technology-plan --minor-niche minecraft_story_adventure`; add `--conference FILE` and `--bakeoff FILE` only when genuine matching evidence exists.
- Windows daily research already has a scheduled task for the two-track discovery workflow. Its technology discover stage is separate from the official-source watch. **The Claude/MCP watch schedule must be separately verified/authorized; do not claim it runs automatically unless its action is confirmed.**
- Tool review includes security/dependencies, licensing, factual changelogs (a page hash is not a release), local runtime, GPU benchmarks, version compatibility, quality, speed, repeatability and rollback.
- Proposed tool upgrades must pass isolated tests and bounded production pilots before any company-wide rollout.

## D. Truthful status language

`DISCOVERED` != `DOWNLOADED` != `EXECUTABLE` != `BENCHMARKED` != `QUALIFIED` != `APPROVED`.

`COMPETITOR_VIDEO_IDENTIFIED` != `THUMBNAIL_OBSERVED` != `TIMESTAMPED_SAMPLED_FRAMES` != `FULL_VIDEO_AUDIO_REVIEW` != `VERIFIED_TECHNIQUE`.

The v213 strategy report intentionally remains **RESEARCH_AND_PILOT_PLAN_ONLY** until independent competitor-review proof, quality/speed trials, rights clearance and owner approval exist. The strategy CLI cannot grant production permission.

## E. Ownership and review roles

- Research/Intelligence: verify sources, counterexamples, public metrics, full-media study progress.
- R&D/Technical Direction: watch official updates, experiment with tools, test security/compatibility.
- Director/Creative/Art/Animation/Audio/Edit: decompose actual video and define quality-bar/shot methods.
- Production: create comparable original pilot scenes and measure quality, speed, rework, VRAM and cost.
- QC/Rights/Operations: evidence chain, license checks, anomaly checks, release controls and rollback.

**Artifacts:** frozen conference, annotated competitor observations, tool strategy, pilot bakeoff, selected shot plans, production receipts and human authorization.
