## F. Character strategy

- Evidence: horror winners use the **player (first person) + a recurring original entity** (SirCloaked's "FARMER.jar" series - STORYBOARD_SAMPLED);
  entity-led search demand is visible (Verity, Jerry, Knocker) but those entities belong to others.
- Maizen-style recurring families work for Maizen and his licensed fans; JJ & Mikey are being expanded into a TV/books/toys franchise
  (theatdb, 2026-05) -> active rights holder, no public fan licence found -> **do not use**.
- **Recommendation:** pilots use **a new original horror entity** (our IP, recurring across episodes) and an unnamed/first-person player.
  Bin/Bo/Zu are **not** used in the horror pilots (cute family + horror sends mixed audience signals, MFK risk). Zu can be tested later as a
  creature cameo only if pilots succeed. Evidence for recurring original characters in Minecraft for a new channel: INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE.
