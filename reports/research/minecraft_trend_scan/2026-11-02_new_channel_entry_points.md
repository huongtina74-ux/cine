## Reading (INFERRED)
New channels get in through Shorts; the original formats that work for them are micro-horror/mystery, short skits, facts/lore and build tips. Long-form entry is rare and, when original, is scripted horror-mystery or a "full movie" story. Shorts pay little (and need 10M views/90 days for revenue share from 2027-02-01), so Shorts should feed a long-form series.
