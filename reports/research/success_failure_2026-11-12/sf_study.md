# Success vs failure of Minecraft creators by sub-niche (sf_study.py, 2026-11-12)

A channel can sit in several sub-niches. All numbers OBSERVED (one reading) unless marked INFERRED/AGG-EST. Channel age = channel creation date, which can predate the niche (repurposed channels).
