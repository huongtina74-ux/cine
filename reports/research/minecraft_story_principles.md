## Nguyên tắc dài hạn (cố định, dùng lặp lại)

### L1 — Truyện cảm xúc gia đình/em bé/nghèo–giàu/buồn là lõi của ngách
- **Evidence (OBSERVED 2026-11-03):**
  - BigSchool, the owner's reference channel: its 3 most popular videos are revenge, a baby turning into a hero, and a poor animal giving birth.
    - [Steve Becomes Herobrine Takes Revenge…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AwRScowxRo0) 7.70M
    - [Baby Steve Becomes Thor](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=twcBG93WBQE) 7.40M
    - [A Poor White Sheep Is Unable To Give Birth](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-7PVKzx41Aw) 7.30M
  - Class Monster School: [The Story of Poor Zombie Boy and Rich…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6nXcC2J8U7Q) 2.00M = 13.7× the channel median.
  - MyMy toon: [SEA LIFE WHALE HAS A BABY!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JFYYad58pGs) 2.70M = 6.7× median.
  - ZomBo: median 64K, yet its sad stories reach 11.1× and 9.8× median ([JJ threw the Cat out the door!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fjArwKrzlTM) 708K).
- **For Bin/Bo/Zu (INFERRED):** Zu is a lost baby zombie that two brothers protect, which already sits on this axis. Every episode needs a clear emotional problem (lost, hungry, abandoned, misunderstood) and a warm payoff.

### L2 — Tiêu đề = Nhân vật + biến cố cực đoan + "?!" hoặc "…"
- **Evidence (OBSERVED 2026-11-03):**
  - 8 of the 10 videos in the deep-study list follow this pattern.
  - [JJ was Buried Under The Water](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WHpNapdjfoQ) 8.20M.
  - [JJ Became Spider-Man, JJ Sister Spider-Girl?!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RjyeHZ5hSaE) 3.45M.
  - [The RISE of LORD VERITY! / … / SAD ORIGIN STORY](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mYRFII_4qSs) 2.15M.
- **For Bin/Bo/Zu (INFERRED):**
  - Names go at the front of the title: "Baby Zombie Zu…", "Bin and Bo…".
  - Use a concrete event verb: Lost, Was Buried, Became, Tries … for the First Time.
  - Close with "?!" or "…".
  - Keep the title under about 60 characters.

### L3 — Thumbnail: 2 nhân vật cận mặt, cảm xúc phóng đại, 1 vật thể/mối đe dọa trung tâm, màu bão hòa
- **Evidence (OBSERVED, thumbnail images seen 2026-11-03):** 6 of the 10 thumbnails show two characters close up with heart eyes, panic, sweat drops, or tears, plus a graphic heart or "!?". The other 4 have one large central object: the yellow monster bus ([SURVIVING in BUS…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UH1HSIN4L20) 4.77M after ~9 days), the cake shaped like a head, the pizza, and so on.
- **For Bin/Bo/Zu (INFERRED):** the thumbnail renders straight out of Mine-imator, with the camera close and the face at its most expressive. Then we add 2D effects (tears, sweat, "!?") in post. Our face rig already has eyes cry/wide/happy and the wail/grin mouths.

### L4 — Độ dài: tập 8–14 phút là lõi; bản tổng hợp 25–60 phút kéo watch time
- **Evidence (OBSERVED 2026-11-03):**
  - MSToons: 30 of 30 recent videos run 9:34–14:19, with a median of 1.60M.
  - GameToons alternates 8-minute episodes with 1-hour compilations ([VERITY: The EARLY YEARs](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhvJf7Q19mA) 60:51, 4.60M).
  - Cubeee: [ALL EPISODE…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g-GgM-kVnNs) 54:29 reached 2.06M after ~6 days, 41× its subscriber count.
  - Class Monster School: [Ninja School: Season 1 + 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nCFiA9lNYho) 45:19, 4.30M = 29.5× median.
- **For Bin/Bo/Zu (INFERRED):** produce single episodes of about 8–10 minutes. Every 4–5 episodes, release one compilation of 30–45 minutes, which adds watch time without adding much rendering.

### L5 — Series hóa video thắng: làm phần tiếp theo ngay khi một video vượt trội
- **Evidence (OBSERVED 2026-11-03):**
  - MSToons released [JJ was Buried Under The Water](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WHpNapdjfoQ) (8.20M), then [JJ BUS EATER was Buried Under The Water](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Lnl-4FT77Ws), which reached 1.28M within ~2 days, the fastest of the round at ~642K/day.
  - MyMy toon followed "The RISE of RAINBOW VERITY!" (4.10M) with "The RISE of LORD VERITY!" (2.15M).
- **For Bin/Bo/Zu (INFERRED):** when one episode reaches ≥2× the channel median in its first 7 days, the next plan should be a sequel with the same title skeleton and a new event.

### L6 — Dàn nhân vật cố định = bản sắc kênh
- **Evidence (OBSERVED 2026-11-03):** every leader on the watchlist reuses the same core cast across almost every video: JJ & Mikey at Maizen, MSToons, Mine Block and Cubeee; Steve/Alex/baby mobs at BigSchool and DL Minecraft. Examples:
  - MSToons, 30 of 30 recent titles contain JJ or Mikey: [JJ VERITY vs Mikey CURIOSITY!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W7R8k10Kjfk) 7.76M.
  - BigSchool: [Baby Steve Becomes Thor](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=twcBG93WBQE) 7.40M.
- **For Bin/Bo/Zu (INFERRED):** keep Bin, Bo and Zu in every episode, each with a recognisable look and personality. Do not change the trio's design between episodes.

### L7 — Kênh nhỏ vẫn có thể bùng nổ: YouTube phân phối theo video, không theo subs
- **Evidence (OBSERVED 2026-11-03):**
  - Cubeee: 49.7K subs, 7 of 8 videos ≥1M, peak 76× subs.
  - Mine Block: 114K subs, 10 of 17 long videos ≥1M.
  - Морфиз: 73.3K subs, 4.60M.
  - DL Minecraft: 2.58K subs, [Steve and Alex rescuing a Baby Villager… Pizza for the First Time!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tRXWDQFJ1Do) 502K = 195× subs.
- **For Bin/Bo/Zu (INFERRED):** a new channel can win with the first video if the topic, title and thumbnail are right. Put the most effort into packaging episode 1, not into "slowly building subs".
