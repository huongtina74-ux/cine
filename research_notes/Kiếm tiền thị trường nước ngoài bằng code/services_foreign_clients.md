# High-paying services a small Vietnam-based studio can sell to US/EU/AU clients (2025–2026)

Research date: 2026-11-12. Labels: **OBSERVED** = number shown on the cited page (URL given); **INFERRED** = my reasoning from observed figures; **ESTIMATED** = rough figure of my own, not from a source. Most price pages below are written by vendors who sell the service (a conflict of interest) — they show what is *asked* in the market, not what clients actually pay. Upwork/Fiverr/YouTube/Mozaik/Contra pages are primary sources for their own rules.

## Q1. Rates and demand by service niche (Upwork, Fiverr, Contra, Toptal, direct)

### Takeaway
Clients still spend a lot on visual 3D work and on AI automation, but on open marketplaces both are crowded and offshore prices sit low (about $15–35/hr). The money is in narrower, results-based packages: furniture/kitchen CGI catalogs, 3D configurators ($4k–40k per build), Amazon listing animations, FOOH/CGI ads ($2k–8k per clip), and automation work that is "reliable / tested / documented". Cabinet shop drafting is a real export market, but offshore competitors already charge about $20/hr there and Cabinet Vision/Microvellum licensing makes outsourced drafting awkward.

### Inferences
- INFERRED: On open marketplaces (Fiverr, Upwork), offshore 3D product rendering and drafting are priced around $15–$35/hr. A Vietnam studio cannot win on price alone; it has to sell **packages with fixed outcomes** (for example "10-SKU furniture catalog: model once → white-background + lifestyle + 360° + colorways") where the shared modelling and look-dev cost is spread across many outputs. freelancerates.net and Velar both describe this "model once, many outputs" economics.
- INFERRED: The best fits for this studio's mix (Blender + Python + WoodFab furniture/CNC knowledge) are (1) furniture/cabinet brand CGI catalogs and (2) WebGL configurators built from the same Blender assets (configurators cost $4k–$40k per build and many vendors sell maintenance retainers). Few competitors combine "knows how cabinets are built and nested for CNC" with "can make the browser configurator and the renders". This combination is a differentiator, not something any source measured.
- INFERRED: AI automation demand is the fastest-growing category on both platforms, but the median Upwork n8n client offers only $25/hr and profile titles are saturated. The data supports a niche pitch: "reliable/tested/documented automations", or a vertical (e.g. "automation for furniture/cabinet shops: quote → cut list → CNC").
- INFERRED: Selling faceless "YouTube automation" production is a high-volume, low-price market ($50–$300/video for most sellers). Since the July 2025 "inauthentic content" wording, templated output is a monetization risk for the client, which threatens client retention. CineFactory's research-backed, original-IP pipeline fits the $750–$3,000 "faceless explainer/documentary" tier better than the $100/video tier.
- INFERRED: FOOH/CGI ads ($2k–8k per 9-second clip, 30–60 artist-hours) is one of the best returns per hour for Blender skills. An RTX 3060 12 GB is enough for short single-shot Cycles/EEVEE product composites; long archviz flythroughs or heavy simulation would strain one GPU (not measured).
- ESTIMATED: A realistic entry price for a new Vietnam studio with no platform reviews is $20–$35/hr effective, rising to $40–$60/hr effective on packaged direct clients after a portfolio and referrals. This is my estimate from the observed bands above, not a sourced figure.

### Gaps
- No Toptal rate or acceptance data for 3D/visualization was found (Toptal does not publish freelancer rates or fees, per Second Talent).
- No source gives actual *transaction* volumes for Amazon listing video work. Only asking prices on Fiverr/vendor sites were found.
- No published per-job price for **CNC nesting-only** services (as opposed to drafting) for foreign cabinet shops.
- No Upwork category-level job counts for 3D rendering (only n8n has a job-volume study).
- Configurator price sources are almost all vendors. No independent survey was found.

## Q2. Vietnam/Asia studios with foreign clients, prices by country, platform fees, getting paid in Vietnam

### Takeaway
Vietnam-based archviz and game-art studios already sell to US/EU clients mainly as **white-label offshore capacity**, competing on GMT+7 overnight turnaround and roughly 60% lower cost. Vietnam developer rates are about $18–$68/hr (mid-level $25–$45). Fees and taxes matter: Upwork's freelancer fee is now variable (0–15%), and individual Vietnamese freelancers on Upwork also have 2% PIT plus 5% marketplace VAT withheld. Fiverr keeps 20%; Contra charges no commission. Payoneer and Wise are the main ways money comes in.

It is white-label and NDA by default, with branch addresses in Dallas and Miami — [MR Rendering](https://mrrendering.com/), [About](https://mrrendering.com/about-us)
- OBSERVED: Archvizstudio3d (Vietnam) positions itself as outsourcing support for CGI companies, developers and real estate teams worldwide, with testimonials from the USA and a Swiss CGI studio — [Archvizstudio3d](https://www.archvizstudio3d.com/)

### Inferences
- INFERRED: Effective take-home on Upwork for an *individual* Vietnamese freelancer is about 100% − ~10% service fee − 5% MFT VAT − 2% PIT − VAT on fees ≈ **82–83%** before FX/withdrawal costs (using Upwork's own example of $1,000 with a $100 fee → $60 VAT). On Fiverr it is 80% before withdrawal fees and Vietnamese tax obligations. Registering as a VAT-registered business entity removes Upwork's 2% PIT withholding but not the studio's own Vietnamese tax duties (I have not checked the tax rules for a company).
- INFERRED: Direct clients (via Contra, Wise invoices or own site) keep most margin. Contra costs the freelancer $0 commission and the client $15–$29 per payment.
- INFERRED: The proven Vietnamese playbook (MR Rendering, Archvizstudio3d) is **B2B white-label for CGI studios/architects/agencies**, using the overnight time zone as the selling point. It is less often direct-to-brand. A small studio can copy this with furniture/cabinet makers and Western CGI studios as clients.

### Gaps
- No revenue or growth figures were found for any Vietnam-based CGI studio (only self-descriptions).
- No GoodFirms/Clutch dataset limited to Vietnamese 3D-visualization firms (only game-dev bands).
- Vietnamese company-level tax/FX rules for export service revenue were not researched in depth. Ask an accountant.

## Q3. Impact of AI tools on prices (2025–2026): commoditized vs premium

### Takeaway
Simple "AI execution" work is losing value: Upwork data shows earnings for AI-based execution tasks fell 28% YoY. Complex AI-augmented work and "AI orchestrators" earn more. Freelancers using AI earn 34% more per hour. Photoreal accurate models, archviz core images and editing quality are holding their prices. Commodity clipping, template faceless videos, simple renders and single n8n workflows are being squeezed.

### Cited Findings
- OBSERVED: "Freelancers performing AI work … earn 34% more per hour"; "AI-based execution tasks saw earnings fall 28% year over year"; lower-complexity AI per-contract earnings fell 13%; AI-augmented professional services grew 72% with earnings +22%; complex AI work earnings +45% (Q1 2026); 38% of skilled workers freelance in 2026, up from 28% in 2025 — [Upwork Future Workforce Index 2026, 2026-07-14](https://www.upwork.com/research/research-future-workforce-index-2026)
- OBSERVED: "Across the industry, rendering prices are not falling… AI helps at the edges: background context, planting, people in the frame and short motion clips from finished stills" (a vendor's view) — [NoTriangle](https://notrianglestudio.com/blog/cost-of-renderings/)
- OBSERVED: An AI-render vendor lists AI architectural deliverables at $0.40–$0.76 per render, compared with studio per-image prices of $50–$1,000 for products — [Vizcraft, Aug 2026](https://vizcraft.ai/blog/posts/3d-rendering-price-list)
- OBSERVED: AI video generation costs about $0.10 per generated second (Gemini API, as cited), used for mood shots and backgrounds — [Maverick Frame](https://maverickframe.com/blog/3d-product-animation-cost)
- OBSERVED: Fiverr: AI video demand rises alongside human editing demand (video editing +36%, short-form +27%); Fiverr Pro excludes sellers who "heavily rely on templates" — [Fiverr Trends](https://www.fiverr.com/resources/guides/reports/business-trends-index-june-2026), [Fiverr Community Standards](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/32242973123985-Our-Community-Standards)
- OBSERVED: "Shorts pricing is tricky because clipping has become commoditized. If you only sell clips, you compete with cheap editors" — [Overseer OS](https://www.overseeros.com/blog/youtube-agency-pricing-guide)
- OBSERVED: AI tools make one faceless short-form video cost about $0.67–$0.71 in software credits (MakeViral's own pricing) vs $200–$1,500 per outsourced long-form video — [MakeViral, Sep 2026](https://www.makeviral.ai/blog/youtube-automation)
- OBSERVED: "Anyone can spin up a prototype now, but there's a massive gap between that and something that actually holds up" (an AI consultant on Fiverr) — [Fiverr press release](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/09/3308866/0/en/businesses-race-to-hire-claude-code-specialists-as-demand-surges-938.html)

### Inferences
- INFERRED **Commoditizing**: simple one-off product stills on a white background, basic 360° spins, template faceless videos, Shorts clipping, AI voiceover, single-workflow n8n builds, generic "AI video ads".
- INFERRED **Holding premium**: accurate CAD-faithful product/furniture models (because the image is a "promise" to the buyer), FOOH/CGI ads, research-backed explainers, configurators with business logic (pricing, quoting, CNC output), production-grade automation with tests and monitoring, and domain-specific tooling (Blender add-ons, CAD/CAM pipelines).
- INFERRED: The studio's AI pipelines (CineFactory, Afanta) are a **cost advantage** inside premium packages (e.g. hybrid 3D-asset + generative background ads). Selling "AI output" as the product would put the studio in the commoditized tier.

### Gaps
- No independent time-series of actual 3D rendering prices from 2024 to 2026 was found. "Prices not falling" comes from studios that have a stake.
- No measured data on how much AI-generated product imagery (e.g. Amazon AI image tools) has cut into the market for basic product renders.

## Q4. Recurring-revenue service models (retainers, productized services) and margins

### Takeaway
Recurring models are common and published: monthly YouTube production plans, prepaid rendering hour blocks, configurator maintenance, embedded automation specialists and dedicated drafting teams. Agency guides aim for about 50% gross margin and recommend a mix of roughly 40% productized and 40% retainer.

### Cited Findings
- OBSERVED: Recommended agency stack in 2026: "40% productized … 40% retainer"; publish prices for productized work (DesignJoy cited as an example); "Above 85% [conversion] and you're underpriced; raise the price 15%"; effective agency rate $115–$175/hr after talent cost on productized dev sprints — [Cadence blog, May 2026](https://cadence.withremote.ai/blog/agency-productized-service-pricing)
- OBSERVED: Minimum price formula = labour + contractor + tools + management + revision buffer + margin, with a 50% target margin in the worked example ($2,850 cost → $5,700 price) — [Overseer OS](https://www.overseeros.com/blog/youtube-agency-pricing-guide)
- OBSERVED: Retainer examples: MAW Motion $800/mo for 4 videos; Proffic $999–$2,999/mo for 10–30 videos; StudioFlow offers the first video free and then a retainer; Vireo $4k–$15k/mo — [MAW](https://mawmotionstudios.com/youtube-automation/), [Proffic](https://profficagencies.com/services/youtube-automation-management), [StudioFlow](https://www.studioflow.ai/guides/youtube-agency-cost), [Menno Kater](https://mennokater.nl/articles/youtube-agency-price-per-video)
- OBSERVED: Prepaid hour blocks valid for 12 months and non-refundable ($2k/$5k/$10k at $22/$18/$16 per hour) — [7CGI](https://7cgi.com/product-renderings)
- OBSERVED: Configurator vendors sell post-launch maintenance: "A configurator earns money when products, materials and prices keep moving. That's a monthly relationship" — [Configura3D](https://www.configura3d.com/); SaaS alternatives charge $547–$1,047/month — [ComeFigure](https://comefigure.com/blog/3d-product-configurator-cost.html)
- OBSERVED: Drafting firms offer "project-based … and dedicated teams (full-time remote drafters on your account)"; ArchiCGI offers per-project or a senior draftsman full-time per month — [A2Z Millwork](https://a2zmillwork.com/), [ArchiCGI](https://drawings.archicgi.com/millwork-shop-drawing-services/)
- OBSERVED: Amazon agency Flapen bundles listing videos into a flat monthly fee from $800 and notes that the margin "lives in two places: revision rounds and usage rights" — [Flapen](http://flapen.com/blog/amazon-product-video-services-and-pricing)
- OBSERVED: Velar offers an "Enterprise" tier billed monthly or quarterly with a turnaround SLA — [VelarVisuals](https://www.velarvisuals.com/pricing)

### Inferences
- INFERRED (example retainer designs for this studio):
  - (a) "Furniture brand content subscription": N new SKUs/month rendered from a shared material library, plus colorway variants and short spins.
  - (b) "Configurator + catalog upkeep": a monthly fee to add new SKUs and finishes.
  - (c) "Cabinet shop drafting desk": a monthly block of drafting hours plus DXF/nesting prep. WoodFab tooling could cut hours per job, which raises margin at a fixed price.
  - (d) "Research-backed faceless channel production" with original IP, priced per video in the $750–$3,000 tier.
- ESTIMATED: Gross margin on productized 3D catalogs could reach 50–70% once a client's material and lighting library exists, because extra SKUs reuse setup. This is not measured; it should be checked against the studio's own `data/production_ledger.jsonl`-style time logs on a pilot.

### Gaps
- No independent survey of real margins for small CGI/video retainer agencies was found (only agency pricing guides with target margins).
- No churn or retention data for YouTube-production or rendering retainers.
