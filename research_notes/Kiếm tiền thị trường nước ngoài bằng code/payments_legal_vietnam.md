## Key question 1: Which platforms accept Vietnam-based sellers/creators, and how payouts reach Vietnam

### Takeaway
Stripe (direct) and Shopify Payments do not support Vietnam, but most merchant-of-record (MoR) platforms and marketplaces do pay Vietnam residents: Paddle (software only), Polar.sh (through Stripe Connect Express, VN listed), Lemon Squeezy (bank payout to VN listed, but the product is being wound into Stripe Managed Payments, which does NOT accept VN businesses), Gumroad (VND bank payout), Etsy Payments (VND), Superhive (Stripe or PayPal), Upwork (VND local bank), Fiverr (PayPal/Payoneer), Google Play and AdSense/YouTube (USD wire to a VN bank). Lowest-friction rails for a VN individual: MoR with a local VND bank payout, or USD wire into a VN foreign-currency account.

## Key question 2: Merchant of record vs own Stripe — VAT/sales tax handling and fees

### Takeaway
An MoR (Paddle, Polar, Lemon Squeezy, Gumroad, Superhive, Fab, app stores) is the legal seller: it collects and remits EU VAT / US sales tax / GST, so a small VN seller has no foreign tax registrations. Own Stripe (only possible via a US/SG company) is cheaper per transaction but the seller becomes liable for global indirect tax.

### Cited Findings
- OBSERVED: Paddle "calculates, collects, and remits taxes" as MoR; 5% + 50¢ all-inclusive — [Paddle pricing](https://www.paddle.com/pricing); [Paddle supported countries](https://developer.paddle.com/concepts/sell/supported-countries-locales)
- OBSERVED: Polar "take[s] on the liability for international sales taxes" — [Polar](https://polar.sh/docs/merchant-of-record/supported-countries)
- OBSERVED: Lemon Squeezy is MoR and "take[s] on tax collection and calculation liability across all jurisdictions"; sticky-tape comparison shows DIY tax software +0.5% and filing $200–$5,000/month — [Lemon Squeezy pricing](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/pricing)
- OBSERVED: Gumroad MoR for all sales since 1 Jan 2025 — [Gumroad pricing](https://gumroad.com/pricing)
- OBSERVED: Superhive is MoR — [Superhive creator terms](https://support.superhivemarket.com/article/44-terms-of-use-creator)
- OBSERVED: On a $10,000/month workload (200 × $50 subscriptions, US cards), estimated fees: Creem $470, Polar Starter $600, Paddle $600, Lemon Squeezy $650, Stripe Managed Payments $700, Gumroad $1,450 — [Automated Wealth Lab, 31 Oct 2026](https://automatedwealthlab.com/merchant-of-record-fees-compared-2026)
- OBSERVED: Own-Stripe path: Stripe Tax Basic 0.5% where registered (not included in base processing); Stripe Billing 0.7% of subscription volume — [supastarter comparison citing Stripe pricing pages](https://supastarter.dev/tools/payment-fee-calculator/lemon-squeezy-vs-paddle)
- OBSERVED: Shopify's tax tools calculate/display tax but the seller remains responsible for registering, declaring and paying — [MONA Media citing Shopify Help](https://mona.media/en/shopify-pricing-2026-store-setup)

### Inferences
- INFERRED: For a small VN team with revenue < ~$40k/year, the 2–5 percentage-point MoR premium is cheaper than the compliance cost and legal exposure of being the seller of record in EU/UK/US states, and avoids needing a foreign company at all.
- INFERRED: MoR platforms also absorb chargebacks/fraud handling, which reduces one major freeze trigger (high dispute rate) for the seller's own accounts.

## Key question 3: Vietnam taxes on foreign income; FX receipt rules; invoices; US withholding and the US–Vietnam treaty

### Takeaway
From tax year 2026, VN business individuals/household businesses with annual revenue ≤ VND 1 billion pay no VAT and no PIT on business income (Decree 141/2026 amending Decree 68/2026), but must still notify revenue by 31 January of the following year and keep books; above VND 1 billion they pay VAT (direct %-of-revenue method), PIT, and must use e-invoices, with a 30% PIT reduction for 2026–2027 if revenue ≤ VND 10 billion. Foreign platforms with ordering+payment functions are supposed to withhold VN tax for VN resident sellers under Decree 117/2025; otherwise the seller self-declares. There is NO US–Vietnam tax treaty in force, so W-8BEN treaty claims are not available and US-source royalty-type income (e.g., YouTube US views) faces up to 30% US withholding.

- OBSERVED: PIT withholding for resident individuals covers transactions "phát sinh doanh thu trong và ngoài nước" (revenue arising both domestically and abroad), while VAT withholding covers revenue arising in Vietnam; foreign platforms may use their Circular 80/2021 tax ID to remit tax withheld for VN resident sellers — [Government portal](https://baochinhphu.vn/quy-dinh-quan-ly-thue-doi-voi-ho-ca-nhan-kinh-doanh-tren-nen-tang-thuong-mai-dien-tu-102250611153921023.htm); [Thư viện Pháp luật, Decree 117 text](https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Thuong-mai/Nghi-dinh-117-2025-ND-CP-quan-ly-thue-hoat-dong-kinh-doanh-tren-nen-tang-thuong-mai-dien-tu-641913.aspx). (BDO says withholding applies "only to transactions that generate revenue in Vietnam" — this appears to describe VAT/nonresidents; the decree text and government summary say resident PIT covers domestic + foreign revenue — [BDO](https://www.bdo.global/en-gb/insights/tax/indirect-tax/vietnam-new-compliance-obligations-for-e-commerce-and-digital-platform-sellers))
- OBSERVED: Decree 68/2026: resident household/individual businesses on platforms without ordering+payment functions self-declare; if revenue exceeds the threshold they aggregate all revenue for annual PIT finalization, deducting PIT already withheld by platforms; withheld tax can be refunded if total annual revenue ends up under the exemption threshold — [Decree 68/2026 English](https://english.luatvietnam.vn/decreeno68-2026-nd-cpdatedmarch052026ofthegovernmentprovidingtaxpoliciesandtaxadministrationforbusinesshouseholdsandbusinessind-427630-doc1.html); [Decree 117 refund clause](https://apolatlegal.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Decree-117-2025-ND-CP.pdf)
- OBSERVED: Royalties/franchise income of residents taxed at 5% on the part exceeding VND 20 million per contract (threshold raised from 10 million) — [KPMG](https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmgsites/vn/pdf/2026/01/new-pit-law-en.pdf); [PwC](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/vietnam/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)

**Foreign-currency receipt rules / outbound investment**
- OBSERVED: Decree 70/2014 (implementing the FX Ordinance) Art. 5: foreign-currency revenue of residents from export of goods and services or other current sources overseas must be deposited into foreign-currency accounts [at licensed credit institutions] — [Ordinance 06/2013 + Decree 70/2014 bundle](https://downloads.regulations.gov/ITA-2023-0010-0038/attachment_2.pdf) (excerpt truncated; bracketed words are my reading)

## Key question 4: Common causes of account suspensions for Vietnam sellers and how to reduce risk

### Takeaway
The documented freeze triggers are: identity/bank/tax-info mismatch (borrowed documents or accounts), failing KYC deadlines, IP-infringement reports (repeat = termination), opening a new account after a suspension (treated as circumvention and leads to all associated accounts being banned), high dispute/chargeback rates, and location mismatch at onboarding. Because the owner's Etsy shop was already suspended, opening another Etsy shop is itself a ban trigger.

### Cited Findings
- OBSERVED: Etsy terminates selling privileges for repeat IP infringement notices; if a member opens a new shop after termination, Etsy may refuse all services and act against "any accounts we believe are associated with or operated by the affected member" (policy updated 29 Oct 2026) — [Etsy IP Policy](https://www.etsy.com/legal/policy/intellectual-property-policy/1106143401341); new shops after termination → Etsy revokes all buying and selling privileges — [Etsy Seller Handbook, 5 IP questions](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/22451909389)
- OBSERVED: Etsy may hold funds 0–180 days, apply reserves up to 90 days, and keep funds for 180 days after termination to cover refunds/chargebacks — [Etsy Payments Policy](https://www.etsy.com/legal/etsy-payments/)
- OBSERVED (third-party VN seller tool blog, July 2026): Etsy bank verification must start within 30 days of first sale and complete within 60 days or the shop is suspended; Etsy re-verifies that ID, bank name and tax info match; borrowed or foreign accounts are "nearly pure downside"; shop bank country cannot be moved — [StoreFleet](https://storefleet.io.vn/blog/etsy-payments-vietnam-seller)
- OBSERVED: PayPal may hold funds up to 180 days (longer under legal process) for risk or Acceptable Use violations; holds common for new sellers, high-risk categories, high dispute rates; circumventing a suspension or accessing from a non-permitted country is prohibited — [PayPal User Agreement (US, Sep 2026)](https://www.paypal.com/us/legalhub/paypal/useragreement-full?locale.x=en_US); [PayPal User Agreement (IN)](https://www.paypal.com/in/legalhub/paypal/useragreement-full)
- OBSERVED: Superhive asks creators to ensure their IP address matches their actual location during Stripe Connect onboarding — [Superhive](https://support.superhivemarket.com/article/273-can-i-be-paid-as-a-creator-on-blender-market)
- OBSERVED: Gumroad's account review requires compliance with Gumroad's and processors' terms and no build-up of chargebacks/suspicious purchases; Stripe KYC after sales accrue — [Gumroad getting paid](https://gumroad.com/help/article/13-getting-paid)
- OBSERVED: Fab and Etsy remove content that uses others' trademarks/copyrighted names unless licensed — [Fab publisher guide](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fab/publisher-get-started-in-fab); [Etsy IP Policy](https://www.etsy.com/legal/policy/intellectual-property-policy/1106143401341)
- OBSERVED: Amazon seller suspensions for supplier documentation, brand and IP/authenticity issues are a common forum complaint (seller forum, July 2026; anecdotal) — [Amazon Seller Forums](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/ea5cb4bd-c222-4d11-afa5-33e974c6b90e?mons_sel_locale=vi_VN)
- OBSERVED: Paddle may request more information before releasing a payment from any location for risk/compliance purposes — [Paddle help](https://www.paddle.com/help/start/intro-to-paddle/which-countries-are-supported-by-paddle); community reports of terminations with retained payouts — [WannaGuide](https://wanna.guide/payments/paddle)
- OBSERVED: Mercury (popular US bank for non-resident LLCs) refuses founders living in Vietnam (residence, not citizenship); a vendor warns not to misstate your address — [Mercury prohibited countries](https://mercury.com/help/prohibited-countries); [Prolify](https://www.prolify.co/platforms/mercury)

### Inferences
- INFERRED risk-reduction checklist (from the above): (1) one real identity per platform, matching ID, bank account holder name and tax info, on a VN bank in your own name; (2) do not use VPN/VPS/residential proxies to appear foreign — onboarding location and stated residence must match; (3) do not reopen on a platform that suspended you (Etsy) without first appealing/resolving, since "associated accounts" get banned too; (4) sell only 100% original IP (consistent with the CineFactory original-IP rule) and keep source files/timestamps to answer DMCA claims and file counter-notices; (5) spread revenue over 2–3 MoR/marketplaces so one freeze doesn't stop income; (6) keep refund/dispute rates low with clear previews, licenses and fast refunds; (7) withdraw regularly rather than keeping large balances on a single platform; (8) registering a household business (hộ kinh doanh) gives a VN business document that some platforms accept as "proof of business registered there" (Gumroad lists that option). 
- INFERRED: Most VN "MMO" bans reported in communities come from rented/borrowed accounts, US-identity workarounds and trademark-infringing POD designs — these are exactly the patterns Etsy's policy names. (Community posts not individually verified.)

## Key question 5: US LLC / Singapore company options for a small Vietnam team

### Takeaway
A US LLC (≈ $400–$1,000 to form, plus yearly state fees and mandatory Form 5472/pro-forma 1120) unlocks direct Stripe, but key US banks (Mercury) refuse Vietnam residents, a missed Form 5472 costs $25,000, and Vietnamese outbound-investment and profit-repatriation rules apply. A Singapore company (≈ S$3,000–6,500 first year with a nominee director) unlocks Stripe SG, Shopify Payments SG and Stripe Managed Payments (SG is eligible), but has higher fixed costs. For revenue below ~USD 40k/year, MoR platforms paying a VN household business are cheaper and lower-risk.

### Cited Findings
- OBSERVED: Vendor price table (Apr 2026): year-1 cost USLLCGlobal $1,000; doola $297–1,999; Firstbase $399–1,298; Stripe Atlas $500 (Atlas defaults to Delaware C-Corp) — [USLLCGlobal](https://usllcglobal.com/stripe-alternative-vietnam); EIN.LLC $399 + state fee one-time — [EIN.LLC](https://ein.llc/blocked/stripe-vietnam); Wyoming state fee $100 — [RazorFile](https://razorfile.com/vietnam/stripe)
- OBSERVED: A US LLC + EIN + non-virtual US business bank account is required to open a US Stripe account; Wise/Payoneer receiving details don't qualify as that bank account (vendor claim) — [EIN.LLC](https://ein.llc/blocked/stripe-vietnam)
- OBSERVED: Foreign-owned US disregarded entities must file Form 5472 attached to a pro-forma Form 1120; penalty $25,000 for failure to file, and a substantially incomplete form counts as failure — [IRS Instructions for Form 5472](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/i5472.pdf)
- OBSERVED: FinCEN interim final rule (21 Mar 2025) and final rule (11 Aug 2026): US-formed companies, including foreign-owned US LLCs, are exempt from BOI reporting — [FinCEN release](https://www.fincen.gov/news/news-releases/fincen-removes-beneficial-ownership-reporting-requirements-us-companies-and-us); [FinCEN BOI Q&A](https://www.fincen.gov/boi/ifr-qa)
- OBSERVED: Mercury cannot open accounts for founders living in Vietnam — [Mercury](https://mercury.com/help/prohibited-countries)
- OBSERVED: Singapore: ACRA fees S$315 (S$15 name + S$300 registration); minimum share capital S$1; 100% foreign ownership allowed; at least one locally resident director required — nominee director S$3,500–5,000/year (VIVOS, Sep 2026) or ~S$2,000 (Sleek); first-year all-in S$3,000–6,500; company secretary S$300–1,500/yr; ACRA annual return S$60; annual compliance S$1,000–2,000 — [VIVOS (Sep 2026)](https://vivos.com.sg/blog/cost-to-incorporate-singapore-foreigner-2026/); [Sleek](https://sleek.com/sg/resources/cost-of-singapore-company-registration)
- OBSERVED: Singapore is a Shopify Payments country and a Stripe Managed Payments business location — [Shopify](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/payments/shopify-payments/supported-countries); [Stripe Managed Payments eligibility](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/managed-payments/eligibility)
- OBSERVED: Stripe Connect payouts require a bank account in the same country as the business in local currency (so a US LLC needs a US bank; a SG company needs an SG bank) — [Polar payout accounts](https://polar.sh/docs/features/finance/accounts)
- OBSERVED: VN outbound investment: < VND 7 billion non-conditional projects need bank FX registration only; profits must come back within 12 months; fines for unregistered outbound investment — [Tin & Partners](https://tinpartners.vn/insights/outbound-investment-law-on-investment-2025-decree-103-2026)

### Inferences
- INFERRED pros of US LLC: direct Stripe/Stripe Billing, Shopify Payments US, Stripe Managed Payments (US is presumably in the Americas list — truncated, not verified), US buyer trust. Cons: Mercury excludes VN residents (other banks unverified), $25k Form 5472 trap, US state fees/registered agent yearly, VN outbound-investment registration and VN tax on repatriated profits, and new identity surface for KYC reviews. Not worth it until revenue clearly exceeds MoR-fee savings (ESTIMATED: MoR premium ≈ 2–3% of revenue; at $40k/yr that is ≈ $800–1,200, roughly equal to LLC yearly compliance + accountant cost).
- INFERRED: Singapore is more "real" (local banking, Stripe SG, Managed Payments eligible) but fixed cost ≈ S$5,000+/year (nominee + secretary + accounting) makes it suitable only at materially higher revenue or when the team actually relocates someone.
- INFERRED: A VN option not to overlook: register a household business or a small VN company (from 2026 CIT-exempt if revenue ≤ VND 1 billion) and sell via MoR platforms; this keeps identity, bank and tax info consistent in one country — the main anti-freeze factor.
