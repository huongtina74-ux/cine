# Game/UGC economies for a small Vietnam-based studio (state as of 12 Nov 2026)

Scope: Roblox (experiences + UGC avatar items), Fortnite UEFN, Minecraft Marketplace (Bedrock) + Java mods (CurseForge/Modrinth), VRChat avatar/asset market (BOOTH, Jinxxy, Gumroad, VRChat Avatar Marketplace), Steam indie, itch.io.
Studio context (OWNER, from the task brief): Blender 3D, Python + Claude Code, Fabric/NeoForge modding skill, animation/video pipeline, one RTX 3060 PC, English-speaking buyers, original IP only.

Labels: **[OBSERVED]** = number/claim shown on the cited page; **[INFERRED]** = my reasoning from cited numbers; **[ESTIMATED]** = scenario arithmetic with stated assumptions, not a measured result. Every source is tagged primary (platform / company / SEC filing) or secondary (press, aggregator, third-party tracker). Secondary aggregators (LootTally, Roconomy, profitable.app, IslandAtlas, Gemlist, mc-mod.net) are used only where the primary page was unavailable, and are marked.

## 1. Roblox: DevEx rates and 2025-2026 changes, total payouts, earnings distribution, UGC requirements, which small studios win

### Takeaway
Roblox is the biggest pool (creators earned about $1.7B in the 12 months to June 2026) and the most top-heavy one. The 10 biggest creator accounts averaged $65.7M each, while the median DevEx participant got about $1,500 a year, and that median only counts the ~42,000 creators who passed the 30,000-earned-Robux gate. For a Vietnam studio, three 2026 changes matter. DevEx payouts have been taxed as US royalties since 1 Nov 2026, so with no US–Vietnam treaty the US-sourced share is withheld at 30%. The new US 18+ rate ($0.0054/Robux) does not apply to avatar items. And UGC selling now requires government-ID verification plus a paid subscription and an upfront Robux advance on every item.

### Cited Findings
**Totals and distribution**
- [OBSERVED] In 2025, Roblox creators earned "over $1.5 billion", up from $923M in 2024. Over 42,000 creators are in DevEx, and "the median creator receiving approximately $1,500 USD during the 12 months ended June 30, 2026". 83% of surveyed US creators operate as individuals, not studios. Primary: [Roblox Newsroom, Global Impact of Creation, 3 Sep 2026](https://about.roblox.com/en-au/newsroom/2026/09/global-impact-of-creation-on-roblox)
- [OBSERVED] At RDC 2026, Roblox said creators have earned more than $5B since DevEx launched in 2013 and about $1.7B in the past 12 months. It also announced Roblox Wallet and Roblox Card. Primary: [Roblox, RDC 2026, 11 Sep 2026](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/09/rdc-2026-the-world-needs-more-play)
- [OBSERVED] Average DevEx payout per account, July 2025–June 2026: top 10 = $65.7M, top 100 = $10.8M, top 1,000 = $1.4M. The 2024 equivalents were $33.9M / $6M / $820K, with a $1,575 median across ~24,500 participants. Secondary: [TechSpot, 15 Sep 2026](https://www.techspot.com/news/113855-roblox-top-creators-making-65-million-year-while.html); [IBTimes UK, 13 Sep 2026](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/roblox-top-creators-devex-payouts-2026-1819403). Both cite RDC 2026.
- [OBSERVED] "Our top 1,000 creators are now earning an average of $1.1 million on an LTM basis" (Q3 2025). The $1B goal for 2025 was reached in nine months. Primary: [Roblox Q3 2025 Shareholder Letter](https://s27.q4cdn.com/984876518/files/doc_financials/2025/q3/Q3-2025-Shareholder-Letter.pdf)
- [OBSERVED] Quarterly "developer exchange fees" from the shareholder letters: Q3 2025 $427.9M (+85% YoY), Q4 2025 $477M (+70%), Q1 2026 $423M (+50%), Q2 2026 $363M (+15%). Secondary compilation: [LootTally](https://loottally.com/how-much-do-roblox-developers-make). The Q2 2026 10-Q is primary: [SEC/Cloudfront PDF](https://d18rn0p25nwr6d.cloudfront.net/CIK-0001315098/a071e2ce-b8c4-4371-82df-d80d12d2f84a.pdf)
- [OBSERVED] Three 2025 games (Grow a Garden, Steal a Brainrot, 99 Nights in the Forest) each reached the top 10 within one quarter of launch, followed by "rapid normalization": Grow a Garden had dropped out of the top 10 by December 2025. Secondary analyst: [Newzoo, 4 Mar 2026](https://newzoo.com/resources/blog/bigger-faster-more-concentrated-the-top-roblox-experiences-of-2025)

**DevEx mechanics and changes**
- [OBSERVED] DevEx rates: $0.0035/Robux for Robux earned before 5 Sep 2025, $0.0038 for Robux earned since then (+8.5%), and $0.0054 for spend by age-verified US adults since 8 Jun 2026. The US 18+ rate covers game passes, developer products, subscriptions and private servers, **not avatar items**. The minimum is 30,000 earned Robux ($114 at the standard rate), the minimum age is 13, and the cap is one request per month. Secondary but cites Roblox docs: [Roconomy DevEx changes, checked Nov 2026](https://roconomy.com/guides/devex-changes). The 8.5% increase is primary: [Roblox RDC 2025](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2025/09/roblox-rdc-2025?utm)
- [OBSERVED] "DevEx payments are classified as royalties." Non-US creators with valid tax information face "0% to 30% U.S. tax withholding … on your DevEx payments related to sales to U.S. players". Without valid tax information, 24% backup withholding applies to the full payout. Payouts go through Tipalti, which charges an FX fee of 1.9–3%. Primary: [Roblox DevEx tax information](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/tax-information); [DevEx Portal](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/devex-portal)
- [OBSERVED] Roblox Wallet rolls out from December 2026 for US 18+ sole proprietors. DevEx stays available in the US until mid-2027, and no end date has been announced for other countries. Secondary: [Roconomy](https://roconomy.com/guides/devex-changes); [TechSpot](https://www.techspot.com/news/113855-roblox-top-creators-making-65-million-year-while.html)
- [OBSERVED] DevEx terms say Roblox "may not be able to approve DevEx Requests to Earning Parties in certain countries". Primary: [DevEx Terms of Use](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005718246-Developer-Exchange-Terms-of-Use)
- [OBSERVED] No US–Vietnam income tax treaty is in force. It was signed 7 Jul 2015 and ratified by Vietnam but not by the US Senate, so US-source royalties to Vietnamese residents are withheld at the statutory 30%. Secondary: [TIN Compliance, updated 28 Oct 2026](https://www.tincompliance.com/treaty-rates/vietnam); [W&A Consulting, Sep 2026](https://www.wna.global/post/us-vietnam-tax-treaty-why-there-is-still-no-treaty-in-force-and-what-us-businesses-and-individuals); treaty text listed as signed only on [US Treasury](https://home.treasury.gov/policy-issues/tax-policy/treaties)

**UGC avatar items (Marketplace)**
- [OBSERVED] Uploading requires government-ID verification (your own, or a linked parent account) plus an upload fee of 80 Robux per submission, or 500 Robux for items with an emissive mask. Fees are not refunded on rejection. Primary: [Roblox Marketplace fees and commissions](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/marketplace-fees-and-commissions)
- [OBSERVED] Refundable publishing advances for non-limited items: Hat/Face 1,500 Robux; Hair/Neck/Shoulder/Back/Waist/Front 1,000; clothing items 600; Body 2,500; Head 1,500; Emotes 1,500. Paid Limiteds cost 5,000–20,000, and emissive items cost 5× the standard advance. The advance is paid back out of Roblox's platform commission as units sell. Primary: [same page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/marketplace-fees-and-commissions)
- [OBSERVED] Commission: the creator gets 30% (Marketplace sale) or 30% plus 40% to the game owner (in-game sale). In the Marketplace a progressive share applies: 30% at the price floor, 50% at 2× the floor, 62% at 3×, and up to 70% at 6× or more. There is a 30-day escrow on each sale. Primary: [same page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/marketplace-fees-and-commissions)
- [OBSERVED] Community answer (May 2026): publishing UGC requires "ID verification, Roblox Plus subscription, and account in good standing", and "upload fees for non limited items and publishing tends to be over 1000 robux per item". Dynamic heads now need proper cages and animation ("not really beginner friendly"). Secondary forum: [Roblox DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ugc-creation-requirementstips/4645334). The Roblox Plus requirement is **not** confirmed on the fees doc fetched; that doc only names "Roblox Plus or Premium" for reselling Limiteds.

**What kind of small team wins**
- [OBSERVED] Roblox says "the majority of the top 1,000 experiences were built by some of the smallest teams in the industry (with, on average, fewer than 10 people)". Primary: [Roblox RDC 2025](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2025/09/roblox-rdc-2025?utm)
- [OBSERVED] Grow a Garden (March 2025) "began with a first version reportedly built by a 16-year-old developer in about three days" and was later scaled by a team. Secondary: [IBTimes UK](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/roblox-top-creators-devex-payouts-2026-1819403)
- [OBSERVED] June 2026 discovery changes reward long-term retention, co-play and spend, and down-rank games that displaced long-term value. RDC 2026 added age-aware ranking and optimization beyond the first 28 days. Primary: [Roblox, Optimizing Discovery, 15 Jun 2026](https://about.roblox.com/en-au/newsroom/2026/06/optimizing-discovery-great-games-reach-millions-players-roblox); [RDC 2026](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/09/rdc-2026-the-world-needs-more-play)
- [OBSERVED] Studio runs as an MCP server and client (open-sourced Studio MCP server). Primary: [RDC 2025](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2025/09/roblox-rdc-2025?utm); [Q3 2025 earnings call](https://s27.q4cdn.com/984876518/files/doc_financials/2025/q3/Q3-2025-Earnings-Conference-Call_Transcript.pdf)

### Inferences
- [INFERRED] Concentration: the top 10 at $65.7M average is ~$657M, about 39% of the ~$1.7B 12-month total. The top 1,000 at $1.4M average is ~$1.4B, roughly 80%+ of the total. The remaining ~41,000 DevEx participants share well under $0.3B, which fits a ~$1,500 median.
- [INFERRED] How many creators earn more than $10K or $1M is not disclosed as a count. Roblox publishes tier averages, not threshold counts. Since the top 1,000 average $1.4M, the number of accounts above $1M is probably in the high hundreds, but this is unverified.
- [INFERRED] Effective take for a Vietnam creator selling a UGC item in the Marketplace at the price floor: 30% creator share × $0.0038 = about $0.00114 per Robux of item price. If 100% of buyers are in the US, 30% US withholding brings that to about $0.0008, minus Tipalti fees. Since withholding applies only to the US-player share, a global audience lowers the hit.
- [INFERRED] DevEx growth slowed from +85% YoY (Q3 2025) to +15% (Q2 2026). The 2025 viral spike is fading, and new entrants compete against ranking that now rewards 28-day+ retention, which is harder for "quick viral" plays.
- [INFERRED] Roblox fits the Blender skillset (UGC accessories, layered clothing) and Claude Code (Luau via Studio MCP). UGC needs an ID-verified account holder. Roblox allows 13+ but the payer/KYC person matters, so the studio owner should be the verified account and sell through a group.

### Gaps
- No public count of creators above $10K or $1M per year (only tier averages and the median).
- Whether DevEx is approved for Vietnam-resident creators, and which Tipalti payout methods exist for Vietnam: the country list linked from the DevEx Terms was not fetched.
- The current UGC subscription requirement (Roblox Plus vs Premium) and current price floors per asset type were not confirmed on a primary page.
- No public data on median UGC creator earnings.

## 2. Fortnite UEFN: Creator Economy 2.0 payout pool, distribution among islands, entry requirements

### Takeaway
Epic puts 40% of eligible Item Shop and real-money net revenue into an engagement pool. It paid $352M in 2024, more than $722M cumulative by Sept 2025, and more than $1B cumulative by June 2026. A 2025 calendar-year total has not been published. The only official distribution (2024) shows that about 80% of paid creators earned under $1K, ~6% earned $10K or more, and 58 creators passed $1M. Since 1 Nov 2025 only playtime from paying players counts. In-island sales pay 100% of V-Bucks value until 31 Jan 2027, then 50%. Entry requires being 18+, receiving payouts through Hyperwallet, and (current docs) a qualifying Epic purchase.

### Cited Findings
- [OBSERVED] "Forty percent of the eligible net revenue from Fortnite's Item Shop and related real-money purchases is placed into an Engagement Pool". The other 60% funds Epic's ecosystem costs. Sponsored Row revenue goes into the pool at 100% through the end of 2026 and 50% after that. Payouts arrive 30 days after the month closes. Primary: [Epic docs, Engagement Payout](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/fortnite/engagement-payouts-in-fortnite-creative)
- [OBSERVED] Current eligibility: 18+, and **one of**: a real-money purchase through Epic Payments in the last 365 days (Epic Games Store / Fab / Fortnite.com / Fortnite on PC; gift card and balance purchases do not qualify), or spending and redeeming $20 in Fortnite. Primary: [Epic docs (en-us)](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/fortnite/engagement-payouts-in-fortnite-creative). The Japanese doc version also lists: account active 30 days with at least 7 days editing islands, acceptance of the Fortnite Developer Terms (individual or legal entity), and payment via Hyperwallet. Primary: [Epic docs (ja)](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/engagement-payouts-in-fortnite-creative?lang=ja)
- [OBSERVED] Payouts are issued only once the $100 minimum is reached within 12 months. Primary: [Fortnite Creator Portal news, 29 Jan 2025](https://create.fortnite.com/news/fortnite-engagement-payout-estimates-now-available)
- [OBSERVED] The formula from 1 Nov 2025 counts only players who have made purchases. It gives 75% of a new or lapsed (6 months+) player's pool contribution for 6 months to the island that acquired them, and uses island-specific retention. Components: minutes played, new and lapsed user acquisition, playtime around V-Bucks spend, island retention. Primary: [Fortnite news, 18 Sep 2025](https://www.fortnite.com/news/fortnite-developers-will-soon-be-able-to-sell-in-game-items?lang=en-US)
- [OBSERVED] In-island transactions: developers "ordinarily earn 50% of the V-Bucks value … but from January 9, 2026 through January 31, 2027, the rate will be 100%." Epic has no US tax reporting obligations for non-US developers "at this time". Primary: [Fortnite Developer Program FAQs](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/fortnite-developer-program-faqs-in-fortnite?lang=en-US)
- [OBSERVED] 2024: $352M paid to creators; 70,000 creators (24,000 in 2023); 198,000 islands published (137,000 in UEFN); 5.23B hours played = 36.5% of Fortnite playtime. Primary: [Fortnite Ecosystem 2024 Year in Review, 22 Jan 2025](https://create.fortnite.com/news/fortnite-ecosystem-2024-year-in-review-celebrating-creators-and-looking-ahead)
- [OBSERVED] 2024 payout distribution (from Epic's chart): ~30,000 creators at $100–999; ~5,400 at $1K–9,999; 1,728 at $10K–99,999; 418 at $100K–300K; 154 at $300K–1M; 37 at $1–3M; 14 at $3–10M; 7 above $10M. Secondary: [PC Gamer, 24 Jan 2025](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/fps/fortnite-has-58-creators-that-got-paid-over-usd1-million-in-2024-and-7-of-those-made-over-usd10-million/)
- [OBSERVED] "$722,000,000 paid out to date" across 260,000 live creator islands as of 18 Sep 2025. Primary: [Fortnite news](https://www.fortnite.com/news/fortnite-developers-will-soon-be-able-to-sell-in-game-items?lang=en-US)
- [OBSERVED] Over $1B paid since UEFN launched (State of Unreal, 17 Jun 2026). Creator islands took 47% of player hours in May 2026 (38% a year earlier). About 75M MAU. More than 3,000 islands earn more from in-island transactions than from engagement payouts. Secondary: [IslandAtlas citing GamesBeat/PocketGamer.biz](https://islandatlas.net/en/news/2026-06-23-uefn-epic-1-billion-creator-payouts-47-percent-playtime)
- [OBSERVED] Third-party tracker estimate: of a 284,000-map catalogue, about 250 maps project above $10K/month. Among maps above 10 CCU, more than a third project under $100/month. The rough rate used is ~$1,100 per million minutes played. Epic has "not declared a 2025 figure". Secondary, model-based: [profitable.app Fortnite stats, Nov 2026](https://profitable.app/fortnite/stats)
- [OBSERVED] Unreal MCP became available in UEFN on 20 Aug 2026, and UEFN source assets arrived on Fab in Oct 2026. Primary (headline listing): [Fortnite news](https://www.fortnite.com/news/fortnite-engagement-payout-estimates-now-available)

### Inferences
- [INFERRED] Of ~37,750 creators paid in 2024, ~79% earned under $1K, ~6.2% earned $10K or more, and 0.15% earned $1M or more. About 32,000 of the 70,000 creators were not paid at all (below the $100 threshold or not enrolled). The median paid creator is in the $100–999 bucket.
- [INFERRED] Cumulative figures imply roughly $280M+ paid between Sept 2025 and June 2026 (from $722M to more than $1B), so the pool is growing. The share that is engagement vs in-island transactions is not separable.
- [INFERRED] The paying-player-only formula and the 75% acquisition bonus favour islands with off-platform audiences (YouTube/TikTok creators bringing in new players) and retention. That fits a studio with a video pipeline that can drive direct links, but EU link attribution is excluded.
- [INFERRED] The 100% in-island transaction window ends 31 Jan 2027, so it is mostly gone for a studio starting now and shipping in 3–6 months.
- [INFERRED] For a Vietnam entity: Epic pays through Hyperwallet and states no US reporting obligation for non-US developers. Unlike Roblox and Steam, it does not document 30% royalty withholding, but this is unverified.

### Gaps
- No official 2025 calendar-year payout total or 2025 distribution chart was found (2024 is the latest official distribution).
- Hyperwallet payout support for Vietnam was not verified.
- UEFN hardware fit on an RTX 3060 (12 GB) was not checked.
- No median per island (only third-party model estimates).

## 3. Minecraft Marketplace (Bedrock) and Java mods (CurseForge / Modrinth / EULA)

### Takeaway
Selling on the Marketplace needs an application. It has historically required a registered business, a portfolio and a professional track record, applications are periodically closed, and Minecraft itself steers newcomers to three publishing partners (Pathway, Waypoint, Blocklab). Microsoft does not publish the revenue share. A "~70%" figure circulates only second-hand. Java mods cannot be sold under the EULA. Ad-revenue rewards exist (CurseForge 70/30 points pool; Modrinth 75/25 ad share) but pay roughly a few dollars per 1,000 downloads (anecdotal). They work as a portfolio and funnel, not as income.

### Cited Findings
**Marketplace**
- [OBSERVED] Partner criteria: a portfolio of "high quality, original content that you've shared with the Minecraft community" (maps, skin packs, texture packs, servers, add-ons, Realms). Partners must "operate like a business" and supply key art, screenshots, trailers and descriptions. "The best way to get your foot in the door … is through our publishers". The page also notes "even if we aren't accepting applications at this time". Publishers named: Pathway Studios, Waypoint Studios, BLOCKLAB Studios. Primary: [minecraft.net/partner](https://www.minecraft.net/partner); [es-es version](https://www.minecraft.net/es-es/partner)
- [OBSERVED] Requirement to be "a registered business, in order to sign an official contract", plus a "hidden requirement" of prior Marketplace content. Secondary: [Bedrock Universe, 26 Jul 2026](https://www.bedrockuniverse.com/en/article/sell-creations-minecraft-marketplace)
- [OBSERVED] "Microsoft publishes no revenue share. Any percentage you find is second-hand." Publisher cuts are also unpublished. Skin packs typically cost 160–310 Minecoins and add-ons 490–990. Marketplace Pass is $3.99/month, and how Pass creators are paid is not published. Secondary: [LootTally, Aug 2026](https://loottally.com/minecraft-marketplace-earnings)
- [OBSERVED] Partners "take home roughly 70% of the revenue". Marketplace "has paid out more than $500 million" since debut; "over 295 partner organisations". A Marketplace affiliate programme launched June 2026 with commission "starting at 5%". Noxcrew: 15 people, estimated 52.8M Minecoins revenue. Secondary: [CORQ, 18 Jun 2026](https://corq.studio/insights/minecraft-marketplace-inside-the-platforms-500-million-creator-economy). The $500M figure comes via an aggregator (sqmagazine), not a Microsoft page.
- [OBSERVED] "Fewer than 100 new partners are accepted … annually", with quarterly payouts and a $200 minimum. Secondary, low-quality, unsourced: [mc-mod.net](https://www.mc-mod.net/how-marketplace-creator-revenue-splits-work). Treat as unverified.

**Java mods: EULA**
- [OBSERVED] EULA: mods you create "belong to you … as long as you don't sell them for money / try to make money from them". Primary: [Minecraft EULA](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/eula)
- [OBSERVED] Usage Guidelines: you may distribute a mod if you distribute the mod only, it has no play-to-earn function, and it does not gate in-game features on out-of-game ownership. Primary: [Minecraft Usage Guidelines](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/usage-guidelines)
- [OBSERVED] "Sell Java Edition mods or plugins: Not allowed". Video paywalls are not allowed, and commissions are the legitimate earning route. Secondary reading of the EULA: [Drate, 6 Oct 2026](https://drate.app/guides/can-you-sell-minecraft-builds)

**CurseForge / Modrinth rewards**
- [OBSERVED] CurseForge: 56,208 authors have received payouts; $36.2M paid out to authors; "70% of revenue goes to creators"; 3.5B monthly downloads, 30M+ monthly users, 592K mods. Premium (paid) mods and CurseForge Pro exist. Primary (undated counter): [authors.curseforge.com](https://authors.curseforge.com/welcome)
- [OBSERVED] One point = $0.05. The points pool is a percentage of the previous month's revenue. "There is no direct 'X Downloads = Y Points' conversion rate." Primary: [CurseForge Reward Program FAQ](https://support.curseforge.com/en/support/solutions/articles/9000197902-reward-program-faq)
- [OBSERVED] Modrinth splits ad revenue 75% to creators and 25% to Modrinth, by page views plus in-app downloads, paid NET 60. Site-wide daily ad revenue examples: $10,883.95 (2 Mar 2026), $6,643.13 (7 Mar 2026); creator share $8,162.96 and $4,982.35. Primary: [Modrinth Rewards Program Info](https://staging.modrinth.com/legal/cmp-info) (staging mirror of the live page); [Modrinth, Sep 2024 split change](https://modrinth.com/news/article/becoming-sustainable)
- [OBSERVED] Anecdotes: "roughly 2$ per 1000 downloads"; "2 mods that get consistent downloads … nets me maybe 10$ a month"; a creator with millions of downloads reportedly made "a little over one thousand dollars from curseforge". Secondary, anecdotal: [r/feedthebeast](https://www.reddit.com/r/feedthebeast/comments/1mrwhi5/can_mod_development_be_a_good_source_of_income/)
- [OBSERVED] Popular mods earn "$50–$500+ monthly"; top mods earn "hundreds to low thousands per month". Secondary, not sourced to data: [Gemlist, Jul 2026](https://www.gemlist.io/blog/how-much-does-curseforge-pay-mod-creators)
- [OBSERVED] Overwolf distributed $300M to creators in 2025 across its whole network (not only Minecraft). Minecraft: 240,000 mods, 93B downloads on the Overwolf network. "Documented top modder earnings: approx. $25,000/month" (Forbes, Nov 2025). Secondary compilation: [VoxBooster, Jul 2026](https://www.voxbooster.com/blog/game-modding-statistics-2026)

### Inferences
- [INFERRED] Annualizing Modrinth's ~$5K–8K/day creator share gives about $2–3M/year shared among ~52,766 authors (author count from VoxBooster). That is a very thin pool per author.
- [INFERRED] CurseForge's lifetime $36.2M across 56,208 paid authors averages about $640 per author lifetime, and the median is far lower.
- [INFERRED] CurseForge "Premium Mods" sales of Minecraft Java mods would conflict with the EULA's no-money clause. Ad rewards are widely run by CurseForge and Modrinth without Mojang enforcement, but the EULA wording ("try to make money from them") makes even that a tolerated grey zone, not an explicit permission.
- [INFERRED] For this studio, Java modding is best used as (a) a public portfolio for a Marketplace or publisher application, (b) content for a Minecraft YouTube channel, or (c) paid commissions/contract work, not as direct income.
- [INFERRED] The realistic Marketplace route for a Vietnam studio is to pitch original Bedrock add-ons, skins or worlds to a publishing partner (revenue share or work-for-hire), after registering a Vietnamese company. Expect months of lead time before the first sale.

### Gaps
- Microsoft's actual revenue share, Marketplace Pass compensation, and publisher terms are not public.
- Whether a Vietnam-registered company can sign the Partner agreement, and whether applications are open in Nov 2026, could not be confirmed.
- No partner income distribution (median vs top) was found. The $500M lifetime figure is second-hand.

## 4. VRChat avatar/asset market (BOOTH, Jinxxy, Gumroad, VRChat Avatar Marketplace): prices and seller earnings

### Takeaway
This is the fastest-growing segment that fits the studio's skills directly, and it needs no gatekeeper. BOOTH's 3D-model category sold ¥10.4B (≈$67M) in 2025, up 79% year on year, across 7.74M orders. Avatars cluster at ¥5,000–6,999 (≈$32–45), and outfits are the largest category by listing count. Fees are low (BOOTH 5.6% + ¥45). The catch is that demand is mostly Japanese and tied to popular avatar bases. The English/Western side (Jinxxy, Gumroad, VRChat's own marketplace) is much smaller and less documented.

### Cited Findings
- [OBSERVED] BOOTH 3D-model category transaction volume: about ¥10.4B in 2025 vs about ¥5.8B in 2024 (~179%). The most orders for 3D characters fall at ¥5,000–6,999. In 2025 users spending ¥100,000+ per year outnumbered those spending ¥50,000–99,999. Primary: [pixiv inside, BOOTH 3D Model White Paper 2026, 6 Feb 2026](https://inside.pixiv.blog/2026/02/06/110000)
- [OBSERVED] Orders went from 4.02M to 7.74M and buyers from 218K to 281K. Orders per buyer rose from 18.4 to 27.4 (repeat-purchase / outfit-swapping culture). ## 5. Steam (Blender-asset indie games) and itch.io: median revenue, fees, odds

### Takeaway
Steam's median 2025 release grossed only about $249–318 (Gamalytic) on about 19.6K–20.5K releases. About 66% earned under $1,000 and about half sold fewer than 100 copies, although Valve says a record 5,863 games earned more than $100K. The fee is $100 per app, recouped after $1,000 in adjusted gross revenue. A Vietnam developer faces 30% US withholding on the US-sourced share because there is no treaty. itch.io has an open revenue share (default 10%) but no earnings distribution data, and its payment-processor exposure was shown in July 2025.

### Cited Findings
- [OBSERVED] Gamalytic 2025 (Oct 2025 snapshot): total $4.7B, mean $358,900, median $249; 66% of games under $1,000; 9.9% at $50K or more; 47.5% sold under 100 copies; 1.8% sold over 100K copies. Secondary: [80.lv, 22 Oct 2025](https://80.lv/articles/analysts-report-drop-in-game-revenue-on-steam-despite-growing-number-of-releases); [GamesRadar, 21 Oct 2025](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/over-5-000-games-released-on-steam-this-year-didnt-make-enough-money-to-recover-the-usd100-fee-to-put-a-game-on-valves-store-research-estimates)
- [OBSERVED] Gamalytic says individual estimates are within 30% error for 77% of games, and less accurate for small and free-to-play games. Secondary: [DualShockers](https://www.dualshockers.com/steam-games-gross-over-100-thousand-less-than-steam-listing-fees/)
- [OBSERVED] Full-year Gamalytic cohort of 19,606 releases: median $318, with 23.4% above $5,000. Valve (GDC, March 2026): 5,863 2025 releases earned more than $100K (vs ~3,000 in 2020). ~300 earned more than $1M (Alinea). Secondary: [16Best](https://gaming.16best.net/indie-game-statistics); [LEVVVEL](https://levvvel.com/statistics/indie-gaming); [ShaneTheGamer](https://www.shanethegamer.com/research/indie-games-statistics/)

The $100 fee is "recoupable … after a game has at least $1,000 in adjusted gross revenue". Secondary: [Steam Page Analyzer](https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/blog/how-many-games-release-on-steam); [Median Steam game 2026](https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/blog/median-steam-game-2026)

Secondary: [Game World Observer, 1 Sep 2026](https://gameworldobserver.com/2026/09/01/the-median-revenue-of-games-on-steam-has-been-declining-for-12-consecutive-years)

- [INFERRED] At a $249–318 median gross, the median 2025 Steam release nets about $175–220 after Valve's 30%, and less after 30% withholding on the US share for a Vietnam studio. - [INFERRED] itch.io is useful for jams, prototypes and Blender asset packs (game assets are a top-level category), but no evidence was found of meaningful median income.

## 6. Which option gives this studio the best expected money in 12 months?

### Takeaway
[INFERRED] Ranking by expected net cash in 12 months for a Vietnam studio with Blender, Python/Claude Code, Minecraft modding and video skills on one RTX 3060:

1. VRChat outfits and accessories on BOOTH + Jinxxy/Gumroad: fastest to first sale, no gatekeeper, low fees, a market growing 79% year on year, and a direct Blender fit.
2. Roblox UGC items plus a small retention-focused Roblox experience: the biggest pool with small-team precedent, but extremely top-heavy, with KYC/subscription/Robux advances and 30% US withholding on the US share.
3. Fortnite UEFN: the 2024 median paid creator earned under $1K, and the formula now rewards bringing paying players, which a YouTube channel could do.
4. Minecraft Marketplace via a publisher: real money but gated with long lead times. Java mods are a funnel only (EULA).
5. Steam: the median release does not recoup much within 12 months.

No source measures these returns for a studio like this one. The ranking is reasoning, not data.

### Cited Findings
- [OBSERVED] BOOTH 3D-model market ¥10.4B, +79% year on year, avatar price band ¥5,000–6,999: [pixiv inside](https://inside.pixiv.blog/2026/02/06/110000). BOOTH fee 5.6% + ¥45: [BOOTH](https://booth.pm/announcements/832)
- [OBSERVED] Roblox DevEx median ~$1,500 among ~42,000 participants: [Roblox Newsroom](https://about.roblox.com/en-au/newsroom/2026/09/global-impact-of-creation-on-roblox). UGC creator share 30–70%, ID verification, Robux advances: [Roblox docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/marketplace-fees-and-commissions)
- [OBSERVED] Fortnite 2024: ~30,000 of ~37,750 paid creators were in the $100–999 bucket: [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/fps/fortnite-has-58-creators-that-got-paid-over-usd1-million-in-2024-and-7-of-those-made-over-usd10-million/)
- [OBSERVED] Minecraft partner program gated, publisher route recommended: [minecraft.net/partner](https://www.minecraft.net/partner). Java mods cannot be sold: [EULA](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/eula)
- [OBSERVED] Steam 2025 median $249–318 gross; 30% no-treaty withholding: [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/analysts-report-drop-in-game-revenue-on-steam-despite-growing-number-of-releases); [Steamworks](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/finance/taxfaq)

### Inferences
- [ESTIMATED] VRChat assets, scenario: assume 20 outfit/accessory SKUs over 12 months at ¥1,500–2,500 each (typical outfit band; VRCFinder reports a typical outfit at ¥1,300). Assume BOOTH net ≈ 90% minus ¥45. If the median SKU sells 10–50 copies a year, revenue is about ¥270K–2.25M (≈$1.7K–14.5K at ¥155/$). These sell-through rates are assumptions, not observed data. The 300+-like threshold in the VRCFinder sample suggests "popular" items sell much more, but likes are not sales.
- [ESTIMATED] Roblox UGC, scenario: an item sold at the floor nets about $0.00114 per Robux of price before withholding. A 100-Robux item needs ~100,000 sales to net ~$11K. Each item first has to earn back its 600–1,500 Robux advance plus the 80-Robux upload fee. Outcome variance is very high and the median is likely near zero for new creators (INFERRED from the DevEx gate filtering out most monetizing creators).
- [INFERRED] Roblox and UEFN experiences are lottery-like: hits pay millions, but top-tier concentration (top 1,000 ≈ 80% of Roblox payouts) means the expected value for a first-year team is dominated by a small chance of a hit. They make sense only as a parallel bet using Claude Code and the Studio/Unreal MCP to prototype fast.
- [INFERRED] Synergy with the owner's Minecraft YouTube plans (memory: the user plans a Minecraft-niche channel, from /areas/youtube-minecraft-channel.md): a YouTube audience is the asset that improves the odds everywhere (Fortnite acquisition bonus, Roblox discovery via direct traffic, BOOTH/Jinxxy sales, Minecraft publisher pitch). Java mods support the channel without EULA risk as long as they are free.
- [INFERRED] Vietnam-specific friction applies everywhere: 30% US withholding on the US share for Roblox (DevEx since Nov 2026) and Steam; ID/KYC tied to the account owner; Marketplace contracts need a registered business. Japanese marketplaces (BOOTH) are not US-source royalty payers, so US withholding does not obviously apply there (INFERRED, not verified).

### Gaps
- No source measures sell-through for a new BOOTH or Jinxxy seller, so the VRChat scenario is assumption-driven.
- Payout rails into Vietnam (Tilia, BOOTH, Hyperwallet, Tipalti country coverage) were not verified one by one. These should be checked on each provider's country list before committing.
- No per-platform data on time to first payout for new creators. Only the gates are known: Roblox 30,000 earned Robux, Fortnite $100 within 12 months, VRChat ~$150, Modrinth NET 60.
