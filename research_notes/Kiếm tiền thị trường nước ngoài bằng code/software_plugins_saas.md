## Q1. Real revenue evidence 2024–2026 by channel (Blender, aescripts, SketchUp, Shopify, Chrome, WordPress, Figma/Framer/Notion, micro-SaaS) and the share earning ~$0

### Takeaway
Every channel follows a steep power law: platform-level money is large (Superhive >$53M paid to creators lifetime; Shopify >$1.3B paid to partners in 2025; Framer $753K paid to creators in one month), but the median product earns very little (Stripe-verified indie median ~$169/month; ~70% of micro-SaaS under $1K MRR; 44% of Gumroad products ~$0 lifetime). Niche pro-tool marketplaces (Blender, AE, SketchUp, WordPress) show the most documented "small-team, steady income" outcomes; B2C AI creator tools show the biggest but most volatile hits.

## Q2. Which categories are growing vs saturated in 2026 (AI coding making clones cheap)

### Takeaway
AI coding has flooded every open store with new products while usage stays flat, so generic categories (Notion/Framer templates, simple Chrome utilities, generic Shopify apps, mobile utilities, generic "AI wrappers") are saturated; demand-side defensibility now comes from domain depth, host-app integration, and owned distribution.

### Cited Findings
- OBSERVED: Wharton study (Sept 2026): AI raised coding productivity up to 180% but releases only ~30%; App Store, Google Play, Chrome Web Store and SourceForge show a surge of new apps since mid-2025 with **no increase in overall usage**; iOS monthly new releases ~30,000 → ~100,000 by April 2026 — [Knowledge at Wharton](https://knowledge.wharton.upenn.edu/article/ai-is-producing-more-software-why-isnt-it-being-used/).
- OBSERVED: iOS releases +80% YoY Q1 2026 (Appfigures via TechCrunch); Apple rejected >371,000 submissions for copying/spam/misleading in 2025 — [Palai](https://palai.media/en/articles/how-ai-slop-changes-mobile-apps); App Store downloads +3% (2025) and +2% (2026 YTD) despite ~560,000 new apps Jan–Jun 2026 — [TechSpot](https://www.techspot.com/news/113213-apple-app-store-inundated-low-quality-vibecoded-apps.html).
- OBSERVED: Shopify — 3,102 new apps in Oct 2026 (+39% MoM); 20.4% of live apps mention AI — [AppstorePulse](https://www.appstorepulse.com/reports/state-of-shopify-app-store-october-2026).
- OBSERVED: Framer templates — 52% of 10,156 templates published within 90 days; "visual flourishes are saturated" — [Framer marketplace report](https://marketplace2026.framer.website/).
- OBSERVED: Superhive average earnings per creator falling (Cyber 2023 $523 → Cyber 2024 $375) while total market grows — [Superhive Cyber 2024](https://superhivemarket.com/posts/blender-market-2024-cyber-sale-recap).
- OBSERVED: Stripe-verified AI products are 24.5% of TrustMRR projects with lower median ($156) than marketplaces/sales tools — [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/i-analyzed-5-079-stripe-verified-startups-f0f6bd053f).
- OBSERVED: Barn2 new WordPress plugin sales −17.8% in 2025; survey of 33 WP companies suggests SEO-led plugin businesses hit hardest — [Barn2 2025](https://barn2.com/blog/2025-year-in-review).
- OBSERVED: Generic Notion templates saturated; niche-profession templates still open (opinion piece) — [Kupkaike](https://kupkaike.com/blog/is-selling-notion-templates-still-profitable-2026).
- OBSERVED: Blender Extensions platform (official, free-only) grows the free-alternative pool: add-ons must be GPL-3.0+, no paywalls, no ads for commercial versions inside Blender UI — [Blender Extensions ToS](https://extensions.blender.org/terms-of-service) (updated Aug 2026).

### Inferences
- INFERRED growing/defensible: (1) vertical B2B tools needing domain knowledge + file-format/machine integration (cabinet/CNC, AEC, post-production pipelines); (2) host-app plugins for pro software where review gates exist (aescripts review, SketchUp EW code review, Shopify "Built for Shopify"); (3) AI tools sold through affiliate/creator distribution (Submagic, AutoShorts) — but these are hit-driven.
- INFERRED saturated: Notion/Framer templates, generic Chrome productivity, generic faceless-video SaaS, subtitle/clip tools, generic Shopify upsell/review apps, mobile utilities.
- INFERRED: AI coding lowers the seller's own build cost equally for competitors, so cost advantage from Vietnam labor rates matters less than before; domain expertise (CineFactory pipelines, WoodFab CNC) is the scarcer asset.

### Gaps
- No hard data on Blender add-on count growth per month or Chrome Web Store new-extension rate in 2026 beyond the Wharton aggregate.

## Q3. Distribution (marketplace traffic vs own audience) and pricing models / typical prices

### Takeaway
Marketplaces that sit inside the host app (Superhive, aescripts, SketchUp EW, Shopify, Figma) supply buyer traffic in exchange for 15–40% fees; Gumroad/Lemon Squeezy/Paddle/Polar are payment rails that bring almost no traffic, so they need an owned audience (YouTube, affiliates, SEO). Pro-tool plugins skew one-time ($20–$300, AOV ~$40–$100); SaaS and AI tools skew subscription ($9–$80/month) with high churn in creator segments.

### Cited Findings
- OBSERVED fees: aescripts 30% flat — [aescripts](https://aescripts.com/faq/article/view/faq/become-an-author); Sketchucation 40%/20% retained — [Sketchucation](https://new.sketchucation.com/creator-support/how-creator-earnings-are-calculated); Figma 15% — [Figma Help](https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/12067637274519-About-selling-Community-resources); Shopify 0% on first $1M + 2.9% processing — [Shopify dev](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/launch/distribution/revenue-share.txt); Framer 0% on marketplace — [Framer](https://www.framer.com/creators?md); Superhive historically creator 70% (2015) — [Year in Review](https://superhivemarket.com/posts/blender-market-year-in-review); Superhive 2024 sale data implies platform gross ≈ 14–20% of GMV (e.g., $78,880 of $562,133 Winter 2024) — INFERRED from [Winter Sale 2024](https://superhivemarket.com/posts/winter-sale-2024-recap). Gumroad 10% + $0.50 direct, 30% via Discover — cited in [InsightRaider](http://insightraider.com/en/answers/how-much-can-you-make-selling-notion-templates-on-gumroad) (secondary).
- OBSERVED: Superhive affiliates earned $25K–$31K per 5-day sale (2024) — affiliate channel is material — [Cyber 2024](https://superhivemarket.com/posts/blender-market-2024-cyber-sale-recap).
- OBSERVED: Chrome founders at $10K+ MRR rarely rely on Web Store search as primary channel (vendor article) — [crxpay blog](https://crxpay.io/blog/chrome-extensions-that-make-money-2026); Easy Folders benefited from in-store intent search — [Small Start](https://small-start.com/en/cases/global-6-months-post-launch-my-chrome-extension-has-h/).
- OBSERVED: Submagic (AI captions) — $8M ARR June 2025 with 13 people; 30% lifetime affiliate program drives ~20% of revenue; **15% monthly churn**; 2,500 new paid conversions/month — [Founderpath](https://founderpath.com/resources/david-zitoun-submagic-bootstrapped-8m-arr-affiliates); [YouTube/Nathan Latka](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lB6-TBpOuAA).
- OBSERVED: makers with 100K+ X followers report median $99K/month vs $4.7K/month for those under 5K (self-reported, n=172) — [IndieMakerList](https://indiemakerlist.com/research/indie-hacker-statistics).
- OBSERVED: MicroConf 2024: SEO and word of mouth named highest-impact marketing — [MicroConf](https://microconf.com/founders/0-10k-arr).
- OBSERVED pricing references: Blender add-ons $17–$299 (see Q1); Superhive AOV $38–$47; Barn2 WP plugin AOV ~$96 annual license; Framer templates typically $29–$149 — [Palai](https://palai.media/en/articles/yak-zaroblyayut-na-framer-shablonah-4-kejsy); faceless-video SaaS $19.9–$79.9/month — [TrustMRR Faceless Video](https://trustmrr.com/startup/faceless-video); cabinet CNC SaaS $125–$295/month (see Q4).
- ESTIMATED: B2B micro-SaaS 3.2x B2C revenue at same age; social/content tools highest churn (8.1%/month) — [Invisible Exit](https://invisibleexit.com/data/micro-saas-revenue-benchmarks-2026) (editorial estimates, labeled as such by the source).

### Inferences
- INFERRED: Best fit for a team without an audience = marketplace-first (Superhive, aescripts, SketchUp EW/Sketchucation) for discovery, then own site + MoR for higher margin once demand is proven.
- INFERRED: Creator-tool subscriptions need either affiliates or a content channel; with 8–15% monthly churn the business is a treadmill without continuous acquisition.
- INFERRED: Hybrid "one-time license + paid annual updates" (WordPress-style renewals 61–75% of revenue) is the most stable model for desktop/plugin products; Superhive is debating maintenance-style licensing (forum thread Mar 2026) — [BlenderArtists](https://blenderartists.org/t/superhive-changes/1634680?page=23).

### Gaps
- Exact current Superhive creator commission (post-2024 "pricing changes") was not found on a primary page.

## Q4. Specific opportunities matching the profile (YouTube faceless tools, Blender automation, furniture/cabinet design-to-CNC, video-editing automation)

### Takeaway
Best-fit opportunities rank: (1) SketchUp/standalone cabinet-to-CNC tooling for small shops (high price tolerance, few indie competitors, WoodFab domain fit); (2) Blender/Resolve/AE pipeline-automation add-ons sold on Superhive/aescripts (CineFactory fit, low traffic risk, modest income); (3) faceless-YouTube SaaS only as a niche, ToS-compliant workflow tool — the category has huge winners but is crowded, high-churn, and policy-exposed.

AutoShorts.ai solo founder $40K MRR in <6 months, ~75% margin, growth via Facebook ads + affiliate loop (date of figures ~2023/24) — [MRR Story](https://www.mrrstory.com/stories/bootstrapping-an-ai-faceless-video-tool-to-40k-mrr-with-75-profit-margins). ## Q5. Legal / IP and store-review rules (GPL for Blender, store policies, payments from Vietnam, past Etsy suspension)

### Takeaway
Blender add-ons must be GPL (you sell download + support, buyers may redistribute); official Blender Extensions forbids paid/locked features; SketchUp/AE/Shopify allow closed-source licensing. Merchant-of-record rails that pay out to Vietnam exist (Paddle, Lemon Squeezy, Polar, Gumroad bank payout in VND), so Stripe's absence in Vietnam is not a blocker.

### Cited Findings
- OBSERVED: Blender FAQ — selling add-ons is allowed "only if you provide the add-on and the sources to your clients under the GNU GPL" — [Blender FAQ](https://www.blender.org/support/faq); [Blender License](https://www.blender.org/about/license).
- OBSERVED: Superhive code products must be GPL or MIT; GPL lets buyers resell, but Superhive does not allow "opportunistic" resold products on its own market; "what you are selling is ... access to your product expertise, support, and updates" — [Superhive licensing options](https://support.superhivemarket.com/article/54-available-licensing-options); [GPL myths](https://support.superhivemarket.com/article/249-gpl-myths-and-misunderstandings).
- OBSERVED: Blender Extensions ToS: GPL-3.0+; assets CC0; pure Python, no pre-compiled dependencies; no payments/keys/registration blocking functionality; no ads or commercial-version promotion in Blender UI; names must not imply official status — [Blender Extensions ToS](https://extensions.blender.org/terms-of-service); [Blender Annual Report 2025](https://download.blender.org/foundation/Blender-Foundation-Annual-Report-2025.pdf) (naming guidance). Superhive also advises avoiding "Blender" in company names — [Superhive rename post](https://superhivemarket.com/posts/beyond-business-as-usual-a-new-name-for-blender-market).
- OBSERVED: Superhive requires first-product review, one product at a time for new creators, 30-day payout hold until first payout; payout via Stripe or PayPal; some countries unsupported — [Who can sell](https://support.superhivemarket.com/article/55-who-can-sell-products-on-blender-market); [How creators are paid](https://support.superhivemarket.com/article/46-how-and-when-creators-get-paid).
- OBSERVED: SketchUp EW code-reviews every listing; EW purchases non-refundable; trials supported — [SketchUp EW dev](https://developer.sketchup.com/ew); [Paid Extensions](https://help.sketchup.com/en/extension-warehouse/paid-extensions).
- OBSERVED payments to Vietnam: Paddle lists Vietnam (VN, VND) as supported — [Paddle](https://developer.paddle.com/concepts/sell/supported-countries-locales) (note: this page is a buyer-country list; seller eligibility not separately confirmed); Lemon Squeezy bank payouts list includes Vietnam, PayPal payouts 200+ countries, $50 threshold — [Lemon Squeezy](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/supported-countries); Polar (MoR) payouts via Stripe Connect Express include Vietnam, explicitly works where Stripe Payments is unavailable — [Polar](https://polar.sh/docs/merchant-of-record/supported-countries); Gumroad bank payouts in VND supported — [Gumroad Getting paid](https://gumroad.com/help/article/13-getting-paid); Figma payouts list includes Vietnam — [Figma Help](https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/12067637274519-About-selling-Community-resources).

Lemon Squeezy has a prohibited-products list (not retrieved) — [LS docs index](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/supported-countries).

- OBSERVED: Superhive may suspend accounts and revoke earnings for policy violations — [Superhive Creator Policy](https://support.superhivemarket.com/article/277-creator-seller-policy).

### Inferences
- INFERRED: Because GPL code is freely redistributable, Blender revenue depends on updates/support/brand and bundled non-code assets (which can carry a separate royalty-free license per Superhive docs). Heavy native/compiled logic or cloud services can't ship on official Blender Extensions but can on Superhive.
- INFERRED: The past Etsy suspension is a KYC/reputation consideration: MoR platforms (Paddle, Lemon Squeezy, Polar) do their own seller review; selling only original software, using accurate identity, and avoiding third-party IP/trademarks (e.g., "Blender", "YouTube", competitor names) in product names reduces suspension risk. Using two or more payout rails reduces single-platform dependency.
- INFERRED: Faceless-video tools that market "fully autopilot channels" invite both YouTube demonetization of customers (churn) and platform/payment scrutiny; positioning as an original-content production aid is safer.
