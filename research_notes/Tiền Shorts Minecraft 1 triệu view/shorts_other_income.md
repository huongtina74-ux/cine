# Non-AdSense income for a Minecraft Shorts channel (Nov 2026): what 1 million Shorts views is really worth

Research date: 2026-11-13. Public web pages only. No accounts were created, nothing was downloaded, and no paid tools were used.

**Labels used on every number**
- **OBSERVED**: read on the cited page this session.
- **INFERRED**: my reasoning from cited material.
- **ESTIMATED**: arithmetic or a modelled number, not a measurement.

**Local context reused, not redone:** `research_notes/Hai ngách Minecraft kiếm tiền nhất/policy_monetization.md` (2026-11-12). It already covers:
- the YPP 2027 thresholds and the Shorts 10M/90-day revenue rule;

- Apex Hosting affiliate (15% recurring);
- the Minecraft Usage Guidelines on sponsors;

**Source-quality warning that applies to the whole file:** almost every "rate per 1,000 views" for sponsors, TikTok, Facebook and Snapchat comes from creator-tool vendors, agencies or calculators. ## Q1. Sponsorship rates for gaming / Minecraft Shorts in 2025–2026 (per 1K views, flat fees by size, Minecraft-specific deals, minimum channel size)

### Takeaway
Sponsored Shorts are priced well below long-form integrations:
- About 0.25–0.5× of an integration fee, or 20–40% of a long-form rate.
- Gaming sits at the bottom of the niche ladder. Published gaming Short benchmarks cluster around **$3–$15 per 1,000 Short views**.
- Minecraft's young audience pushes real offers toward the low end unless the sponsor is gaming-related (hosting, mobile or PC games).

Sponsor money is lumpy and depends on inbound demand: a channel earns it only on the few Shorts it actually sells. Per 1M *total* Shorts views, a realistic range is **$0 for a new channel with no deals, up to roughly $300–$2,000 for an established channel** that sells a few sponsored Shorts (ESTIMATED, see Q7).

### Cited Findings
**Format multipliers (how Shorts are priced against long-form)**
- [OBSERVED] Guides checked by 1DS Management put "a sponsored Short at 0.25x to 0.5x" of the integration fee. Published integration CPMs mostly fall in "$10 and $50 per 1,000 views", with "gaming at the bottom". Creators Agency (May 2026, index of 200,000+ videos) lists **Gaming $4–$12 CPM** for integrations. — [1DS Management, 7 Oct 2026](https://1dsmgmt.com/blog/youtube-sponsorship-rates)
- [OBSERVED] Collab Only: "Shorts deals commonly land at 20 to 40 percent of an equivalent long-form video rate." Its CPV benchmark for mainstream consumer audiences is **$0.003–$0.01 per view ($3–$10 per 1K)**. Per-Short flat fees by subscriber tier:
  - Nano (1K–10K subs): $50–$300
  - Micro (10K–100K): $300–$1,500
  - Mid (100K–500K): $1,500–$5,000
  - Source: [Collab Only, 12 Sep 2026](https://blog.collabonly.com/blog/youtube-shorts-sponsorships)
- [OBSERVED] Aspire's 2026 survey of 1,200 creators: Shorts are priced like TikTok videos and Reels. Modash says brands mostly buy Shorts "as an add-on to a long-form deal". Influencer Marketing Hub puts Shorts from creators under 10K subscribers at about $20–$100. Kolect reports a median of **$6.85 per 1,000 views on TikTok** at 1M+ followers, against $33.06 on YouTube long-form. — [Kolect, 6 Nov 2026](https://www.kolect.ai/guides/youtube-sponsorship-cost)
- [OBSERVED] Passionfroot creator storefronts (Oct 2026): a sponsored YouTube Short has a **median listed price of $1,050**. That is a *listed* price from creators with storefronts (skewed toward established channels), not a closed-deal price. — [Mark Studios research, 19 Oct 2026](https://www.markstudios.com/research/youtube-sponsorship-rates)
- [OBSERVED] Shortstack, checked 28 Oct 2026: "There is no reliable public data for YouTube Shorts" sponsorship CPM. Modash's TikTok range is $7–$25 CPM; Reels $15–$45. — [Shortstack](https://shortstack.tools/calculator/sponsorship-rate/)

**Gaming-specific figures**
- [ESTIMATED by vendor] SponsorCraft model: a gaming sponsored Short is about **$150 on 12,000 views (≈$12.50 per 1K)**, versus about $15.60 for fitness. Shorts ad revenue is $0.03–$0.10 per 1K (vidIQ, May 2026). — [Variant/SponsorCraft, 14 Jul 2026](https://variant-intl.com/blog/youtube-shorts-cpm-rates.html)
- [OBSERVED, aggregated third-party] Bounty Board's indie-game creator marketplace (checked Sep 2026):
  - Flat fee for a Short: **$10 / $25 / $75** for small / mid (5K–50K) / established creators.
  - Pay-per-view programs on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok: **$3 per 1,000 views, capped at $40 per post**.
  - Gaming Reels/TikTok sponsorship "premium shrinks to $11 to $18 CPM".
  - Source: [Bounty Board, 27 Oct 2026](https://www.bountyboard.gg/gaming-influencer-rates)
- [OBSERVED, vendor] Snippet gaming rate table (June 2026), YouTube "Story / Short" by tier: $40–$100 (nano), $100–$350 (micro), $350–$2,000 (mid 50K–500K), $2,000–$7,000 (macro). "Avg pay per 1K views: $8–$20". — [Snippet](https://usesnippet.app/tools/influencer-rates/gaming)
- [OBSERVED, vendor] SponsorRadar gaming benchmark (165,098 sponsored gaming videos, reviewed Jul 2026): **$10–$25 per 1,000 average views** for long-form integrations, against $2–$5 AdSense RPM. — [Icon Era citing SponsorRadar, 22 Sep 2026](https://icon-era.com/statistics/gaming-youtube-channel-earnings/)
- [OBSERVED, vendor] Why gaming pays less: "The gaming audience skews younger (13-24 is the dominant demographic)… lower average purchasing power", and "A Minecraft creator is one of tens of thousands of Minecraft creators", which weakens negotiating leverage. — [InfluencerFee, 21 May 2026](https://influencerfee.com/blog/gaming-influencer-pricing)

**Minecraft-specific deals**
- [OBSERVED, anecdote, via local note] A 250K-subscriber Minecraft channel (500K–700K views per video) was told $8.5K–$10K, or about $5K for a 250K-view benchmark. A commenter added the young audience lowers CPM "unless the promotion is for something related to Minecraft/gaming". Another commenter suggested adding Shorts for about $1,000 extra. — [r/PartneredYoutube](https://www.reddit.com/r/PartneredYoutube/comments/1pjy8qe/whats_a_fair_sponsorship_rate_for_my_channel)
- [ESTIMATED by vendor] SponsorRadar detects Minecraft-war videos on Plastic Scot sponsored by Wepwawet (a game), "Minecraft Server Hosting" and Instant Gaming. Example: a 108K-view video with a model value of $1.1K–$2.7K. These are model values, not disclosed fees. They show what kind of sponsors pay for Minecraft content: games, server hosts and key resellers. — [SponsorRadar Plastic Scot](https://sponsorradar.com/channels/plasticscot)
- [OBSERVED] **Server hosts recruit Shorts creators.** Shockbyte's partner page asks "do you create long form content, or are shorts more your thing?" Its partners include Minecraft creators PhoenixSC and ucq. — [Shockbyte Partners](https://shockbyte.com/partners)
- [OBSERVED] **BisectHosting partner minimums for YouTube:** 50,000+ subscribers, 75,000+ views in the last 30 days, regular uploads over the last 3 months, relevant audience. Smaller creators can still use the self-serve affiliate link (Q2). — [BisectHosting partnerships (zh-cn page)](https://www.bisecthosting.com/zh-cn/partnerships)
- [OBSERVED] **Mojang sponsors Minecraft streams directly on Twitch.** The "Tiny Takeover" campaign (Apr 2026) paid Affiliates and Partners "up to $1,000" for streaming at least 1 hour of Minecraft. This is Twitch-only and first-come, first-served; it is not a YouTube Shorts deal. — [Twitch blog, 6 Apr 2026](https://blog.twitch.tv/en/2026/04/06/minecrafts-tiny-takeover)

**Minimum channel size**
- [OBSERVED] "There is no subscriber minimum for deals made directly with brands." YouTube's Creator Partnerships tab requires the fan-funding/Shopping YPP tier. — [Mark Studios](https://www.markstudios.com/research/youtube-sponsorship-rates)
- [OBSERVED] Creator Partnerships uses the same YPP tier as Shopping and fan funding: 500 subscribers plus 3,000 qualified watch hours in 365 days, or 3M qualified Shorts views in 90 days. This is unchanged in 2027. — [YouTube Help 12843009](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12843009?hl=en)
- [OBSERVED] Mobile-game sponsors such as Raid: Shadow Legends: this session found **no published 2025–2026 rate card or creator disclosure** for Minecraft Shorts specifically.

### Inferences
- **Working CPM band for a Minecraft Short (INFERRED):** **$2–$10 per 1,000 views of the sponsored Short.**
  - The top of the gaming Short benchmarks ($12.50 SponsorCraft, $8–$20 Snippet) assumes a mixed-age audience.
  - The Reddit Minecraft advice ($5K per 250K views = $20 CPM, for *long-form*) times the 0.25–0.5× Short multiplier gives about $5–$10.
  - Pay-per-view indie-game programs pay $3 per 1K.
  - Kid skew and many substitute creators pull the number down.
- **Sponsor money scales with deals, not with views (INFERRED).** Hosting and game sponsors buy specific Shorts, usually bundled with long-form. A Shorts-only channel with no long-form, under about 50K subscribers, will mostly see self-serve affiliate links, pay-per-view marketplaces and occasional $25–$300 flat fees.
- **YouTube's "production credits for brand deals"** (Q3) could subsidise early deals for channels below 10M Shorts views per 90 days. No amounts are published yet.

### Gaps
- No 2025–2026 public rate card from Raid, Royal Match, other mobile games, or from Apex/Shockbyte/Bisect for flat-fee Shorts integrations.
- No dataset of *closed* deal prices for Minecraft Shorts specifically. All Short CPMs above are vendor models or listings.
- The Passionfroot "median per-1K for a Short" derived value (DRV-014) was not visible in the excerpt.

## Q2. Affiliate economics for a Minecraft audience (Minecraft 5%, hosting programs, Marketplace, YouTube Shopping) and age limits

### Takeaway
Affiliate rates look generous on paper:
- Minecraft 5% on game and Marketplace sales
- Apex 15% recurring
- Bisect 10% recurring
- Shockbyte 70% of the first month
- YouTube Shopping median commission about 15%

The order value per click is small (a $30 game, a $5–$25/month server). The viewers who can pay are a minority: about 20% of Minecraft players are under 15 and 43% are 15–21. Clickable paths from a Short are also limited.

**Per 1M Minecraft Shorts views, affiliate income is realistically tens of dollars, not hundreds (ESTIMATED: $5–$100).** The exception is a hosting Short that targets "how to make a server" viewers, who are older and have buying intent.

### Cited Findings
**Minecraft and hosting programs**
- [OBSERVED] Minecraft Affiliate Program (impact.com):
  - Commission on Marketplace offers sold on web, Java, Bedrock, Deluxe/Ultimate collections and Dungeons, for purchases on or after 29 Aug 2026.
  - "Subscription earnings do not count."
  - "Affiliate eligibility requires age 18+."
  - Affiliates must follow brand guidelines and disclosure rules.
  - Source: [minecraft.net/affiliates](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/affiliates)
  - The 5% rate comes from the local note citing [impact.com PR, 1 Jun 2026](https://impact.com/press-releases/impact-minecraft-affiliate-program/).
  - Affiliate purchases stay refundable for 30 days before they lock. — [Xtendedview, 14 Mar 2026](https://xtendedview.com/minecraft-statistics)
- [OBSERVED] **Apex Hosting:** "recurring 15% commission on every sale", paid monthly. — [Apex partnerships](https://apexminecrafthosting.com/partnerships)
- [OBSERVED] **BisectHosting:** "A 10% commission… This commission structure remains applicable for all forthcoming and recurring purchases". Worked example: a $22.48 server earns **$2.48 in account credit**. The help page describes payout as *account credit*, usable for servers. — [Bisect help, 1 Mar 2026](https://help.bisecthosting.com/hc/en-us/articles/40028852241435-How-to-Join-the-BisectHosting-Affiliate-Program)
- [OBSERVED] **Shockbyte:**
  - "70% of every sale (first month of service)" within a 3-month window after the click.
  - $10 signup bonus; $25 minimum payout.
  - 10% fee on PayPal or bank withdrawals.
  - Commissions are held 35 days and voided on refund.
  - On annual plans, commission covers only the first month's value.
  - Sources: [Shockbyte affiliate KB, Oct 2024](https://shockbyte.com/help/knowledgebase/articles/shockbyte-affiliate-program-earn-real-money); [Shockbyte ToS](https://shockbyte.com/legal/terms-of-service)
- [OBSERVED] A hosting-review site lists its active terms as:
  - Bisect: 10% recurring revshare
  - Apex: hybrid, revshare plus per-signup
  - Shockbyte: CPA, 70% of first payment, 90-day cookie
  - ScalaCube: 50% first or 30% recurring
  - Source: [SyntaxMine disclosure, 2 Jun 2026](https://syntaxmine.com/affiliate-disclosure)
- [OBSERVED] Shockbyte ToS: users aged 13–17 need a parent's or guardian's permission to buy. — [Shockbyte ToS](https://shockbyte.com/legal/terms-of-service)

**YouTube Shopping affiliate**
- [OBSERVED] Requirements and rules:
  - The channel must be in YPP, at the subscriber threshold, and not a music/OAC channel.
  - Commissions are paid via AdSense 60–120 days after purchase.
  - A temporary launch incentive gives higher rates in new markets.
  - Source: [YouTube Help 13376398](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13376398?co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid&hl=en)
- [OBSERVED] "Median commission rates in the YouTube Shopping affiliate program were around 15%, whereas the bottom 25% of commission rates were at 10% or less." — [Google Merchant Center Help 13773426](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/13773426?hl=en)
- [OBSERVED] Shopping is unavailable when the channel "is set as Made for Kids" or has a significant number of MFK videos. — [YouTube Help 72857](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72857?hl=en)
- [OBSERVED] **Oct 2026:** YouTube began testing **affiliate bonus campaigns**, "exclusive offers" with "bonus amounts, commission rates, and active dates" shown in the Shopping Hub. — [Social Media Today, 19 Oct 2026](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/youtube-tests-new-shorts-incentives/833420/)

**Marketplace**
- [OBSERVED, via local note] Selling on Marketplace is a separate partner business (registered business, 18+, portfolio, usually through a publisher). It is not a Shorts monetization lever. — local `policy_monetization.md` citing [minecraft.net/partner](https://www.minecraft.net/partner)

**Audience age**
- [OBSERVED, third-party estimate] Minecraft player age split: <15: 20.59%; 15–21: 43%; 22–30: 21%; 30–65: 15%. Average age 24. — [DemandSage, 3 Oct 2026](https://www.demandsage.com/minecraft-statistics)
- These are *player* demographics, not Shorts-viewer demographics, and the method is not stated.
- [OBSERVED] Common Sense Media 2026: "many children under 13 are using AI, short-form video, and social media", and short-form video is "one of the biggest parts of kids' media diets". — [Common Sense Census 2026 (PDF)](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/sites/default/files/research/report/commonsensemedia_techmediausetweensteens_report_2026.pdf)

### Inferences
**Affiliate model per 1M Shorts views (ESTIMATED; every input is an assumption)**
- **Link clicks.** Viewers reach a link from a Short through the related-video link, a pinned comment or the channel page. Assume 0.05–0.2% of views click: **500–2,000 clicks**.
- **Minecraft game link, 5%.** Conversion 0.5–2% (many viewers already own the game or are minors) gives 3–40 sales. At about $30 × 5% = $1.50 each, that is **$5–$60**.
- **Hosting link.**
  - With a generic Minecraft Short, conversion is about 0.1–0.5%: 1–10 orders at about $2–$15 each, so **$2–$150**.
  - Recurring 10–15% adds a tail of a few dollars a month.
  - Shockbyte's 70% one-off can pay more per order (about $5–$15).
  - With a dedicated "free server vs paid server" Short, which is older and intent-driven, **$100–$500** is plausible for that one video.
- **YouTube Shopping** (Minecraft LEGO sets, plush, merch, *if* tagged items carry commissions): 15% median rate, but a kid-heavy audience gives **$0–$200**. No Minecraft-specific Shopping data was found.
- **Constraints:**
  - The 18+ creator rule (Minecraft affiliate) and the 13–17 parental-consent rule (Shockbyte) limit both creator and buyer.
  - Under-13 viewers cannot legitimately buy.
  - Any MFK designation kills Shopping entirely.

### Gaps
- No measured CTR or conversion data for Minecraft-audience affiliate links on Shorts.
- Apex's current per-signup amount and average order value were not published on the fetched page.
- This session did not verify whether YouTube currently makes links in Shorts descriptions clickable for all channels. The help center lists a "Brand Partnerships: External Links on Shorts" article, which was not fetched.
- No data on which Minecraft-licensed products, if any, are in YouTube Shopping's affiliate catalogue.

## Q3. YouTube on-platform income besides Shorts AdSense: the 2026–2027 bonus programs, Shopping, Super Thanks, memberships, targeted Shorts ads

### Takeaway
Shorts AdSense disappears for channels under **10M qualified Shorts views per 90 days** from 1 Feb 2027, so a 1M-views-a-month Minecraft channel gets **$0 from the pool**.

YouTube's replacement is a set of incentive programs. The first one went live on **15 Oct 2026**: an **invite-only Shorts bonus** in 6 pilot markets (US, UK, DE, BR, JP, KR). It pays a guaranteed monthly minimum for posting N public Shorts (e.g. 12 or 16 a month), plus a view-based extra.
- Reported offers: about ¥150,000 (≈$1,000) for 12 Shorts/month, up to ¥300,000 for 16 Shorts/month, over Nov–Jan.
- Another report: "$300 base, up to $2,000 monthly".
- These figures are unconfirmed by YouTube.

Shopping bonuses and brand-deal production credits are announced but have no published amounts. Super Thanks, memberships and Shopping need the 500-subscriber tier and are **off for Made-for-Kids content**.

### Cited Findings
**Shorts revenue rules from 2027**
- [OBSERVED] From 1 Feb 2027, earning from the Shorts Creator Pool needs "10M qualified Shorts views over the last 90 days". Channels below the threshold "remain in YPP and continue earning on long-form content". — [YouTube Blog, 10 Aug 2026](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-partner-program-updates-2027-new-opportunities-earn/); [YouTube Help 12843009](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12843009?hl=en)
- [OBSERVED] "New Shorts Incentive Programs": "bonuses for YouTube Shopping, production credits for brand deals, and earnings boosts for cultural trend activations. Eligible creators will get direct notifications on how to opt-in". — [YouTube Help 12843009](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12843009?hl=en)
- [OBSERVED] **Targeted Shorts ads:** "If an advertiser targets an ad to a group of five or fewer channels, eligible creators can earn a direct 45% revenue share", paid on top of the pool. — [YouTube Help 12843009](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12843009?hl=en)
  - [INFERRED] Whether this applies to channels below 10M is not stated; the text says "eligible creators".

**The Oct 2026 Shorts bonus pilot**
- [OBSERVED] TeamYouTube update, 15 Oct 2026: "a new bonus program designed to give Shorts creators across YPP — including those who don't qualify for ads revenue sharing — more ways to earn, starting with… a guaranteed monthly bonus for maintaining consistent Shorts uploads, with additional performance-based earnings as their views grow. Rollouts begin today for an initial group of creators across 6 pilot markets (US, UK, Germany, Brazil, Japan, and South Korea)… specific goals, bonus amounts, and durations will vary." — [TeamYouTube community post (updated 15 Oct 2026)](https://support.google.com/youtube/blog/451804019/updates-to-the-youtube-partner-program-new-earning-opportunities-changes-to-eligibility?hl=en-AU)
- [OBSERVED] Official program page:
  - Invite-only "for select creators in specific regions".
  - The extra bonus is "calculated based on your total valid engaged Shorts views for that month and be based on the typical earnings in your country".
  - You must "continue to be monetizing at the end of that same month".
  - Source: [YouTube Help 18336041](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/18336041?hl=en)
- [OBSERVED, secondary] Japanese creators shared offers of "12 Shorts a month for up to ¥150,000 and 16 Shorts for up to ¥300,000, over a three-month window from November to January (reported, not confirmed by YouTube)". The extra bonus "isn't guaranteed". — [How Creators Get Paid, 16 Oct 2026](https://howcreatorsgetpaid.com/news/youtube-shorts-bonus-program-invites)
- [OBSERVED, low reliability] An X/Grok trend summary: "12 public original Shorts each month… Some offers promise up to $2,000 monthly, including a $300 base, but missing even one Short means no payout that month." — [X trending summary, 16 Oct 2026](https://x.com/i/trending/2110873549212967375)
- [OBSERVED] YouTube is also testing affiliate bonus campaigns in Shopping. — [Social Media Today, 19 Oct 2026](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/youtube-tests-new-shorts-incentives/833420/); [Music Ally, 26 Oct 2026](https://musically.com/2026/10/26/youtube-is-testing-out-bonus-payments-for-regular-shorts-posts)

**Fan funding (Super Thanks, memberships)**
- [OBSERVED] Super Thanks works on "Shorts and long-form videos". Conditions:
  - The fan-funding tier is required.
  - The channel must not be MFK and must not have a significant number of MFK videos.
  - It is unavailable on MFK, age-restricted or copyright-claimed Shorts, and on Shorts with comments off.
  - Source: [YouTube Help 10879035](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10879035)
- [OBSERVED] Super Thanks and Super Chat require the creator to be "at least 18 years old". — [YouTube Help 72857](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72857?hl=en)
- [OBSERVED] Fan funding (memberships, Super Chat, Super Stickers, Super Thanks) pays the creator 70% of net revenue. — [Icon Era citing YouTube Help, 22 Sep 2026](https://icon-era.com/statistics/gaming-youtube-channel-earnings/)
- [OBSERVED] Tier for fan funding, Creator Partnerships and Shopping: 500 subscribers plus 3,000 hours or 3M Shorts views in 90 days. Unchanged in 2027. — [YouTube Help 12843009](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12843009?hl=en)

**Shorts AdSense baseline (context only)**
- [OBSERVED] AIR (274 channels): Shorts RPM is $0.07–$0.20 per 1K in most niches, about $0.33 for US-heavy audiences. "1 million Shorts views from a U.S.-heavy audience earns about $300." Gaming example: a Shorts RPM of $0.17. — [AIR, 16 Sep 2026](https://air.io/en/air-data-findings/youtube-shorts-rpm-vs-long-form-how-much-do-shorts-earn-in-2026)

### Inferences
- **Bonus pilot value (ESTIMATED).** If invited, the guaranteed base is reportedly a few hundred to about $1,000–$2,000 a month for 12–16 Shorts.
  - That is *per month*, not per 1M views. For a channel doing about 1M views a month it could be worth more than all other Shorts income combined.
  - Eligibility is by invite, YPP membership and creator region.
  - A channel owner based outside the 6 pilot markets (for example in Vietnam, where this project's research folders suggest the team works) **cannot count on it today**.
  - Model it as $0 until an invite arrives.
- **Super Thanks on Minecraft Shorts (ESTIMATED): $0–$30 per 1M views.**
  - Kid-heavy audiences cannot pay, and Shorts viewers rarely tip.
  - No public dataset of Super Thanks per view was found.
- **Memberships (INFERRED):** driven by long-form and live loyalty, not Shorts. About $0 per 1M Shorts views unless the channel has a long-form or live layer.
- **"Trend boosts" (INFERRED):** may reward jumping on Minecraft trends (updates, the movie, Minecraft LIVE) but are unquantified.

### Gaps
- Official bonus amounts, invite criteria and the full list of eligible countries for the bonus pilot are not published.
- No launch date or amounts yet for "production credits for brand deals" or Shopping bonuses for creators under 10M.
- No Super Thanks or membership revenue data per Shorts view.

## Q4. Cross-platform reposting: TikTok Creator Rewards, Facebook Reels, Snapchat Spotlight, Instagram (rates, eligibility, cross-post policy)

### Takeaway
Reposting the *same original* Minecraft video is allowed in principle on every platform: originality rules target other people's content and watermarks, not your own cross-posts.

TikTok pays the most per view but only on videos **≥1 minute**, from creators **18+ with 10K followers and 100K views in 30 days**, in an **eligible country**. Reported RPM is **$0.40–$1.00 per 1K qualified views**; gaming is at the low end.

Facebook (invite-only, cents per 1K on Reels) and Snapchat (1-minute Spotlight, 100 hours of view time) add small amounts. Instagram pays $0 per view.

**Per 1M views on each extra platform (ESTIMATED):**
- TikTok: about $150–$600 if eligible.
- Facebook: about $20–$200.
- Snapchat: unknown/low.
- Instagram: $0.

Sponsored videos are excluded from TikTok rewards.

### Cited Findings
**TikTok Creator Rewards Program (CRP)**
- [OBSERVED] Eligibility:
  - "Be at least 18 years old… Have at least 10,000 followers… at least 100,000 video views in the last 30 days… Post original content… Post videos that are at least one minute long."
  - Not original: "Content reproduced from others with only slight modifications", Duets/Stitches, others' watermarks.
  - Videos start earning after 1,000 qualified views.
  - Qualified views are unique FYP views, excluding views under 5 s and paid or promoted views.
  - Source: [TikTok Support (es mirror, English text)](https://support.tiktok.com/es/business-and-creator/creator-rewards-program/creator-rewards-program)
- [OBSERVED] Only personal accounts; accounts must be in a country where the program is available. — [TikTok Creator Academy, updated 10 Sep 2026](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/creator-rewards-program); [TikTok FAQ](https://www.tiktok.com/support/faq_detail?id=7581821550694013452&category=web_account)
- [OBSERVED] RPM factors: watch time and finish rate, search value, **location**, engagement, and advertising value. — [TikTok Support: how rewards work](https://support.tiktok.com/en/business-and-creator/creator-rewards-program/how-rewards-work)

- **TikTok per 1M views (ESTIMATED):** assume 60–80% of views qualify (FYP, >5 s), at a gaming RPM of $0.25–$0.80, giving **≈$150–$640**.

  - A Vietnam-registered account would not qualify, because Vietnam is not in the listed CRP countries.
- **Facebook per 1M Reels views (ESTIMATED): $20–$200** once invited. Fast Track ($1K/month × 3) is the bigger prize, but it needs 100K followers elsewhere.
- **Instagram per 1M (ESTIMATED): $0** from views. Value comes only from brand-deal bundles.
- **Snapchat (ESTIMATED):** unknown; likely tens of dollars per 1M. Treat as $0 for planning.
- **Platform originality checks (INFERRED):** reposting your own original video is fine, but upload a clean master each time. A TikTok watermark on YouTube or Facebook, or a CapCut end card, triggers "recycled" signals.

## Q5. Shorts-to-long-form funnel value (subscriber conversion, long-form views, case studies)

### Takeaway
For **gaming specifically**, measured data says Shorts are a weak funnel:
- In AIR's 18,000-channel study, gaming channels posting long-form only had **+85% more subscribers** than mixed channels.
- Gaming Shorts reach 3.4% of subscribers per upload, versus 4.4% for long-form.
- Modelled conversion is about 0.1–0.5% of Shorts views becoming subscribers, and only about 5% of those watch the next long video.

A long-form view is worth about $2–$3.4 per 1K in gaming, against about $0.17 per 1K for a Short. The funnel value of 1M Shorts views is therefore **tens to low hundreds of dollars in long-form AdSense (ESTIMATED $10–$300)**, unless the channel deliberately bridges Shorts to series content. It is bigger only if long-form sponsors are sold on the larger subscriber base.

### Cited Findings
- [OBSERVED] AIR, 18,000 English channels, Mar 2025–Mar 2026: "gaming (+85% more subscribers with long-form only)". — [AIR, 28 May 2026](https://air.io/en/audience-growth/do-youtube-shorts-help-your-long-form-videos-grow-data-from-18000-channels)
- [OBSERVED] AIR, 274 channels:
  - Gaming RPM "barely moved" when Shorts were added ($3.42 without vs $3.29 with), unlike entertainment ($4.58 → $1.84).
  - "1 million Shorts views from a U.S.-heavy audience earns about $300, while a million long-form views pays about $2,300 at the median."
  - "The economic value of Shorts lies in that conversion."
  - Sources: [AIR, 23 Oct 2026](https://air.io/en/youtube-hacks/should-you-chase-shorts-views-or-double-down-on-long-form-for-channel-growth); [AIR, 16 Sep 2026](https://air.io/en/air-data-findings/youtube-shorts-rpm-vs-long-form-how-much-do-shorts-earn-in-2026)
- [OBSERVED] Long-form performance drops when Shorts exceed 55% of uploads. The best mix is 25–40% Shorts (about 1 Short per 2–3 long videos). — [Revid stats page citing AIR, 3 Oct 2026](https://www.revid.ai/youtube-shorts-statistics); [AIR 1M views page](https://air.io/en/monetization/how-much-does-youtube-pay-for-1-million-views)
- [OBSERVED] 312 big channels: median views per subscriber per upload, **gaming 4.4% long-form vs 3.4% Shorts**. Gaming Shorts had the highest like rate (3.51%). — [SoInfluential, 28 Oct 2026](https://soinfluential.com/blog/youtube-shorts-vs-long-form-views-2026)
- [OBSERVED, other niche] Indian healthcare, 2,431 Shorts vs 5,987 long-form: **2.75 subscribers per 1,000 Shorts views vs 27.3 for long-form** (9.9×); 30-day return-viewer rate 11% vs 34%. — [ICG, 9 Sep 2026](https://ichelonconsulting.com/reports/youtube-shorts-vs-long-form-healthcare-india-study-2026)
- [ESTIMATED by author] A worked model: "One million views at 0.5% is 5,000 new subscribers… 5% watch the next long video, which is 250 people", or about 22.5 watch hours. Shorts views do not count toward watch hours. — [FalconVid, 4 Aug 2026](https://falconvid.ai/blog/do-shorts-bring-subscribers-to-long-videos)
- [OBSERVED, anecdote, self-promotional] One creator claims Shorts converted at 2.1% and Shorts subscribers watched long-form 2.3× more. This is unverified marketing content and an outlier. — [HookPilot, Apr 2026](https://hookpilot.co/blog/youtube-shorts-strategy.html)
- [OBSERVED, academic, pre-2024 data] 250 prominent creators: average long-form views per channel *decreased* after introducing Shorts; the effect was significant for 5M+ subscriber channels. — [arXiv 2402.18208](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.18208v2)

### Inferences
**Funnel value of 1M Minecraft Shorts views (ESTIMATED)**
- Subscribers: 0.1–0.5% conversion gives **1,000–5,000 subscribers**.
- Long-form views over the following months: about 2,000–50,000. The low end is no deliberate bridge; the high end is serialized Shorts that tease a long-form series with pinned links.
- At a gaming long-form RPM of $2–$3.4 that is **$4–$170**, plus long-form sponsorship upside if the long videos are sponsored ($10–$25 per 1K: about $20–$1,250 on the same views).
- **Strategic value is mostly non-cash:**
  - 1M Shorts views counts toward the **3M/90-day fan-funding and Shopping tier**.
  - Shorts can unlock the 500-subscriber requirement quickly.
  - Shorts serve as a cheap hook-testing lab.
  - For full YPP entry from Feb 2027, Shorts count only on the separate 20M/90-day path; Shorts views do not add to the 8,000 watch hours.
- **Implication for the owner (INFERRED):** if long-form Minecraft is the money engine (per the earlier local notes), keep Shorts to about 25–40% of uploads and make them trailers or cut-downs of the long-form series, not a separate meme feed.

### Gaps
- No Minecraft-specific measured Shorts → long-form conversion rate.
- AIR's gaming subscriber result compares channel *strategies* (correlation), not a causal test.

## Q6. Brand-safety constraints: Minecraft usage guidelines on sponsorships, kids audience, YouTube and TikTok disclosure

### Takeaway
Every sponsor or affiliate placement on a Minecraft Short must:
- sit **outside the gameplay footage**, as a title card, end card or separate segment;
- be **suitable for children**;
- not imply Mojang endorsement;
- be **disclosed** with YouTube's paid-promotion toggle (and its equivalents on TikTok and Meta);
- avoid Made-for-Kids classification, which disables Shopping, Super Thanks and memberships.

Brands may not commission Minecraft-built ads ("constructed promotions") without Mojang's written approval.

### Cited Findings
- [OBSERVED] Minecraft Usage Guidelines:
  - "You place any title card, end credits, sponsorship notices, and other callouts outside of the actual game content. Sponsors and any callouts must be suitable for children and minors and must not cause harm to the brand."
  - "Minecraft is a place where players of all ages come together to play, not to be a part of an advertisement."
  - The guidelines "do not authorize commercial companies, corporate brands, advertising agencies… to use" Minecraft.
  - In-game events: limited to about 150 participants, and "You may not accept sponsorships for purposes of profit".
  - Source: [minecraft.net Usage Guidelines](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/usage-guidelines)
- [OBSERVED] Minecraft affiliates must follow "brand guidelines, accurate messaging, and all required disclosure rules". — [minecraft.net/affiliates](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/affiliates)
- [OBSERVED] YouTube: "If you feature branded content, sponsorships, endorsements, or other commercial relationships in your videos, you have to let YouTube know by selecting the paid promotion button… adds a disclosure label." Optional age and country restrictions are available. — [YouTube Help 154235](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/154235?hl=en)
- [OBSERVED] Shopping affiliate content that requires disclosure must follow YouTube's branded content policies. — [YouTube Help 13376398](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13376398?co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop&hl=en-EN)
- [OBSERVED] MFK channels or videos lose Shopping, Super Thanks and fan funding. — [YouTube Help 72857](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72857?hl=en); [YouTube Help 10879035](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10879035)
- [OBSERVED, via local note] The MFK RPM penalty is about 6× ($2.05 gaming vs $0.33 kids median). — local `policy_monetization.md` citing [AIR](https://air.io/en/air-data-findings/youtube-monetization-statistics-2026)
- [OBSERVED] TikTok: sponsored content earns no Creator Rewards. — [PayDM](https://paydm.app/blog/tiktok-creator-rewards-program)
- [OBSERVED] Meta: brand deals need the paid partnership label. — [Lumiflo](https://lumiflo.io/creators/facebook-content-monetization/)
- [OBSERVED] Shockbyte requires its servers to follow the Minecraft Usage Guidelines. Affiliates may not hide or redirect the affiliate link. — [Shockbyte ToS](https://shockbyte.com/legal/terms-of-service)

### Inferences
- **Sponsor categories (INFERRED).**
  - Safe: game server hosts, family-rated games (check age ratings), gaming peripherals, Minecraft-licensed merch.
  - Off-limits: gambling, skin-betting or case-opening sites, crypto, alcohol, mature-rated games, and anything Mojang could call "harm to the brand".
  - Many high-CPM non-endemic sponsors (finance, VPN-style mature ads) fit poorly with a kid-skewed Minecraft audience. This is another reason realistic Minecraft Short CPMs sit low.
- **Production rule (INFERRED).** Build sponsor reads as a 3–6 s non-gameplay card (a branded frame or the narrator on a neutral background), not overlays on gameplay. Tick the paid-promotion box. On TikTok, use separate non-CRP posts for sponsored versions.
- **Kid targeting (INFERRED).** A Shorts channel optimised for the youngest Minecraft viewers raises MFK risk, which zeroes Shopping, Super Thanks and memberships. That pushes toward a teen/general framing (also recommended in the local policy note).

### Gaps
- This session did not re-verify YouTube's rules on paid promotions inside MFK content or on YouTube Kids.
- No Mojang statement on sponsor reads inside *Shorts* specifically. The guidelines apply to all videos.

## Q7. Putting it together: the realistic "total money" of 1M Minecraft Shorts views (Nov 2026 → 2027)

### Takeaway
For a **new Minecraft Shorts channel** with 1M Shorts views spread across a month, the realistic non-AdSense total is about **$0–$150**. It is mostly small affiliate amounts plus a few long-form views; brand deals are absent at that size and Shorts AdSense goes to $0 from Feb 2027.

For an **established, well-run channel** (YPP and fan-funding tier unlocked, 50K+ subscribers, a few sponsored Shorts, cross-posted ≥60 s masters, a long-form series to feed), it is about **$500–$3,500 per 1M YouTube Shorts views**. Add about $150–$650 for each extra 1M TikTok qualified views, and possibly a $300–$2,000/month bonus *if* invited in a pilot market.

Sponsorship is the single largest lever. Platform payouts are minor.

### Cited Findings
- All inputs are cited in Q1–Q6. Key anchors:
  - Shorts AdSense is about $300 per 1M for a US-heavy audience. — [AIR](https://air.io/en/air-data-findings/youtube-shorts-rpm-vs-long-form-how-much-do-shorts-earn-in-2026)
  - It is $0 below 10M/90 days from 1 Feb 2027. — [YouTube Help 12843009](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12843009?hl=en)
  - Gaming Short sponsor rates are about $3–$15 per 1K. — [Bounty Board](https://www.bountyboard.gg/gaming-influencer-rates); [SponsorCraft](https://variant-intl.com/blog/youtube-shorts-cpm-rates.html); [1DS](https://1dsmgmt.com/blog/youtube-sponsorship-rates)
  - TikTok CRP pays $0.36–$1.04 per 1K qualified views. — [Mubert/Stack Influence](https://mubert.com/blog/tiktok-creator-rewards-pay-per-1000-views)
  - The Shorts bonus pilot reportedly pays up to ¥150K–¥300K a month. — [How Creators Get Paid](https://howcreatorsgetpaid.com/news/youtube-shorts-bonus-program-invites)

### Inferences
**Model per 1M YouTube Shorts views (ESTIMATED; assumes general-audience framing, not MFK)**

| Stream | New channel (<10K subs, no deals) | Established (50K+ subs, YPP + fan tier, 1–3 sponsored Shorts) | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shorts AdSense (reference, before Feb 2027) | $0–$300 | $70–$300 | AIR $0.07–$0.33/1K. **$0 after 1 Feb 2027 if <10M/90d** |
| Sponsored Shorts | $0 | $300–$2,000 | 10–20% of views on sponsored Shorts × $3–$10/1K; or 1–3 flat fees of $300–$1,500 |
| Affiliate (Minecraft 5%, hosting) | $5–$60 | $20–$300 | Q2 funnel model; spikes with intent-driven hosting Shorts |
| YouTube Shopping | $0 (not eligible before YPP) | $0–$200 | 15% median commission; kid-skewed buyers |
| Super Thanks / memberships | $0 (needs 500 subs + 3M/90d) | $0–$50 | 70% share; Shorts tippers are rare |
| Long-form funnel (AdSense on driven views) | $4–$50 | $20–$170 (+ sponsor upside on those long views) | Q5 |
| YouTube Shorts bonus pilot | $0 (invite, YPP, pilot country) | $0 or **≈$300–$2,000/month** if invited | Reported offers; per month, not per view |
| **Total, YouTube only** | **≈$10–$110 (+$0–$300 AdSense pre-2027)** | **≈$360–$2,700 (+ bonus if invited)** | |

**Same video reposted elsewhere, per additional 1M views on that platform (ESTIMATED)**
- TikTok CRP: **$150–$650**. Requires ≥60 s, 18+, 10K followers, an eligible country, and no sponsorship on that post.
- Facebook Reels (invite-only): **$20–$200**. Creator Fast Track adds $1K/month × 3 for 100K+ followers elsewhere.
- Snapchat Spotlight: probably **tens of dollars**. Requires ≥60 s, 18+, 100 h of view time per 28 days.
- Instagram Reels: **$0** from views.

**Sensitivity (INFERRED)**
- Biggest upside levers:
  - selling Shorts as add-ons to long-form sponsor deals;
  - gaming or hosting sponsors that accept kid-adjacent audiences;
  - a US/UK-heavy audience;
  - being invited to the bonus pilot.
- Biggest downside risks:
  - MFK classification (kills Shopping and fan funding, and cuts ad value about 6×);
  - an under-15 audience mix;
  - sub-60 s Shorts, which earn $0 on TikTok and Snapchat;
  - creator or account country outside the TikTok CRP and YouTube pilot lists;
  - a creator under 18, which blocks the Minecraft affiliate program, Super Thanks, TikTok CRP and Snapchat.
- **Bottom line (INFERRED):** 1M Minecraft Shorts views is worth roughly **one to ten US dollars per thousand views across all streams combined**, only at the established end and only with sponsors. Without sponsors it is worth **cents per thousand**. That supports the earlier local conclusion: Shorts are discovery and a 3M/90-day key for the fan-funding tier, while long-form plus sponsors is the business.

### Gaps
- Every row except the AIR AdSense anchor rests on vendor estimates or anecdotes. None of it is measured on a Minecraft Shorts channel.
- Bonus-program amounts are unconfirmed reports; country eligibility outside the 6 pilot markets is unknown.
- No measured Minecraft Shorts sponsor deal flow (how many deals a channel at N subscribers actually gets per month).
