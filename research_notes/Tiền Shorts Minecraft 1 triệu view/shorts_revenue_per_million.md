# How much 1 million Minecraft / gaming YouTube Shorts views earns (as of 2026-11-13), and the rules that decide it

Labels: OBSERVED = read on the cited page or local file in this session; INFERRED = my arithmetic or reasoning from OBSERVED figures; ESTIMATED = a range I built from several OBSERVED figures that are not Minecraft-specific. No Minecraft-specific Shorts RPM measured with a screenshot was found (see Gaps), so every "1M Minecraft Shorts = $X" number below is ESTIMATED.

## Q1. Measured Shorts RPM data 2025–2026 and what 1M Minecraft Shorts views pays

### Takeaway
No public source gives a measured Shorts RPM for Minecraft. The best measured data (AIR Media-Tech, 274 channels, Sept 2026) puts the median Shorts RPM at $0.07–0.20 in most niches and $0.02–0.05 for large Kids & Teens channels. Single-channel disclosures sit at $0.03–0.16. **ESTIMATE: 1M Minecraft Shorts views pays about $40–100 for a typical mixed-geography channel, $100–200 if the audience is mostly US/UK/CA/AU teens or adults, and $10–50 if it is kid-heavy or mostly from India, SEA or LatAm. It pays $0 if the channel is not in YPP, has not accepted the Shorts module, or (from 2027-02-01) has fewer than 10M qualified Shorts views in the trailing 90 days.**

## Q2. Mechanics: Creator Pool, 45%, music, engaged views, country allocation, Made for Kids

### Takeaway
Each month, Shorts Feed ad revenue in each country is pooled. Music-licensing cost comes out of the pool, and the rest is divided by each monetizing creator's share of engaged views in that country. The creator keeps 45%. Music reduces the pool, not the individual creator's allocation. Low Minecraft Shorts RPM comes from the country mix (young, global, India/SEA/LatAm-heavy viewers) and from Kids-type ad limits, not from a Minecraft penalty.

### Cited Findings
**Pool and split**
- Monthly Shorts Feed ad revenue is pooled to fund both creator rewards and music licensing. A Short without music sends all its revenue to the Creator Pool. With 1 track, "half of the revenue associated with its engaged views would be allocated to the Creator Pool". With 2 tracks, one third. Only music from YouTube's music partners or Dream Track triggers the deduction. OBSERVED — [YouTube Help 12504220](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220)
- The pool is allocated "by their share of total engaged views from monetizing creators' Shorts in each country". "each monetizing creator will be allocated 100% of the total number of engaged views on their Shorts", and the page says music use does not reduce this. "Monetizing creators will keep 45% of their allocated revenue, regardless if music was used or not." OBSERVED — [YouTube Help 12504220](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220)
- YouTube's worked example (illustrative, not a real rate): Country A has 100M engaged views and $100,000 of ad revenue, of which $10,000 covers music, leaving a $90,000 pool. A Short with 1M engaged views gets 1%, which is $900, and the creator earns $405. OBSERVED — [YouTube Help 12504220](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220)
- The 45% Shorts split is restated in the Aug 2026 blog: "55% for long-form videos and 45% for Shorts". Premium: creators share "30% of the net subscription revenue for Premium and 60% for Premium Lite". OBSERVED — [YouTube Blog 2026-08-10](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-partner-program-updates-2027-new-opportunities-earn/)

**Which views count**
- Ineligible views: non-original Shorts (unedited clips, reuploads, compilations with no added original content), artificial views, and views of content that is not advertiser-friendly. Shorts over 1 minute with claimed content are blocked from monetization. Eligibility starts on the date the Shorts Monetization Module is accepted, and earlier views do not count. OBSERVED — [YouTube Help 12504220](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220)
- From 2027-02-01, targeted Shorts ads shown to groups of 5 or fewer channels earn direct revenue, of which creators keep 45% of net. OBSERVED — [YouTube Help 12504220](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220)
- YouTube changed how public Shorts views are counted on 2025-03-31 (starts and replays now count), with "no change to monetization". Engaged views remain the payout basis. OBSERVED (headline-level from search result) — [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-changes-shorts-view-counts-no-change-to-monetization/543005/)

**Made for Kids and audience**
- Content set Made for Kids loses personalized advertising, comments, notifications, cards and end screens, channel memberships, Super Chat/Thanks and similar features. "Ads in certain categories may still appear." OBSERVED — [YouTube Help 9632097](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9632097)
- AIR Kids & Teens: Shorts RPM $0.02–$0.05 at the large tier, and a −26% long-form RPM effect is cited in AIR's checklist. AIR's text does not confirm the −26% (OBSERVED, flagged). — [AIR niche article](https://air.io/en/air-data-findings/what-is-youtube-shorts-rpm-in-your-niche-in-2026)
- Local sample of 782 Minecraft Shorts across 8 sub-niches (2026-11-13): 0% were flagged Made for Kids in every sub-niche. Third-party IP in titles was 38% in animation story, 15% in mob battle and 12% in noob-vs-pro. OBSERVED — `/home/claude/cine/reports/research/mc_shorts_2026-11-13/mc_shorts.md`

### Inferences
- **Why Minecraft Shorts skew low.** The audience is young and global, so a large share of engaged views fall in low-CPM country pools. Ads for under-18 viewers are restricted, and Made for Kids content loses personalized ads entirely. Every Minecraft channel draws from the same country pools as all other Shorts, so a US engaged view is worth the same whether it comes from Minecraft or another niche. Low Minecraft RPM is about *who watches*, not about Minecraft. INFERRED (age and geography skew not measured; see Gaps).
- **Self-declaring Made for Kids.** Most top Minecraft Shorts are not set to MFK (0% in the local sample). A channel that self-declares MFK would also lose comments and notifications, which hurts the funnel role of Shorts. INFERRED.
- **What protects revenue.** Keep content original (no reuploads or compilations of others' footage) and advertiser-friendly. Ineligible views earn $0 even inside YPP. INFERRED from OBSERVED rules.

### Gaps
- No public measurement of Minecraft Shorts audience age or country split was found. YouTube's support page does not publish per-country pool rates.
- The support page does not say how YouTube treats Made for Kids Shorts in the Creator Pool. AIR's Kids data is the only proxy.

## Q3. Eligibility rules now and from 2027-02-01

### Takeaway
Today, Shorts ad revenue requires YPP: 1,000 subscribers plus either 4,000 watch hours in 12 months or 10M qualified Shorts views in 90 days. The 500-subscriber tier unlocks only fan funding and shopping, with no ad revenue. From 2027-02-01, new applicants need 8,000 hours in 365 days or 20M Shorts views in 90 days. Every partner, existing ones included, then earns Shorts ad and Premium revenue only in periods with at least 10M qualified Shorts views in the trailing 90 days.

### Cited Findings
**Current YPP**
- The two ad-revenue routes: "Get 1,000 subscribers with 4,000 qualified watch hours in the last 12 months" or "…with 10 million qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days". Qualified Shorts views are views "from Shorts you've set public that appear in the Shorts Feed". Private, unlisted, deleted, ad-campaign and Image Post views are excluded. Creators must accept the updated terms in Studio by 2027-01-31. OBSERVED — [YouTube Help 72851](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851)
- Expanded/early YPP: 500 subscribers, 3 valid public uploads in the last 90 days, and either 3,000 qualified watch hours in 12 months or 3M qualified Shorts views in 90 days. It unlocks memberships, Super Chat/Stickers/Thanks, Jewels and gifts, and Shopping. It does not unlock ad revenue, which still needs the 1K-subscriber thresholds. OBSERVED — [YouTube Help 13429240](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13429240)

**Changes from 2027-02-01 (blog dated 2026-08-10)**
- New applicants: "8,000 qualified watch hours in the last 365 days … or 20 million qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days". Fan-funding and shopping thresholds are unchanged. YouTube calls these "the first significant changes since 2018". OBSERVED — [YouTube Blog](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-partner-program-updates-2027-new-opportunities-earn/)
- All partners: Shorts ad and subscription revenue sharing requires "creators who have 10 million qualified Shorts views over the last 90 days". Partners below that level stay in YPP and keep long-form revenue, and Shorts revenue resumes automatically once they are back above 10M. OBSERVED — [YouTube Blog](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-partner-program-updates-2027-new-opportunities-earn/); confirmed on [YouTube Help 12504220](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220)
- AIR repeats the 10M qualified Shorts views / 90 d threshold from 2027-02-01. OBSERVED — [AIR](https://air.io/en/air-data-findings/what-is-youtube-shorts-rpm-in-your-niche-in-2026)

### Inferences
- **Window under the current rules.** As of 2026-11-13, about 79 days remain to get accepted under the 1K + 10M/90 d (or 4,000 h) rules. Whether acceptance must be *completed* or only the *application submitted* by 2027-01-31 is not stated on the pages read. INFERRED; verify with YouTube.
- **Getting in early does not protect Shorts income.** Even a channel accepted before Feb 2027 earns $0 from Shorts in any 90-day window under 10M qualified views. For a small Minecraft Shorts channel, Shorts AdSense then becomes binary: about $130–330+/month at or above the threshold (ESTIMATED, Q1), and $0 below it. INFERRED.
- **Same mechanics everywhere.** These are YouTube-wide rules with no Minecraft or gaming exception. INFERRED.

### Gaps
- No YouTube page read here says whether the 500-subscriber tier thresholds change in 2027 (the blog says fan-funding thresholds are unchanged).
- The pages read do not say whether the 10M/90 d test is run daily or monthly.

## Q4. Value of Shorts beyond AdSense (subscriber conversion, effect on long-form)

### Takeaway
For gaming, AIR found that adding Shorts does not measurably lower long-form RPM: $3.42 vs $3.29, about −4%, within noise. That is unlike Entertainment (−60%) and Kids (−26%). Shorts' main value for a Minecraft channel is subscribers and reach feeding long-form. Long-form pays roughly 10–30× more per view in gaming.

### Cited Findings
**AIR gaming results**
- Gaming within-channel comparison: long-form RPM $3.42 without Shorts vs $3.29 with Shorts, about 4%, "within measurement noise". The companion article states the order ambiguously. OBSERVED — [AIR niche article](https://air.io/en/air-data-findings/what-is-youtube-shorts-rpm-in-your-niche-in-2026); [AIR companion](https://air.io/en/air-data-findings/youtube-shorts-rpm-vs-long-form-how-much-do-shorts-earn-in-2026)
- Gaming dose-response: $3.31 at 0 Shorts/month, $1.25 at 6–20, $2.11 at 21+. AIR says this "breaks the monotonic pattern" and "likely reflects a cohort effect". Causality is "not proven". OBSERVED — [AIR companion](https://air.io/en/air-data-findings/youtube-shorts-rpm-vs-long-form-how-much-do-shorts-earn-in-2026)

**AIR other niches, for contrast**
