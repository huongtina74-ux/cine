## Q1. Sub-formats and leading channels

### Takeaway
"Civilization" on Minecraft YouTube is really **three different products** that share a title word:
1. **Real mass-player events** (100–10,000 recruited humans).
   - Leaders: Ish, MrBeast Gaming, Parrot, Spoke, SpeedSilver, FlameFrags.
   - Typical video: 1.5–4 h, millions of views.
   - Owned almost entirely by channels with ≥1M subs.
2. **Scripted roleplay "X Civilization: The Movie" / "Minecraft, but I join X Civilization"** films (Evbo "Parkour Civilization" lineage).
   - Players: a small cast.
   - Typical video: 45–80 min.
   - This is the **only sub-format where 2024–2025-born channels reached 85K–294K subs**: Mac & Ollie, Gorf, Crumble.
3. **AI-agent simulations** (LLM bots via Mindcraft or Project Sid).
   - Only one sizeable original creator: Emergent Garden.
   - Around it sits a flood of re-explainer videos about Altera's Project Sid.

### Cited Findings

**A. Origin and definition of the mass-event format**
- TV Tropes credits **Magic Gum** with originating the format. He first posted videos about hypothetical Minecraft civilizations, then ran his own "Civilization Experiments". The format spread to Ish, SpeedSilver (Silver) and MrBeast ("1000 People Simulate Civilization") — [TV Tropes: Minecraft Civilization Experiments](https://www.tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/WebVideo/MinecraftCivilizationExperiments)
- Format per TV Tropes:
  - Player count is "usually 100 or above", on a "supervised server", usually in hardcore mode.
  - Teams start in "separated locations". The goal is to eliminate the other civilizations or reach "world peace".
  - The event runs "over a number of real life days".
  - Almost every video opens with a narration of the premise.
  - Source: [TV Tropes](https://www.tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/WebVideo/MinecraftCivilizationExperiments)

## Q2. Production method: real players vs bots vs scripted actors

### Takeaway
- **Mass events use real humans recruited by application.** Ish's team of 100+ handles applications, rule enforcement, tech and editing. The film comes from many player POVs and takes up to ~2 years to edit. Players appear to be **unpaid volunteers**; the exception is MrBeast's $50K prize pool.
- **"Civilization: The Movie" films are scripted roleplay** with a small cast and voice actors.
- **AI-agent videos use open frameworks** (Mindcraft on Mineflayer with any LLM API, or Altera's PIANO architecture). Scale is ~1–50 agents for creators. Project Sid reached 500 stable agents; 1,000+ "exceeded" its server's limits.

### Cited Findings

**Ish recruitment and management**
- Participants are accepted via applications read by a team. Accepted players get a **one-week prep period** to set up nations.
- Rules:
  - No killing "for no reason".
  - A "repeat rule" stops past main characters from being main characters again soon.
- Around day 7–8, nation leaders join a **Discord call** to decide how conflicts end.
- Source: [The Science Survey](https://thesciencesurvey.com/spotlight/2026/03/08/the-rise-of-minecraft-civilization-videos/)

**Ish team and tech**
- "Ish's team has over 100 people", covering applications, rule enforcement, technical problems and post-event editing.
- Servers can't normally hold 1,000+ players, so Ish **combines multiple servers**. The article describes servers with "no mods" and "zero gimmicks".
- Source: [The Science Survey](https://thesciencesurvey.com/spotlight/2026/03/08/the-rise-of-minecraft-civilization-videos/)
- Ish's thank-you post names the team roles: **Event Staff, Applications Team, Developers, Build Team, Production/Admin Team** — [ish on X](https://x.com/ish13c/status/1973161316887175598)

**Scripting (Ish)**
- Not scripted. Most clips are the actual event, with few re-shoots. Participants agree to roleplay, including villain roles. Staff are sometimes unsure what will happen. — [The Science Survey](https://thesciencesurvey.com/spotlight/2026/03/08/the-rise-of-minecraft-civilization-videos/)
- Ish sets only the opening scenario. Players formed their own nations. Ish flies around filming in first person and later shapes the footage. — [Push to Talk](https://www.pushtotalk.gg/p/the-best-youtube-video-of-2025-was-filmed-in-minecraft)

**Payment and participants**
- No source mentions paying ordinary participants: [The Science Survey](https://thesciencesurvey.com/spotlight/2026/03/08/the-rise-of-minecraft-civilization-videos/) explicitly does not mention funding or participant payment.
- What participants get is **exposure**:
  - The main character's channel grew from 10K to >181K subs.
  - Participant Lingulini later got a main role in MrBeast's 1,000-player video.
  - Source: [The Science Survey](https://thesciencesurvey.com/spotlight/2026/03/08/the-rise-of-minecraft-civilization-videos/)
- MrBeast's 1,000-player event:
  - $50,000 prize split among the winning team; first-come sign-up.
  - The girls' side had planned for 500 streamers and fell short, so regular players filled the spots.
  - Participants alleged that ~30% of the "girls" were men who sabotaged their team, and that some players built swastikas.
  - Rule-breakers were banned after review and replaced by the next registrants.
  - Source: [Mein-MMO](https://mein-mmo.de/en/mrbeast-lets-girls-compete-against-boys-but-one-participant-says-30-of-the-girls-were-guys,1544032)

**SpeedSilver recruitment (2022)**
- Announced a "Space Civilization event". Application info was in the replies, and "content creators REPLY OR DM ME if you wanna join". — [SpeedSilver on X, 2022](https://x.com/speedsilverr/status/1572979078944137216)

**Scripted movie sub-format**
- Evbo's Parkour Civilization part 2 used other YouTubers as voice actors and has a story with lore and characters — [Sportskeeda](https://sportskeeda.com/minecraft/what-minecraft-parkour-civilization-all-need-know)
- Mac & Ollie's description credits no cast — [YouTube nvX_3J-u1u0](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nvX_3J-u1u0)

**Project Sid (AI agents)**
- **Agent counts by experiment:**
  - 25-agent and 49-agent (4 h) single-agent benchmarks.
  - 50 agents for relationships (>4 h, equal to 12 in-game days).
  - 30 agents per village for specialization.
  - 29 agents for taxes/constitution (25 constituents, 3 influencers, 1 election manager).
  - **500 agents** for cultural transmission (2.5 h).
  - Runs of "over 1000 agents" "exceeded the computational constraints of our Minecraft server environment". Agents were "sporadically unresponsive".
- **Models:** GPT-4o for best results. A Claude 3.5 Sonnet snapshot appears in the base-model comparisons.
- **Not disclosed:** hardware, server configuration, cost and compute budget.
- **Emergent behaviors:** roles (farmer, miner, guard, blacksmith…), tax-rate changes after a vote (deposits fell from ~20% to ~9%), Pastafarianism spreading from 20 priests, town-specific memes.
- **Limitations:**
  - No vision or spatial reasoning, which limits building.
  - Item collection saturated around ~320 items.
- Source: [arXiv 2411.00114](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.00114v1)

**Mindcraft (Emergent Garden's framework)**
- 5.9k GitHub stars, MIT license.
- 17 LLM providers, including OpenAI, Gemini, Anthropic, xAI, DeepSeek, **Ollama (local)**, Groq, OpenRouter and vLLM.
- Runs multiple agents from profile JSONs. Supports Minecraft Java up to 1.21.11 (1.21.6 recommended). Needs Node 18/20.
- Warnings: "Do not connect this bot to public servers with coding enabled". Code-writing is off by default and Docker is advised.
- Source: [GitHub mindcraft-bots/mindcraft](https://github.com/mindcraft-bots/mindcraft)

**Hosting / sponsorship**
- Server hosts run sponsorship/partnership programmes. Apex Hosting advertises partnerships and an event guide ("How to run a Minecraft event"), and was "official partner of Minecraft Monday" — [Apex partnerships](https://apexminecrafthosting.com/de/partnerships/); [Apex blog](https://apexminecrafthosting.com/blog/were-now-the-official-partner-of-minecraft-monday/). The programme's terms could not be retrieved.

### Inferences
- **Mass-event cost structure (INFERRED, not observed).** The money cost is mostly in staff, dev/build and multi-month editing; player wages are near zero.
  - Recruits are free because the host's audience supplies demand: 8,000 applicants for 1,000 spots, then 60,000 for 1,300.
  - A new channel has no such pull. It would have to pay players, offer prizes (MrBeast: $50K), or borrow an established creator's audience.
- **Moderation risk at scale (INFERRED).** The MrBeast case shows that 1,000 strangers bring sabotage, impersonation and hate-symbol builds. Staff headcount scales with player count.
- **Scripted-movie production (INFERRED from format, not verified by viewing).**
  - Typical setup: a custom map/datapack, 3–15 actor accounts recorded in first person (Replay Mod or several POVs), a script, voice acting and heavy editing.
  - This is the sub-format a small studio could partly automate: NPC/bot bodies via Mineflayer or Citizens, camera paths via Replay Mod, and TTS or hired voices.
  - Whether audiences accept AI voices here is **not measured**.
- **AI-agent production (ESTIMATED).**
  - Cost formula: agents × LLM calls per agent-minute × minutes × tokens per call × provider price.
  - Neither Project Sid nor Mindcraft publishes per-run cost, and provider prices were not fetched here.
  - Local Ollama models remove API spend but cost GPU time. The owner's RTX 3060 12 GB can realistically run small local models for a handful of agents, not hundreds (INFERRED).
  - Project Sid's own server choked at 1,000+ agents, so "1,000 AI agents" claims by small creators should be treated as suspect unless shown.
- **AI-video authenticity (INFERRED).** Emergent Garden's videos mix genuine runs with heavy editing and narration. The "how much is scripted" share is not measured.

### Gaps
- No found source on Ish's server hosting costs, plugin stack (e.g. which proxy, such as Velocity/BungeeCord), editor headcount vs volunteer share, or whether any staff are paid.
- Not found:
  - Player-release or consent-form practice for any organizer.
  - The Wheels/Sigils production model.
  - Mac & Ollie's cast size and voice method.
  - LLM spend for any public AI-agent video.
- The Voyager paper (NVIDIA/Caltech, 2023) was not fetched in this pass, so no claims are made about it.

## Q3. Money, concentration, new entrants, saturation

### Takeaway
- **Views are enormous but extremely concentrated.** In the local snapshot, 5 of the 7 ≥1M multiplayer-event videos came from >1M-sub channels. Restricted to <1M-sub channels, the median is 181K.
- No public revenue or RPM figure was found for any channel in this niche.
- New-entrant evidence exists only for the **scripted "Civilization: The Movie"** sub-format: Mac & Ollie (2025-04, 207K subs), Crumble (≈2025-09, 85.9K), Gorf (≈2024-10, 294K). It does not exist for real mass events or AI-agent originals.

### Cited Findings
- MULTIPLAYER_EVENT: n=12, median 1.744M, P90 15.1M, 7/12 ≥1M, **5/7 of the ≥1M videos from >1M-sub channels**. Only one ≤365-day channel, a creator spin-off. The <1M-sub cut has median 181K. — `mc_only.md`, OBSERVED 2026-11-11
- Ish went from 1.1M to 2M subs after one film, and that film drew 34M views in four months — [The Science Survey](https://thesciencesurvey.com/spotlight/2026/03/08/the-rise-of-minecraft-civilization-videos/)
- Momentum readings:
  - **Parrot's 10,000-player war** reached 21.51M at ~1.76M views/day over 7 days, which is 0.80× its lifetime pace and decelerating.
  - **Spoke's COLD WAR** slowed from ~505K/day to ~366K per 24 h.
  - Tracked status: "hot" but "**không hợp kênh** — needs hundreds of real players; production scale we do not have".
  - Source: `minecraft_story_principles.md` §S10, OBSERVED 2026-11-05…11
- Mac & Ollie: 207K subs, 31.76M lifetime views in ~585 days, 42 videos, recent median 170K/video — `sf_channels.json`, OBSERVED
- The MrBeast event cost at least $50K in prizes and drew >12M views — [Mein-MMO](https://mein-mmo.de/en/mrbeast-lets-girls-compete-against-boys-but-one-participant-says-30-of-the-girls-were-guys,1544032)
- The local CineFactory money model does not cover this niche. It uses an AGG-EST RPM of $2–4 for Minecraft challenge content and notes a measured kids RPM of $0.33 — `docs/niches/minecraft_success_failure_2026-11-12.md` §5 (AGG-EST, not channel-specific).

### Inferences
- **ESTIMATED: Mac & Ollie revenue order of magnitude.**
  - Monthly views: 31.76M ÷ (585/30.4) ≈ 1.65M views/month on average.
  - At an assumed $2–4 RPM (AGG-EST, not measured for this channel): ≈ $3.3K–6.6K/month lifetime-average.
  - Recent months are probably lower, because the recent median is 170K vs a 756K lifetime average.
  - Sponsorship income is unknown.
- **INFERRED: participants earn exposure, not wages.** Hosts monetize the edited film plus livestreams (Ish State streams playlist). Participants are rewarded with exposure (one main character went 10K → 181K subs). Hosting companies likely sponsor, but no terms were found.
- **INFERRED on saturation:**
  - The "N players simulate X" title template is saturated at the top: Parrot, Spoke, SpeedSilver, FlameFrags, MrBeast and Ish all run it, with player counts escalating from 100 to 1,000 to 10,000 vs 10,000. A new channel cannot out-scale them.
  - "AI agents simulate civilization" is saturated with *re-explainers* of one 2024 paper but thin on *original* recurring runs.
  - The scripted "X Civilization: The Movie" template is still admitting new channels (three in 2024–2025). Each new gimmick (Size, Speedbridge, Strong, Lucky Block) yields a new title.
- **INFERRED fit for a faceless automation studio:**
  - Real mass events: **no**. They need a community, 100+ staff and months of editing.
  - Scripted civilization movies with a bot/NPC cast: **possible secondary niche**. It is the only sub-format with proven 2024–25 entrants, and it overlaps the owner's existing machinima/original-IP tooling.
  - Original AI-agent runs (Mindcraft + local or cheap LLM): **possible experiment**. It plays to the automation skill set, but the only proven channel (Emergent Garden) wins on programmer personality and novelty. Its view ceiling (14K–1.7M, avg ~250K) is far below the mass-event tier.

### Gaps
- No public RPM, CPM, sponsorship rate or revenue disclosure was found for Ish, Parrot, Spoke, SpeedSilver, Mac & Ollie, Gorf, Crumble or Emergent Garden.
- Not measured:
  - Failure rate of new channels attempting mass-player events. The local sample had only one ≤365-day channel, a spin-off.
  - Long-run view decay of AI-agent videos.

## Q4. Risks: EULA/usage guidelines, consent, YouTube inauthentic-content policy, audience age

### Takeaway
- **Mojang's guidelines allow monetized YouTube videos** if they are free to view and add enough original content. Restrictions on events (sponsorship-for-profit, an ~150-participant cap for in-person events) and on using "Minecraft" as a primary name need care.
- **YouTube's July 2026 clarification** targets "templated storylines" and "AI-generated content made with generic or unoriginal templates":
  - This bears directly on mass-produced AI-agent "1000 AIs…" explainers and on weekly formula "X Civilization" movies.
  - Genuine, varied, commentated runs remain monetizable.
- **Mass events carry moderation and consent risk** (MrBeast case).

### Cited Findings

**Mojang usage guidelines** — [minecraft.net/usage-guidelines](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/usage-guidelines) (page shows © 2026, no updated date)
- Videos:
  - Must be "free to view" in their entirety.
  - Creator must "add enough of your own unique content".
  - No selling copies.
- Servers:
  - Access cost must be the same for everyone.
  - Cosmetics may be sold, except capes.
  - In-game currency must not imply a Mojang link.
- Events:
  - In-person events are limited to "approximately 150 participants".
  - "You may not accept sponsorships for purposes of profit".
  - Live in-game events must be free to view or attend.
  - Mojang can stop non-compliant events.
- Naming: "You may not use the Minecraft name as the primary or dominant name or title."
- No AI-specific clause.

**YouTube policy**
- July 15, 2025: "repetitious content" was renamed "inauthentic content", described as a "minor update".
  - Example of the target: "Channels that upload narrative stories with only superficial differences between them".
  - AI tools are welcome. Realistic altered or synthetic content must be disclosed.
  - Source: [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/youtube-clarifies-monetization-update-inauthentic-repeated-content/752892/)
- July 13, 2026 clarification ("not a new policy", a "communications fix"):
  - "Generic or repetitive content" = "content that looks like it's made with a template". This includes "templated storylines" and "AI-generated content made with generic or unoriginal templates".
  - "Unsatisfying or off-putting" = content relying on "emotionally manipulative formulas".
  - AI personas posing as experts on sensitive topics are not allowed.
  - A creator's "transformative 'spin'" keeps a channel in good standing.
  - It says nothing specific about gameplay or automation.
  - Source: [Tubefilter, 2026-07-13](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/07/13/youtube-inauthentic-content-monetization-policy-update/)

**Consent / moderation**
- Impostor sign-ups, team sabotage, swastika builds and bans with replacement in a 1,000-player event — [Mein-MMO](https://mein-mmo.de/en/mrbeast-lets-girls-compete-against-boys-but-one-participant-says-30-of-the-girls-were-guys,1544032)
- Ish participants "agree to roleplay" villain roles — [The Science Survey](https://thesciencesurvey.com/spotlight/2026/03/08/the-rise-of-minecraft-civilization-videos/)

**Audience age and kids flag**
- One Ish main character is a college student, age unknown — [The Science Survey](https://thesciencesurvey.com/spotlight/2026/03/08/the-rise-of-minecraft-civilization-videos/)
- In the local snapshot, the Parrot, Spoke and SpeedSilver multiplayer videos all have `made_for_kids=False` — `classified_videos.csv`, OBSERVED

**Mindcraft security**
- Mindcraft can execute LLM-written code, and the project warns against public servers with coding enabled — [GitHub](https://github.com/mindcraft-bots/mindcraft)

### Inferences
- **INFERRED: policy risk by sub-format.**
  - **Lowest:** original AI-agent runs with distinct experiments and real commentary, plus disclosure where realistic synthetic voices are used.
  - **Medium:** a weekly "X Civilization: The Movie" with the same story beats and only the gimmick swapped. This could read as "narrative stories with only superficial differences", so each film needs a distinct plot.
  - **Highest:** channels re-narrating Project Sid with stock or AI voice over generic templates. These hit "generic or unoriginal templates" and reused-content rules.
- **INFERRED: the Mojang sponsorship clause is ambiguous.** The "no sponsorships for profit" clause sits in the events section. How it applies to an online YouTube event sponsored by a server host is not settled by the text. Many large events appear to carry host partnerships (Apex/Minecraft Monday). Ask Mojang or legal before taking event sponsorship money.
- **INFERRED: consent and likeness.**
  - Mass events need written participant terms covering recording, use of username/skin/voice, minors (parental consent), and bans.
  - AI-agent videos avoid the human-consent problem entirely, which is a structural advantage for a faceless studio.
- **INFERRED: audience age.** Audiences skew teen. Not-for-kids status keeps RPM above the $0.33 kids figure, but no demographic data was found.
- **CineFactory hard-rule fit (INFERRED):**
  - Real-player events would require recruiting strangers and, at scale, probably paying prizes. That is an owner decision with spend implications.
  - AI-agent runs must not use metered LLM APIs without owner approval. Local Ollama is the zero-credit path.

### Gaps
- No public creator-side participant release forms or consent templates were found.
- No COPPA or minor-participant handling policy was found for Ish, MrBeast or SpeedSilver events.
- No audience age breakdown was found for any channel in the niche.
- No case of a civilization or AI-agent Minecraft channel being demonetized under the inauthentic-content policy was found.
