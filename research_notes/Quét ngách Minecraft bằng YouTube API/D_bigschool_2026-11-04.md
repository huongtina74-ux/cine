## Reading (INFERRED)
1. BigSchool shows that the format scaled: original-style family, emotion and revenge animation with generic cast archetypes (Noob, Noob Girl, Herobrine family, Zombie Girl) reached 32M subscribers and 14B views.
2. Then the channel dropped long-form. Its Shorts sit around a 50K median, with one burst of interactive "choose" Shorts in June 2026. From October 2025 it began borrowing trending third-party IP (Verity, JJ & Mikey), and it has now gone quiet.
3. The cast is made of generic archetypes, not ownable characters. That makes the cast easy to copy and hard to franchise, unlike Maizen's JJ & Mikey.
4. Very high volume (7,394 videos, 166 in one month) is a risk under the inauthentic/repetitive-content rule.

## Opportunity (INFERRED)
The demand BigSchool built for long-form family/emotion stories (the 1.7M median) is now served mainly by Maizen fan channels. An original long-form cast with distinct, ownable characters can fill that gap.
