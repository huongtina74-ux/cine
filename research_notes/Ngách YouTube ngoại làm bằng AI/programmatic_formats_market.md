# Programmatic / 3D / AI-produced faceless long-form YouTube formats: market map (state 2026-11-12)

Scope: foreign (non-Vietnamese), English or other high-RPM audiences. The local docs already measure history docs, disaster 3D, WW2, western/faith stories, sleep, slow English, finance, true crime, space docs, tech/AI explainers, business docs, geopolitics, nature, kids, ambient music and Minecraft horror (`docs/niches/youtube_wide_niche_choice_2026-11-12.md`, `reports/research/wide_scan_2026-11-12/wide_scan.md`, `docs/niches/youtube_money_niches_2026-11-12.md`). Those are not redone here. Where a format below overlaps one of them (space, tech/AI), the local OBSERVED numbers are quoted and marked "LOCAL OBSERVED".

Labels:

## 1. Programmatic/visual formats: RPM evidence, demand, example channels, 2025–2026 breakouts, competition and AI saturation

### Takeaway
No source measures RPM at the format level (data viz, 3D scale comparison, "how it works" 3D, Manim, medical 3D, software tutorials). The only measured anchor is AIR Media-Tech's class table: Education & Science has a median RPM of $10.22 (P25–P75 $2.31–$19.50) and Gadgets & Tech $2.33. A format's RPM therefore depends mostly on whether YouTube and advertisers treat it as **education with long watch time** or as **tech/entertainment/Shorts traffic**.

Demand evidence is strong for **3D "true scale"/size-comparison** and **3D mechanical "how it works"** videos. It is weak for cheap 2D bar-chart races and generic AI-tool tutorials, which are saturated. Software tutorials (Excel and similar) earn mainly outside AdSense, through courses.

## 2. Which formats give the best revenue per hour of production with Blender/Python/AI?

### Takeaway
INFERRED: The best expected revenue per hour is likely **long-form (15–40 min) 3D "true scale" comparisons and procedural/engineering "how it works" explainers aimed at US/Western adults**. They combine:
- the Education-class RPM ceiling,
- strong evidence that viewers watch them (Big Data Factor averages 441.7K views over 61 videos),
- the fact that once a CineFactory Blender asset or rig library exists, each video becomes mostly data, scripting and rendering rather than hand animation.

Bar-chart races and AI-tool tutorials take the fewest hours but return almost nothing per video. Hand-crafted Jared Owen–level 3D gives the most per video but takes about 200 hours each.

### Cited Findings
- OBSERVED: Top-end mechanical 3D takes about 200 creator hours plus contractors per video, about every 2 months, and AdSense is the main income. — [TubeTalk/Jared Owen, 2026-06-01](https://tubetalk.buzzsprout.com/271511/episodes/19196651-how-jared-owen-builds-3d-videos-that-last)
- ESTIMATED: The 3D true-scale channel Big Data Factor averages 441.7K views per video over 61 videos, with 46% of views from Shorts. — [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/channel/bigdatafactor)
- ESTIMATED: The programmatic data-viz channel @DataVisualization2 averages 25.9K views per video over 840 videos, with estimated ad revenue of $18–25 per month. — [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/channel/datavisualization2)
- OBSERVED: Education has a 77% monetized-view rate against Tech's 34%, and longer watch time enables mid-rolls. — [AIR](https://air.io/en/air-data-findings/which-youtube-niche-makes-the-most-money-in-2026-ranked-by-real-rpm-and-cpm)
- OBSERVED: "Slideshows that all have the same narration" and near-identical narrative videos are named as inauthentic, a monetization risk across the whole channel. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/youtube-clarifies-monetization-update-inauthentic-repeated-content/752892/)

### Inferences
- INFERRED, illustrative arithmetic (not forecasts):
  - Revenue per video ≈ views × long-form share × RPM / 1000.
  - **Big Data Factor-like 3D scale** (441.7K × 0.54 long-form):
    - At the Education median of $10.22: about **$2,440 per video**.
    - At the Tech median of $2.33: about **$560 per video**.
  - **Data-viz bar race** (25.9K average): at $2–10, about $50–260 per video.
  - If a templated Blender/Python 3D-scale video took about 20–40 owner/agent hours (not measured for CineFactory), that would be roughly $14–120 per hour. A Jared Owen–style video at about 200 hours and a few million views could land similar or higher per hour, but with much higher variance and cash-flow lag.
  - None of these are measured, and the figures are for a channel that has already succeeded.
- INFERRED, CineFactory fit:
  - We already run Blender 4.5 headless on an RTX 3060 and Python pipelines (`cf_auto_edit.py`, Director Cut). This lets us procedurally generate:
    - scale lineups (objects from a parameter table),
    - camera fly-bys and labels,
    - mechanical cutaways (gears, engines, locks, CNC machines),
    - simple physics with Blender rigid body or fluids.
  - Our WoodFab/CNC domain knowledge suits "how CNC/woodworking machines work". This is a niche, so its demand is not measured.
- INFERRED, ranking by expected revenue per production hour for a NEW CineFactory channel (highest first):
  1. 3D true-scale/size comparisons, long-form, US framing
  2. Procedural "how it works" 3D (machines, infrastructure, engineering), 15–25 min
  3. Manim/Motion Canvas science and math explainers, which are code-only but need deep scripting and are a smaller niche
  4. Space 3D sims (Education RPM, but LOCAL OBSERVED weak new-channel survival)
  5. 2D bar-chart races and map timelapses (cheap, but low views and often non-US audiences)
  6. Generic AI-tool tutorials (Tech RPM, saturated, need an on-screen/voice persona and fast news)
  7. Satisfying loops and medical 3D (low class RPM or YMYL)
- INFERRED: The biggest lever on revenue per hour is reusing assets across videos: one rig or library of machines, buildings or vehicles used in many episodes. Varied script structure and real data sources are needed to stay clear of the "inauthentic" label.

### Gaps
- Real CineFactory hours per minute for these formats have not been measured. They should be logged in `data/production_ledger.jsonl` in a pilot.
- There is no measured long-form/Shorts split or RPM for any 3D scale channel.
- The Aether 3D figures (a breakout candidate) could not be verified; the page was blocked.

## 3. Non-AdSense money in these niches: sponsorships, software affiliate, selling templates/assets, B2B leads

### Takeaway
For software tutorials, AdSense is secondary: the documented money is **courses** (Leila Gharani, over $3M a year, 2024). Software affiliate terms are mixed:
- Adobe pays 85% of the first month, which is large on a one-off basis.
- Autodesk pays only 7–9% of the first year.

3D explainer creators say the real side income is **work-for-hire contracts** that come in through the channel, plus digital-product sponsors such as VPNs for global audiences. CineFactory's existing Blender assets could also be sold (Superhive 70%, Fab 88%, Gumroad about 10% + $0.50).

### Cited Findings
- OBSERVED: Leila Gharani's Excel courses make over $3M a year, with most traffic from YouTube (July 2024). — [Online Course Show ep. 230](https://metacast.app/podcast/the-online-course-show/ccaN3HtQ/230-3-million-per-year-with-excel-courses-featuring-leila-gharani/7SCAP2Tp)
- OBSERVED: Jared Owen says sponsors are hard to fit, VPN-type digital products work better for a worldwide audience, and early **work-for-hire** contracts from viewers enabled his full-time move. — [TubeTalk, 2026-06-01](https://tubetalk.buzzsprout.com/271511/episodes/19196651-how-jared-owen-builds-3d-videos-that-last)
- OBSERVED: Adobe affiliate terms (via Partnerize, with no minimum audience but approval required):
  - 85% of the first month's price for monthly plans and annual plans paid monthly
  - 8.33% of the first year for prepaid annual plans
  - 30-day cookie
  - Source: [Adobe affiliates](https://www.adobe.com/no/affiliates.html)
- OBSERVED (third-party directory, updated 2026-02-03): Autodesk pays 7–9% on new subscriptions, first year only, with a 60-day cookie via CJ Affiliate. — [UpPromote directory: Autodesk](https://uppromote.com/affiliate-directory/autodesk/)
- OBSERVED (sibling note, sourced there):
  - Superhive (ex-Blender Market) pays creators 70% on the Free plan, 80% on Pro ($49/month) and 90% on Ultimate ($199/month), less $0.49 and 5.5% per sale on Free. It forbids fully AI-generated products.
  - Fab pays sellers 88%.
  - Gumroad charges 10% + $0.50 plus card fees on direct sales.
  - Source: `research_notes/Kiếm tiền thị trường nước ngoài bằng code/3d_asset_marketplaces.md` citing [Superhive pricing](https://superhivemarket.com/pricing), [Fab docs](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/fab/publisher-get-started-in-fab) and [Gumroad fees](https://gumroad.com/help/article/66-gumroads-fees)
- OBSERVED: The Subscribr software-tutorials article lists affiliate partner *types* (hosting, domains, editors, fonts, drawing tablets) but gives **no** commission or sponsorship rates. — [Subscribr](https://subscribr.ai/youtube-strategy/youtube-niche-monetization-software-tutorials)
- OBSERVED: 3Blue1Brown links Patreon and merchandise, and its FAQ lists "Can my organization sponsor one of your videos?" without public pricing. — [3Blue1Brown FAQ](https://www.3blue1brown.com/faq/)

### Inferences
- INFERRED: The money model per format:
  - **Software/CAD/CNC tutorials:** courses, templates or plugins, and B2B leads. CineFactory/WoodFab could funnel cabinet/CNC tutorial viewers to its own nesting/CAM software, which connects to the sibling notes `cabinet_cnc_software_market.md` and `software_plugins_saas.md`.
  - **3D "how it works" and true-scale videos:** AdSense first, then work-for-hire 3D explainers for companies (manufacturers, edtech), then selling the Blender rigs or assets on Superhive or Fab.
  - **Data viz:** templates or "chart race" tools sold on Gumroad. Its AdSense is weak.
  - **AI-tool tutorials:** affiliate- and sponsor-driven, but saturated and fast-churning.
- INFERRED: For a faceless Vietnam-based studio, B2B work-for-hire and asset sales probably beat one-off affiliate commissions, because the channel itself acts as the portfolio. Autodesk's 7–9% first-year rate is low. Adobe's 85% of the first month is attractive per conversion but needs creative-software buyers.

### Gaps
- No sponsorship CPM or flat-rate data was found for faceless 3D/data/education channels in 2026. Sponsorship marketplaces (ThoughtLeaders, SponsorRadar) require login.
- No affiliate terms were verified for Blender add-on stores, SketchUp, SolidWorks, Fusion, ElevenLabs or AI video tools.
- There is no income disclosure for Animagraffs, Branch Education, Kurzgesagt or Primer (Patreon, store, sponsors). Patreon counts were not retrieved.
- Courses-vs-AdSense splits for CAD/BIM/Blender tutorial channels were not found.
