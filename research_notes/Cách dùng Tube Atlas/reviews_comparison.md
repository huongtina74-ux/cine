# Tube Atlas (tubeatlas.com): independent reviews, accuracy and comparison with alternatives (as of 13 Nov 2026)

> Scope note (read first). "Tube Atlas" at tubeatlas.com is **not** a cloud niche-finder or outlier database like NexLev, TubeLab or 1of10. It is a **one-time-purchase Windows/Mac desktop app** from AppBreed (Innantech Industries Inc., developer Dave Guindon). It has been sold since Aug 2021 through PayKickstart affiliate launches with a 50% affiliate commission and upsells. It extracts data from YouTube and Google pages from your own computer. No other product named "TubeAtlas", "Tube-Atlas" or "tubeatlas.ai" turned up in searches; look-alike names (TubeMind, TubeSpace, TubeFathom, TubeLab) are different products.
> Number labels follow the CineFactory rule: OBSERVED = read on a cited page; INFERRED = my reasoning; no OWNER numbers here. Nothing was measured hands-on, because no sign-up or install was allowed.

## 2. How accurate are its metrics vs YouTube's real numbers?

### Takeaway
No published accuracy test of Tube Atlas exists. Raw counts (views, likes, comments, subscribers, publish date) are read from YouTube pages, so they should match whatever YouTube shows publicly. The changelog, though, shows these extractors break repeatedly when YouTube changes its pages. "Estimated earnings" uses an undisclosed formula and should be treated like any public-view revenue estimate: a rough range at best. Tube Atlas has **no outlier score and no RPM-by-niche model**.

### Cited Findings
- The video grid has 35 columns, including Total Video Views, Likes, Comments, Date Published, Publish Age in Days, **Average Views per Day**, **Estimated Video Earnings**, Estimated Earnings per Day, Total Channel Subscribers, Ads Found, Emails and Phone Numbers in Description. — [tubeatlas.com](https://tubeatlas.com/)
- The sales page does not list an outlier or "views vs channel median" score. The channel tool gives "27+ data points", and the affiliate page says "17+", which is inconsistent. — [tubeatlas.com](https://tubeatlas.com/), [tubeatlas.com/affiliates](https://tubeatlas.com/affiliates)
- The public changelog (AppBreed) shows repeated breakage from YouTube and Google changes (OBSERVED):
  - v1.0.5.0 (21 Jan 2024): fixed likes, and channel title, subscribers and "date joined", not extracting "due to changes on youtube".
  - v1.0.5.5 (6 Aug 2024): fixed total channel views, total channel videos and subscribers extraction.
  - v1.0.5.7 (15 Aug 2024): descriptions "not the same as the descriptions on youtube".
  - v1.0.6.0 (24 Jan 2025): Trends "still restricted due to Google getting more and more strict on extracting data to fast".
  - v1.0.6.3–1.0.6.4 (Oct 2025): trending-videos source swapped; video-to-text and the player fixed after YouTube changes.
  - v1.0.2.9 / v1.0.3.0 (Dec 2022): channel tool broken for EU users (GDPR); the "channel country" column was removed because it froze the software.
  - Source: [appbreed.com version history](https://appbreed.com/apps/api/app-download.php?aid=26)
- v1.0.6.6 (12 Apr 2026) "improved video extraction to deal with YouTube's new 'too many requests' limit". It is the latest release; there have been only two in 2026. — [SaaSPic](https://saaspic.com/tools/tube-atlas)
- Search volume, CPC and competition come from "free resources" or an optional paid Keywords Everywhere account. They are not Tube Atlas's own data. — [SaaSPic](https://saaspic.com/tools/tube-atlas), [tubeatlas.com](https://tubeatlas.com/)
- For comparison, Social Blade's earnings method is views/day × $0.25–$4.00 RPM. It "assumes that every view on the channel is monetized… It is entirely possible NO views are monetized, or views come from a lower monetized medium such as shorts." — [Social Blade FAQ](https://socialblade.com/help/how-are-estimated-earnings-calculated)
- Public-view revenue tools "are modeled from public views, not payouts". Real RPM only comes from YouTube Studio or authenticated Analytics. — [TubeAnalytics blog, Apr 2026 (vendor of a competing tool)](https://www.tubeanalytics.net/blog/social-blade-revenue-estimates-accuracy)
- YouTube's API policy allows third-party revenue estimates only if they are "clearly presented as third-party estimates and not as YouTube approved or published data". — [YouTube derived-metrics policy](https://developers.google.com/youtube/terms/derived-metrics-policy)

### Inferences
- **Trust (cross-check rarely):** views, likes, comment counts, publish date, title, tags and description. They are copied from public pages, but re-extract after any app update and spot-check 3–5 rows against youtube.com, because the changelog shows silent "not extracted" failures. v1.0.5.4 even added a "ReExtract NOT EXTRACTED rows" feature.
- **Subscriber counts:** YouTube's public subscriber figures are abbreviated (rounded to three significant digits). This comes from general knowledge and was not re-verified in this session. So any subscriber-based ratio is approximate for small channels.
- **Never trust as-is:** "Estimated Video Earnings". The method is undisclosed; it ignores Shorts vs long-form, country mix, niche RPM, Made-for-Kids status and whether the channel is even monetized. For a faceless studio deciding on a niche, it is not a substitute for NexLev or TubeLab RPM ranges, which are also estimates but at least niche-aware, or for real Studio data.
- **Outlier score:** export the grid to CSV and compute views ÷ channel median ourselves (`tools/cf_research_stats.py` already does medians and outliers). This also fits the CLAUDE.md rule that "the benchmark is the niche MEDIAN".
- **Channel age:** "date joined" is extracted, but it broke in Jan 2024, so verify it on the channel's About page for any decision-critical channel.

### Gaps
- No side-by-side test of Tube Atlas numbers vs YouTube was published by anyone, so actual error rates are unknown. **Not measured.**
- The "Estimated Video Earnings" formula and RPM assumption are not documented on any public page I found.
- I could not confirm whether Tube Atlas reads exact or rounded subscriber counts.

The subscription tools are better for "which niche, which outlier format", and Tube Atlas is better for cheap bulk scraping of a niche you already chose.

## 4. Where Tube Atlas is best, and where free alternatives give the same answer

### Takeaway
For our use, roughly 70% of Tube Atlas's research output can be reproduced free (INFERRED estimate, not measured). The YouTube Data API v3 gives exact public stats and outlier math. YouTube search filters and autosuggest give keyword ideas. Social Blade gives channel history. Tube Atlas adds real value only in convenience and bulk: comment mining, tag and title n-grams across many videos, multi-country Trends, and transcripts in one grid.

### Cited Findings
- YouTube Data API v3 default quota: 10,000 units/day for most endpoints; `videos.list` and `channels.list` cost 1 unit each. As of the Oct 2026 docs, `search.list` has **its own bucket of 100 calls/day, 1 unit per call**, and each extra results page costs another call. — [YouTube quota calculator](https://developers.google.com/youtube/v3/determine_quota_cost), [API overview](https://developers.google.com/youtube/v3/getting-started)
- API clients must not store non-authorized statistics (e.g. a channel's subscriber count) for more than 30 days without refreshing or deleting them. — [YouTube API Developer Policies](https://developers.google.com/youtube/terms/developer-policies)
- Third-party revenue projections are allowed if clearly labelled as estimates. — [Derived-metrics policy](https://developers.google.com/youtube/terms/derived-metrics-policy)
- Social Blade gives public channel stats free, with earnings as a wide $0.25–$4 RPM range. — [Social Blade FAQ](https://socialblade.com/help/how-are-estimated-earnings-calculated), [Social Blade calculator](https://socialblade.com/youtube/calculator)
- Free tiers with outlier features exist: vidIQ Free (150 AI credits; Outliers/Most Viewed), 1of10 Free (60 credits), Subscribr Free (10 intel searches/mo), TubeMind and TubeSpace free plans. — [vidIQ plans](https://vidiq.com/plans/?refer=similarlabs_ai-writing_best-ai-script-writing-tools), [1of10](https://1of10.com/), [Subscribr](https://subscribr.ai/pricing), [TubeMind](https://tubemind.io/), [TubeSpace](https://tubespace.cloud/)
- Pay-per-use outlier scoring with no API key: an Apify actor scores each search result against its channel's recent median (~$3 per 1,000 scored videos). — [Apify zaiq/youtube-outlier-finder](https://apify.com/zaiq/youtube-outlier-finder)

### Inferences (decision table for CINE)
| Question | Free / existing route gives the same answer? | Use Tube Atlas? |
|---|---|---|
| Views, likes, comments, publish date for a list of videos | Yes. API `videos.list` (1 unit per 50 IDs) is exact and ToS-clean. | No |
| Outlier score (views ÷ channel median), views/day | Yes. API + `cf_research_stats.py` gives transparent math. | No (it has no score) |
| Channel age / date joined, subscriber count | Yes. API `channels.list` returns `publishedAt` and subscriber count. | No |
| Revenue / RPM of a niche | Nobody has real data. Social Blade range, NexLev/TubeLab niche RPM, or our own Studio after 7 days (analytics-loop). | No. Its estimate is undisclosed. |
| Autosuggest keyword tree | Partly. Manual typing in the YouTube search box, or vidIQ Free. | Yes, faster in bulk. |
| Comment mining for viewer questions (r03-comment-insight) | API `commentThreads.list` works but needs scripting. | Yes, convenient. |
| Tag frequency / title n-grams across 500+ videos | Scriptable from API data. | Yes, if we don't want to code it. |
| Multi-country Google/YouTube Trends | Google Trends website, manual. | Maybe; the vendor says the Trends tool is "restricted". |
| Finding brand-new breakout channels across all of YouTube | No. Needs a crawled index (TubeLab, 1of10, NexLev, TubeMind). | No, it can't do this. |

- Under the 100 search-calls/day limit, a free API scanner needs seed lists of channels or video IDs rather than heavy keyword searching. This is where a scraper-based tool like Tube Atlas, or a crawled database, saves effort (INFERRED).

### Gaps
- Whether Tube Atlas's "browse & extract" really returns more videos per query than the API's 100 searches/day was **not measured**.

## 5. Risks: account/ToS, scraped data, lock-in

### Takeaway
Tube Atlas works by **automated extraction from YouTube and Google web pages**. YouTube's Terms of Service forbid accessing the service "using any automated means (such as robots, botnets or scrapers)" except for search engines or with written permission. It also bundles tools whose intended use (spinning competitor transcripts, downloading videos, harvesting emails) conflicts with CineFactory's hard rules. The money risk is small, but the compliance and operational risks are real. Lock-in risk is low: data exports to CSV and there is no subscription.

### Cited Findings
- YouTube ToS: users may not "access the Service using any automated means (such as robots, botnets or scrapers) except (a) in the case of public search engines, in accordance with YouTube's robots.txt file; or (b) with YouTube's prior written permission". — [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/static?template=terms)
- YouTube API policies also ban API clients from scraping or obtaining scraped YouTube data. — [YouTube API Developer Policies, updated Nov 2026](https://developers.google.com/youtube/terms/developer-policies)
- Tube Atlas advertises "Extract Unlisted Videos", a "Video Downloader", "Convert Video to Text & Spin… make it unique", "Emails in Description" and "Phone Numbers in Description" columns, and a "Save emails to CSV" option (v1.0.4.1). — [tubeatlas.com](https://tubeatlas.com/), [AppBreed changelog](https://appbreed.com/apps/api/app-download.php?aid=26)
- The changelog shows rate-limit friction: "too many requests" warnings (v1.0.4.1, Jun 2023) and YouTube's new "too many requests" limit (v1.0.6.6, Apr 2026). It also shows Google restricting Trends extraction (v1.0.6.0). — [AppBreed changelog](https://appbreed.com/apps/api/app-download.php?aid=26), [SaaSPic](https://saaspic.com/tools/tube-atlas)
- The trends tool shipped with proxy support and "free proxy extraction" in v1.0.0.1 (Aug 2021); proxies were removed in v1.0.1.0 (May 2022). — [AppBreed changelog](https://appbreed.com/apps/api/app-download.php?aid=26)
- SaaSPic: the spinner and transcript tools "invite reusing other creators' content, which can breach copyright and YouTube's rules"; contact extraction "raises privacy and consent questions". — [SaaSPic](https://saaspic.com/tools/tube-atlas)
- Lock-in: free updates only for "major version 1". The license normally covers 1 computer, and the promotion includes 10. — [SaaSPic](https://saaspic.com/tools/tube-atlas), [tubeatlas.com](https://tubeatlas.com/)

### Inferences
- **Account risk (low to moderate, INFERRED):** the app runs from our IP, not through our YouTube login. The realistic consequence is IP rate-limiting or CAPTCHAs, not a channel strike. Even so, don't run heavy extraction from the same machine and IP that manages the owner's channels during uploads. Never log the owner's YouTube account into the app's built-in browser.
- **Policy conflict with CLAUDE.md hard rules:** "no downloading third-party videos" and "Original IP only: no competitor… scripts" mean the Downloader and the transcript-plus-spinner workflow must stay **off** for CINE. Read-only metadata research (counts, titles, tags, comment themes) remains compatible with our Research policy. The email/phone extraction should not be used either (privacy; we do no outreach).
- **Scraping fragility = data-quality risk:** after any YouTube front-end change, columns can silently come back empty or wrong until the next patch, and patches are slowing (2 in 2026). Every Tube Atlas export used for a decision should carry the app version and extraction date, and a 5-row spot-check against youtube.com (OBSERVED evidence for the decision log).
- **Vendor-continuity risk:** a one-person, family-run desktop vendor with slowing updates. If it stops patching, the tool degrades without notice, but there is no lock-in because CSV exports stay ours.
- **Malware risk:** cracked builds circulate. Install only the official AppBreed download.

### Gaps
- No public report of any user's IP being blocked or account being actioned because of Tube Atlas was found.
- AppBreed's own Terms and privacy policy (appbreed.com/terms-and-conditions) were not reviewed in this pass.
