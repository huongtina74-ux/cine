# How real users and creators use Tube Atlas: tutorials, walkthroughs and practical workflows (as of 13 Nov 2026)

> **Read this first: what the tool is (and what it is not).**
> "Tube Atlas" at tubeatlas.com is a **one-time-purchase Windows/Mac desktop app** made by AppBreed (developer Dave Guindon). It launched on **23 May 2022**. It is a scraper/keyword/data-grid tool (keyword generator, trends, video and channel analyzers, browse-and-extract, comments, tags, titles, transcripts, downloader). It is **not** a cloud "outlier finder / young-channel finder / faceless niche database" like NexLev, TubeLab, 1of10, OutlierKit, TubeMind or NicheRoza. I found **no other product called TubeAtlas, Tube-Atlas or tubeatlas.ai/io** in Nov 2026 searches. Most of what exists about it is **affiliate launch content from May 2022**. There are a few later items: a re-upload around 2024, a 2023 affiliate video, a Vietnamese video from Nov 2025, two affiliate articles from Sept 2025, and one scored review with an affiliate disclosure from Nov 2026. I found **no independent Reddit, X, Skool or Discord discussion** of Tube Atlas.
> Evidence labels used below: OBSERVED = the source states it; INFERRED = my reasoning; "not measured" = no source found.

## Q2. Common workflows (outliers, young fast-growing channels, low-competition niches, high-RPM niches, Shorts, validation, competitor spying, ideas/titles, content plan)

### Takeaway
Documented Tube Atlas workflows follow one pipeline: **seed keyword → Keyword Generator (autosuggest plus volume) → Trends → Video search or Browse & Extract → sort or filter the grid (views/day, estimated earnings, publish age) → Channel Analyzer (sort a channel's videos by views/day) → Comment, Title and Tag analyzers for ideas and titles → save or export.** The tool has no built-in outlier score, channel-age filter, faceless detector or niche RPM table. "Outlier" and "young channel" research has to be approximated by sorting and filtering the data columns.

### Cited Findings
- **Core pipeline (vendor design).** Each tool fills a data grid "you can filter and send to the next tool", moving from keyword ideas to trends, then into videos, channels and comments. The apps "share a design: search or browse, extract the data into a grid, then filter, analyze and export it." — [SaaSPic](https://saaspic.com/tools/tube-atlas)
- **Keyword step.**
  1. Enter a seed and click Generate to get hundreds of YouTube autosuggest phrases. — [Vidsilo keyword video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TKpGsi4pXfo)
  2. Add search volume, CPC and competition "from free sources or a paid Keywords Everywhere account". — [SaaSPic](https://saaspic.com/tools/tube-atlas)
  3. The 2022 review says volume came from Storybase (free) or a Google AdSense account. — [imnights](https://imnights.com/tube-atlas-review)
- **Trends step.** Add the keywords from the Keyword Generator. Trends gives a search-volume index with mini charts for 258 countries across YouTube, Google, Image, News and Shopping search. — [SaaSPic](https://saaspic.com/tools/tube-atlas); [imnights](https://imnights.com/tube-atlas-review)
- **Video discovery.**
  1. Enter a seed in Video Analyzer, keep the defaults and click the red Search button. — [imnights](https://imnights.com/tube-atlas-review)
  2. If you don't know what to search, the "Trending Videos" button shows what is performing now. — [tubeatlas.com](https://tubeatlas.com/)
- **Browse & Extract (the "killer feature" per reviewers).** You surf YouTube inside the app "using their powerful filters" and extract data for every video you see. A beta tester said a search returns only one results page "until I saw how the browser extractor thing worked… extracting data for over 1000 videos in just minutes." — [tubeatlas.com](https://tubeatlas.com/); [Medium](https://medium.com/@earnmoneyonlinefast/youtube-success-isnt-about-luck-it-s-about-intelligence-92188ecfc2de)
- **Competitor / channel spying.**
  1. Pick a seed for your niche.
  2. Find related channels.
  3. Extract a channel's videos.
  4. Sort by views, average views per day or estimated earnings.

  — [imnights](https://imnights.com/tube-atlas-review). The channel detail shows date joined, country, keywords and external links. — [IM Toolkit demo ~1:57:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAiYwVGZ6JI)
- **"Outlier inside a channel" (closest documented equivalent).** Sort one channel's videos by "most views per day". The demo contrasts a 1-day-old video at 228/day with an 800-day-old evergreen video at 69/day. — [Vidsilo ~1:54:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Vt8ktMezDJ4)
- **Small-channel signal tip.** "Look for smaller channels with recent videos that are getting strong engagement", which may point to popular keywords. — [imnights](https://imnights.com/tube-atlas-review)
- **Shorts.** The vendor claims you can "pull back data for UNLIMITED number of Youtube Short videos" and "browse Youtube Shorts and reveal the top earning videos with a click". The bonus includes an 83-page Shorts guide and 15 Shorts video tutorials. — [tubeatlas.com](https://tubeatlas.com/); [affiliate page](https://tubeatlas.com/affiliates). No practitioner walkthrough of Shorts niche-finding with Tube Atlas was found. The Medium reviewer says creators "focused only on shorts" should avoid it. — [Medium](https://medium.com/@earnmoneyonlinefast/youtube-success-isnt-about-luck-it-s-about-intelligence-92188ecfc2de)
- **Ideas and titles.**
  - Title Analyzer finds common word combinations.
  - Tag Analyzer counts tag frequency with average views and likes per tag.
  - The title generator offers 1,200+ templates.
  - Comment Analyzer can show only comments that contain questions or a chosen word combination.

  — [SaaSPic](https://saaspic.com/tools/tube-atlas). The Comment Analyzer is pitched to "uncover hidden niche ideas and identify user issues". — [imnights](https://imnights.com/tube-atlas-review)
- **Weekly content-plan workflow from one (affiliate) reviewer**, about 2 h per week:
  1. Weekly trend analysis (30 min): check Trends for emerging topics, "filter by 3–6 month growth patterns", and pick 3–5 opportunities.
  2. Competitor deep dive (45 min): run Channel Analyzer on your top 5 competitors, find their best videos from the last 90 days, and compare title, topic and timing patterns.
  3. Content optimization (20 min per video): Keyword Generator, then Video Analyzer on similar winners, then extract the tags and title patterns that work.
  4. Audience research (15 min per video): run Comment Analyzer on competitor videos to find questions and complaints.

  — [Medium, Sep 2025](https://medium.com/@earnmoneyonlinefast/youtube-success-isnt-about-luck-it-s-about-intelligence-92188ecfc2de)
- **Self-reported speed tests (same affiliate author, unverified):**
  - 25 trending videos in 8 min, against 10 videos in 45 min manually.
  - 15 competitor channels in 22 min, against 5 in 2 h.
  - 2,847 comments in 3 min.

  — [Medium](https://medium.com/@earnmoneyonlinefast/youtube-success-isnt-about-luck-it-s-about-intelligence-92188ecfc2de)
- **High-RPM / earnings angle.** Every video row has "Estimated Video Earnings" and "Estimated Earnings per Day". Channel rows have estimated total earnings and average earnings per video. — [tubeatlas.com](https://tubeatlas.com/); [Marketing Island ~5:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aFs7HlShjJg)
- **Contrast with what dedicated tools offer (not Tube Atlas).** TubeMind presets include:
  - "Recent channels: created in the last 6 months, uploading in the last 30 days, under 25K subscribers"
  - "Rising channels: under a year old, 100K+ views on uploads from the last 30 days"
  - "Breakouts… under 250K subscribers with at least one video at 5× their median"
  - "RPM of $4+"

  — [TubeMind discover](https://tubemind.io/discover). SaaSPic notes Tube Atlas "does not claim outlier-video detection, which is a headline feature of Subscribr and 1of10." — [SaaSPic](https://saaspic.com/tools/tube-atlas)

### Inferences
- A beginner can approximate "young channels growing fast" in Tube Atlas (INFERRED; no source demonstrates it):
  1. Run Browse & Extract on YouTube search filtered to "This month/This year".
  2. Add a custom filter on Total Channel Subscribers (e.g. < 10K) with high Average Views per Day.
  3. Open the candidate channels in the Channels tool and check "date joined".
  4. Sort each channel's videos by views/day.
- An outlier multiple (views ÷ channel median) is not a column. You would have to export CSV and compute it outside the tool, for example with `tools/cf_research_stats.py` in this repo (INFERRED).
- The earnings columns are not niche RPM. They appear to apply a generic rate to views (INFERRED from "not 100% perfect" and SaaSPic's "estimates"). They should not be used to rank niches by RPM.

### Gaps
- No source documents a Shorts-specific niche workflow in Tube Atlas (filters, a vertical-video flag or a Shorts column). Whether the video grid distinguishes Shorts from long-form is **not measured**.
- No source shows how the Estimated Earnings figure is calculated (the RPM assumption, and whether it varies by country or category).
- No source documents a "validate a niche" checklist built specifically on Tube Atlas.

## Q3. Which filter settings and thresholds do practitioners recommend?

### Takeaway
**None of the Tube Atlas sources recommend numeric thresholds** (channel age, subscriber cap, minimum views). The only filter guidance is mechanical: column quick-filters, custom filters, saved filter files, and, for ad placement, "monetized videos, then most daily recent views". Commonly quoted thresholds such as "<6 months old, <25K subs" come from other tools' presets, not from Tube Atlas users.

### Cited Findings
- Filters available: "quick-filters at the top of the columns", custom filters on any column, and saved filter files. TIP: "Save your filters to a custom filter file to instantly uncover the best videos even FASTER!" — [tubeatlas.com](https://tubeatlas.com/)
- The one vendor-stated filter recipe is for ad placement (finding videos to place Google Ads on): "see the videos that allow monetization, then filter your results by the most daily RECENT views". — [tubeatlas.com](https://tubeatlas.com/)
- Filterable columns relevant to niche research (from the vendor's 35-column list): Total Video Views, Total Likes, Total Comments, Video Length in Seconds, Date Published, Publish Age in Days, Average Views per Day, Estimated Video Earnings, Estimated Earnings per Day, Category, Subtitles, Creative Commons License, Ads Found, Total Channel Subscribers, Tag Count and Tag Keywords. — [tubeatlas.com](https://tubeatlas.com/)
- There is a dedicated help video on filters: V24 "How to use the column quick filters and custom filters". Columns can be configured (V39). — [AppBreed help](https://appbreed.com/help-videos-tube-atlas)
- LearnWire's tutorial has a "Filtering and exporting data" chapter from 9:40 to 14:11. Its content was not extracted. — [LearnWire](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bQDZ2T7jphg)
- The Medium reviewer suggests filtering trends by "3–6 month growth patterns" and looking at competitors' best videos "from the last 90 days". These are time windows, not thresholds. — [Medium](https://medium.com/@earnmoneyonlinefast/youtube-success-isnt-about-luck-it-s-about-intelligence-92188ecfc2de)
- For comparison only (other tools): TubeMind "Recent channels" = created ≤ 6 months ago, uploaded within 30 days, < 25K subs; "Rising" = < 1 year old, 100K+ views on last-30-day uploads; "Breakouts" = < 250K subs with a video ≥ 5× the channel median; high RPM = est. $4+. — [TubeMind](https://tubemind.io/discover)

### Inferences
- If the owner adopts Tube Atlas, starting thresholds have to be OWNER decisions or borrowed from other tools' presets and labelled as such (INFERRED). Possible starting values: Publish Age ≤ 180 days, Total Channel Subscribers ≤ 25K, Average Views per Day ≥ some multiple of the niche median. Any such threshold should be benchmarked against the niche median, as the repo's research policy requires (INFERRED).

### Gaps
- No practitioner-recommended numeric thresholds for Tube Atlas were found anywhere: not on YouTube, in blogs, on Reddit or on X.

## Q4. Tips, tricks, hidden features, common mistakes, and what users say the tool misses

### Takeaway
Under-explained but useful features:
- Browse & Extract, to get past the one-page search limit.
- Saved filter files.
- Related-video and unlisted-video extraction.
- "Trending Videos" button.
- Channel "date joined" in the detail pane.
- Tag Analyzer with average views per tag.
- Comment filters for questions or word combinations.
- CSV export.

The main limitations:
- Data comes from scraping and breaks when YouTube changes (rate limits; only two releases in 2026, the last in April).
- Trends is restricted.
- Earnings are rough estimates.
- No outlier score, channel-age filter, faceless detection or RPM by niche.
- Desktop only.
- Several headline features (spinner, "unlimited legal content", downloader, email/phone extraction) carry copyright, reuse-policy and privacy risk.

### Cited Findings
**Tips and hidden features**
- Browse & Extract gets past the "only 1 page of results" limit of plain search and lets you pull 1,000+ videos in minutes (beta-tester testimonial chosen by the vendor). — [tubeatlas.com](https://tubeatlas.com/)
- You can extract related videos, video ads inside videos and unlisted videos (help videos V31–V33). There is also an ad-free built-in player and column configuration. — [tubeatlas.com](https://tubeatlas.com/); [AppBreed help](https://appbreed.com/help-videos-tube-atlas)
- The Tag Analyzer shows tag occurrences, percentages and average views and likes per tag. — [SaaSPic](https://saaspic.com/tools/tube-atlas). The vendor demo assigns a dollar "worth" to a tag (e.g. "this particular tag is worth 32 000"). — [Vidsilo ~1:03:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Vt8ktMezDJ4)
- Save projects in the Tube Atlas format or as CSV. The tool remembers the last save folder. — [Vidsilo keyword video ~10:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TKpGsi4pXfo)
- Multiple YouTube logins are supported (V29), and keywords can be imported (V40). — [AppBreed help](https://appbreed.com/help-videos-tube-atlas)
- The Trends tool may need free or paid proxies for big batches. — [imnights](https://imnights.com/tube-atlas-review)
- Don't ignore smaller-volume keywords, because they can signal buyer intent. — [imnights](https://imnights.com/tube-atlas-review)

**Limitations and what reviewers say it misses**
- It "depends on extracting data from YouTube and Google, so tools can stall when those services change". "Trends is restricted" because Google has tightened limits on fast data extraction. There were "only two releases in 2026, none since April". — [SaaSPic, Nov 2026](https://saaspic.com/tools/tube-atlas)
- Version history:
  - v1.0.6.6, 12 Apr 2026: "improved video extraction to deal with YouTube's new 'too many requests' limit".
  - 5 Jan 2026: fixed AI punctuation.
  - Late 2025: fixed Video-to-Text and the player after YouTube changes, and switched the Trending Videos source.

  — [SaaSPic](https://saaspic.com/tools/tube-atlas)
- The earnings estimates "aren't perfectly accurate (how could they be?)" — [Medium](https://medium.com/@earnmoneyonlinefast/youtube-success-isnt-about-luck-it-s-about-intelligence-92188ecfc2de); "not going to be 100% perfect" — [Marketing Island](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aFs7HlShjJg)
- "Can feel overwhelming at first" — [Differ](https://differ.blog/p/honest-tube-atlas-review-12-tools-that-changed-everything-8ed24c). Other reviewer complaints: a learning curve, no live data ("you need to run fresh searches"), regional limits on trend data, and "Windows-optimized" despite Mac support. — [Medium](https://medium.com/@earnmoneyonlinefast/youtube-success-isnt-about-luck-it-s-about-intelligence-92188ecfc2de)
- It does not help with your own channel's analytics inside YouTube Studio. It is desktop only, with no cloud sharing, mobile or Chromebook support. — [SaaSPic](https://saaspic.com/tools/tube-atlas)
- No free plan or trial. Keywords Everywhere data needs a separate paid account. — [SaaSPic](https://saaspic.com/tools/tube-atlas)
- Pricing pressure: a $67 list price ($47 with coupon TUBE20), plus upsells (a $47 translator and rank tracker, and a $97 bundle). — [tubeatlas.com](https://tubeatlas.com/); [Differ](https://differ.blog/p/honest-tube-atlas-review-12-tools-that-changed-everything-8ed24c)

**Common mistakes and risks**
- Email and phone extraction "raises privacy and consent concerns". The spinner and transcript tools "can encourage reuse of others' content, with copyright and platform-rule risks". — [SaaSPic](https://saaspic.com/tools/tube-atlas)
- One affiliate reviewer describes pulling "an entire competitor's video script… spun it, and used it as research notes". — [Differ](https://differ.blog/p/honest-tube-atlas-review-12-tools-that-changed-everything-8ed24c). The vendor's bonus list even includes "Copy & Paste Videos And Earn $217 Per Day". — [tubeatlas.com](https://tubeatlas.com/)
- The trap is reading "Estimated Earnings" as truth, or as niche RPM (INFERRED from the reviewers' accuracy caveats above).

### Inferences
