## 1. Which product is "Tube Atlas"? URL, maker, launch, positioning

### Takeaway
"Tube Atlas" is a one-time-purchase **Windows/Mac desktop app** at **tubeatlas.com**. It is sold by **AppBreed Software**, a brand of **Innantech Industries Inc.** (British Columbia, Canada), and written by developer **Dave Guindon**. Version 1.0.0.0 shipped on **22 Aug 2021**, and the public affiliate launch was around **May 2022**. It is **not** a cloud channel database or an outlier finder. It is an on-demand **scraper/extractor**: it pulls YouTube autosuggest keywords, Google/YouTube Trends, video, channel, comment, tag and title data into local, filterable grids. Two other unrelated projects have similar names (listed below).

## 3. Data: sources, size, refresh, geography, Shorts, revenue estimates, accuracy

### Takeaway
Tube Atlas has **no database of its own**. Every number is **extracted live, on demand, from your own computer**. Sources are YouTube autosuggest, YouTube search and pages loaded in a **built-in web browser**, Google search, Google Trends, and optional third-party keyword-volume sources. Data is therefore as fresh as the moment you run the extraction, and limited to what you search or browse. "Estimated Video Earnings" and "Estimated Earnings per Day" columns exist, but **the formula or CPM/RPM assumption is not published** anywhere I could find. Treat them as rough guesses. The changelog shows extraction **breaking often when YouTube or Google change things**. The Trends tool is **still "restricted"** by Google rate-limiting, and a YouTube "too many requests" limit was being worked around in April 2026.

### Inferences
- [INFERRED] Tube Atlas gets its data mainly by **scraping YouTube and Google web pages** through an embedded browser and HTTP requests, not through the official YouTube Data API. Evidence: the many fixes "due to changes on youtube" in page markup, the "too many requests" throttling, GDPR consent-wall breakages, the former proxy support, and no API-key setup for YouTube. This drives every accuracy and reliability judgement below.
- [INFERRED] "Database size" and "refresh frequency" do not apply. You only see what you extract, and it is a snapshot from that moment. To track a niche's median over time, the studio has to re-run extractions and keep its own files. Saved data files and HTML/CSV exports exist (help videos 22–23 and "Save emails to CSV or TXT").
- [INFERRED] Country/language coverage is whatever YouTube serves to your IP and locale, plus a country dropdown in the Keywords and Trends tools. Several fixes for "users located in Europe" suggest results and reliability differ by user location. Vietnam-based use was not tested.
- [INFERRED] Earnings columns are almost certainly views × a fixed or category CPM-style constant. Real RPM varies a lot by audience country, niche, format (Shorts versus long-form), made-for-kids status and ad suitability, so per-video "earnings" can be off by large factors. Use these columns only to rank videos in the same niche against each other, never as revenue forecasts. They are not measured.
- [INFERRED] "Average Views per Day" (views ÷ publish age) is a deterministic calculation from public fields and the most trustworthy derived metric. It is still an average and hides early spikes.

### Gaps
- The exact estimated-earnings formula and CPM/RPM assumption are not published. The help video presumably explains it, but its content is not text-accessible. Answering this needs someone to watch help video #27 or to see it in the app.
- Whether "Search Volume Index" is Google Trends' 0–100 relative index (very likely, given the wording) or absolute volume is not stated in vendor text.
- Accuracy has not been independently tested. No source compared Tube Atlas numbers against YouTube Studio or other tools.

## 4. Account requirements: sign-up, OAuth, extension, API, seats

### Takeaway
You buy through **PayKickstart** with an email address. You **download the installer from AppBreed and activate it with that purchase email**, then manage up to 10 installs. **No YouTube OAuth or channel connection is required**, though you can optionally add YouTube logins inside the built-in browser. ### Inferences
