# Brand-new YouTube Shorts channels that exploded (2025–Nov 2026): case studies, formats, platform response, money

Research date: 2026-11-13. Every number is labelled by source. Tags: **[3P-TRACKER]** = third-party estimate from vidIQ, Social Blade or CreatorDB (modelled or rounded, not YouTube-reported). **[VENDOR]** = the source sells AI-video or MCN services and has a commercial interest, so treat with caution. **[PRIMARY]** = YouTube's own blog or Help Center. Channel handles are copied exactly as they appear in the cited pages so they can be checked later with the YouTube Data API.

## Q1. Which named new channels hit 100M–1B+ views within their first weeks? Exact numbers, dates and sources

### Takeaway
No top-tier outlet (Guardian, Verge, TechCrunch, BI, NYT) documented a brand-new channel aimed at a US or EU audience that reached 1B YouTube views within weeks in Sep–Nov 2026. The documented "explosions" fall into four groups:
- **(a) Reactivated or pivoted channels.** SASHA SHOW went from about 24K subscribers in Dec 2025 to 17.6M in Nov 2026, with 637M views in October 2026 alone.
- **(b) Syndicated or licensed content farms.** Grandma Dong gained 1.18M subscribers in 25 days.
- **(c) AI micro-drama series that were mainly a TikTok phenomenon.** Fruit Love Island got about 300M views in a few weeks in March 2026.
- **(d) 2025-era AI-slop channels.** These reached 1–2B lifetime views within months. Several were later deleted.

### Inferences
- The "billions in weeks" stories are mostly from 2025, the peak window after Veo 3 and Grok Imagine. They come from Asia or LatAm-registered channels whose audiences are global, and many targeted children.
- In 2026 the documented record-breakers are older or pivoted channels (SASHA SHOW, Lucan Pevidor) or industrial farms (Grandma Dong, His/Her Story). Genuinely new AI-only channels mostly reach hundreds of thousands to low millions of views per Short, not billions.
- Fruit Love Island shows a serialized AI micro-drama with a familiar IP *parody* format (Love Island) can reach about 300M views in about 10 days. But it was TikTok-centric, its pay was minimal, and its audience decayed fast in season 2.
- English titles plus visual, language-free setups (challenge, prank, ASMR, AI baby, animal) are the common thread for reaching US and EU audiences from non-English operators.

## Q2. What formats dominate these explosions in US/EU now (as of Nov 2026)?

### Takeaway
The dominant formats are:
- AI-generated cute subjects: babies, animals, talking fruit.
- Serialized AI micro-dramas (fruit and object dramas, "His Story" dubbed POV stories).
- Language-free challenge and prank sketches with English titles.
- Syndicated or licensed clip farms posting 5–26 times a day.
- AI ASMR and satisfying loops.
- Italian brainrot and Roblox-style kids animation.

Commerce-led Shorts (YouTube Shopping affiliate) are growing, but they are a monetization layer, not a view-explosion format.

## Q3. How did YouTube respond, and were fast channels demonetized or terminated?

### Takeaway
YouTube's response escalated in steps:
- July 2025: renamed "repetitious" to "inauthentic content".
- Nov 2025: added mass AI uploads to its spam and termination rules.
- Jan 2026: purged top slop channels.
- May 2026: auto AI labels.
- 16 July 2026: split inauthentic content into three non-monetizable buckets.
- August 2026: doubled YPP entry thresholds and set a 10M-view floor for Shorts pay, from 1 Feb 2027.
- 1 Oct 2026: Shorts feed demotes re-upload channels.

Fast slop channels were deleted (the Kapwing #1 most-subscribed and two of the top 5), and TikTok cut Fruit Love Island from monetization.

### Cited Findings
- **15 July 2025:** YouTube renamed the "repetitious content" policy to "inauthentic content". — [PPC Land timeline](https://ppc.land/googles-safe-uses-4-ai-agents-to-investigate-youtube-slop-networks); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/technology/youtube-updates-monetisation-policies-ai-and-repetitive-content-ban-begins-july-15-1.500192660)
- **Aug 2025:** after the Guardian's queries, YouTube removed 3 fast-growing AI channels and blocked 2 more from monetization, without naming them. — [Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2025/aug/11/cat-soap-operas-and-babies-trapped-in-space-the-ai-slop-taking-over-youtube); [Orbital Today](https://orbitaltoday.com/2025/08/12/from-space-stranded-baby-to-killer-cats-the-ai-video-boom-flooding-youtube)
- **13 Nov 2025:** YouTube listed mass uploading of auto-generated or low-value content as a practice behind channel terminations. — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/googles-safe-uses-4-ai-agents-to-investigate-youtube-slop-networks)

  - Removed channels include @CuentosFacianantes (5.9M subscribers, 1.2B+ views) and Imperio de Jesus (5.8M subscribers).

  - Labels moved below the player on long videos and onto an overlay on Shorts.

  3. AI personas discussing sensitive topics (health, finance, legal).

### Inferences
- A brand-new 2026 channel that scales on AI babies or animals with one template is precisely what buckets 1 and 2 and the cluster-termination research target. Expect monetization denial at YPP review, or cluster termination if the operator runs multiple near-identical channels.
- The risk is larger for operators running "franchise" twin channels, the pattern CreatorDB found.

### Gaps
- No public evidence that SASHA SHOW, Lucan Pevidor or Grandma Dong faced policy action.
- The removal reasons for the January 2026 channels were never stated by YouTube; spam, inauthentic content and kids-safety are all possible.
- The primary YouTube Help text for the July 2026 three-bucket policy was not fetched directly; it is covered via TechCrunch, Dexerto and Creator Insider.

## Q4. How much money do such channels make (Shorts RPM US/EU, YPP timing, affiliate)?

### Takeaway
Shorts ad pay is small:
- About $0.33 per 1,000 views for US audiences, and about $0.07–$0.20 in most niches.
- That is roughly 3–14% of long-form RPM.
- 600M monthly views therefore pays only about $20K–$60K.

Kapwing's multi-million estimates for slop channels are Social Blade midpoints, not reported income. From 1 Feb 2027, new channels need 20M Shorts views in 90 days to join YPP, and channels need 10M qualified Shorts views in 90 days to keep receiving Shorts pool pay. Commerce and affiliate income are the stronger levers.

### Cited Findings
- Shorts RPM was 3–14% of long-form RPM in almost every niche (274 channels, YouTube Analytics API). US audiences earned $0.328 per 1,000 Shorts views. Most niches earned $0.07–$0.20 per 1,000 (engaged views). — [AIR Media-Tech, Sep 2026 [VENDOR/MCN]](https://air.io/en/air-data-findings/youtube-shorts-rpm-vs-long-form-how-much-do-shorts-earn-in-2026); [Mubert summary](https://mubert.com/blog/youtube-shorts-views-count-pay); [revid.ai [VENDOR]](https://www.revid.ai/blog/youtube-shorts-automation)
- Since 31 Mar 2025, a Shorts view counts on every start or replay. On one channel, 20.8M views were 9.5M engaged views (45.7%). — [MilX [VENDOR]](https://milx.app/en/cases/how-much-do-youtube-shorts-pay-to-successful-creators)
- Example: SASHA SHOW had about 640M views in 28 days, yet vidIQ models only about $17K in ad revenue. — [vidIQ [3P-TRACKER]](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/@sashashowbro/about/)
- Kapwing's estimates (Social Blade midpoints): Bandar Apna Dost about $4.25M a year; Three Minutes Wisdom about $4.04M. All 278 slop channels together are about $117M a year from 63B views and 221M subscribers. These are modelled, not reported. — [Kapwing](https://www.kapwing.com/blog/ai-slop-report-the-global-rise-of-low-quality-ai-videos/); [Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2025/dec/27/more-than-20-of-videos-shown-to-new-youtube-users-are-ai-slop-study-finds)
- **YPP changes (announced 10 Aug 2026, effective 1 Feb 2027):**

### Inferences
- A brand-new channel that reaches 100M Shorts views in its first weeks would earn on the order of $7K–$33K from the pool, at the US $0.33 RPM upper bound and only after YPP admission. This is an inferred calculation from the AIR RPMs.
- After 1 Feb 2027, entry needs 20M Shorts views in 90 days. That is easy for a viral channel, but it collides with YPP's human review against the July 2026 buckets.
- For an original-IP studio, Shorts are an acquisition funnel; revenue sits in long-form (55% share, 10–30x RPM), Shopping and brand deals.
