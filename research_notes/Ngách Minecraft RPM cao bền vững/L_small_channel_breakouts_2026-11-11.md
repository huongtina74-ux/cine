# Small and new channel breakouts - what niche and which characters (2026-11-11)

**Data (OBSERVED 2026-11-11 11:25Z):** official YouTube Data API run of `cf_mc_api_dataset.py` on the owner PC
(US region, English, 84 searches, last 7 and 15 days, 9,130 units, key never printed). 2,886 videos, 1,938 channels,
uploads of 320 channels (11,025 rows). Raw data stays on the PC: `Downloads\cine_niche_survey\out\2026-11-11_api_dataset`.
Thumbnails of 116 breakout videos were looked at in the built-in browser. No video watched, no audio heard.

Definitions: small channel = under 100K subs. Breakout = 100K+ views (long) / 1M+ (Shorts) and views >= 5x (10x) subs.
New channel = created <= 365 days ago.

## 1. Where new channels actually break in
- 570 channels in the sample are <= 1 year old; 75 already have >= 100K subs.
  - 57 of 75 post mostly Shorts (>= 70% of last uploads).
  - 33 of 75 ride someone else's IP in titles (Maizen JJ/Mikey fan animation, Verity, K-pop Demon Hunters, Sonic, memes).
- Long-form breakouts from small channels (7-15 days): 61. By lane: SMP/PvP drama ~15, horror/scary mod ~10,
  Maizen fan animation ~8, 100/500-days survival builds 3, mob/animal animation 3.
- Lane table (search 7 days, long-form): `minecraft smp` 9 small-channel videos >= 100K, `hide and seek` 4,
  `speedrun` 4, `horror` 3, `100 days` 3, `movie` 3; `zombie apocalypse` 1 (median 20K), `survival movie full` 0 (median 4K),
  `family roleplay` 0, `poor vs rich animation` 0.

## 3. Character styles in breakouts
- Classic simple Minecraft skins or Mojang mobs (villager, zombie, axolotl, enderman) in almost all breakouts.
- Zero breakouts used elaborate anime-voxel AI heroes. "Realistic" outliers are horror creatures, not heroes.
- Mob-as-hero works: villager (Logetor 21.9M, Gau FE), baby enderman (4.3M), snow golem (Nyko 1.2M on 60.7K subs).

## 4. Timely hook
Minecraft 26.4 snapshots (since 2026-10-06) add the Frostbite zombie, Ice Caves, icicles, freezing. Release date not announced
(Beebom guesses ~mid-December). Early videos already pop: "Minecraft, But the World is Brittle Ice" 1.46M,
"Ice Caves in superflat?!" 818K.

## INFERRED conclusions
1. The family-sibling Minecraft animation demand is huge but mostly served by Maizen fan channels living on the
   "JJ Mikey" search keyword. An original family does not get that search traffic; it must win in Shorts/Browse feeds.
2. Long-form original story from a 0-sub channel is the hardest entry. The proven entry is Shorts mini-stories
   (60-120 s, wordless or near-wordless, one twist), then long-form "Full Movie" and 100-days episodes once a feed audience exists.
3. Our baby zombie Zu is the strongest asset: a Mojang mob silhouette with an original personality = the Logetor/villager model.
