## Q1. Which Minecraft sub-niches have the largest viewing volume right now?

### Takeaway
Long-form "story animation" is the largest measurable pool in our sample. Within it, the Maizen JJ & Mikey universe and its fan-animation clones dominate, together with crossovers using hot mascots (Verity, Poppy Playtime, Sprunki). Long-form gameplay/roleplay of the "Minecraft, But…", challenge and survival kind is a close second, with the highest single-video velocities. No reliable 2025–2026 public data was found for Shorts-only, parkour/background, lore, building, analog horror, nursery or Minecraft-movie content.

### Cited Findings
- **Minecraft overall.** Minecraft-related videos passed 1 trillion cumulative YouTube views in 2021. That was about 8 years to reach 500B and about 2 more years to reach 1T (OBSERVED, dated 2021). The formats YouTube itself lists as core are "100 Days", Animation, "Minecraft, But…", Parody, Role-Play, Speedrun, Survival Games and SMP, and it says Shorts "took hold" in 2021. — [YouTube Culture & Trends, "Minecraft trillion"](https://www.youtube.com/trends/articles/minecraft-trillion)
- **Animation has the longest track record.** It was the most consistently dominant category of popular Minecraft videos, with about 5B views in 2020 and again in 2021 (OBSERVED, article dated 2021-12-15). Many of the most-viewed animations are "not made in Minecraft" (for example a Herobrine monster-family short with 178M views). — [PC Gamer](https://pcgamer.com/minecraft-videos-now-have-1-trillion-views-on-youtube-but-the-most-popular-ones-arent-actually-made-in-minecraft)

### Inferences
- **Ranking by demand** (INFERRED, from median views of recent long videos in our sample):
  1. Maizen universe plus fan animation (channel medians 1.1M–1.9M).
  2. Gameplay/roleplay "Minecraft, But…", 24h/100-day challenges and myth-busting (single videos 2M–7M within 2 weeks).
  3. Mascot-crossover animation (medians 0.4M–0.9M, with 2M–4.6M outliers).
  4. "100 Days" (strong when attached to a big brand).
  5. Original-IP family/poor-rich/mob story animation (historically huge through BigSchool; current active medians are lower, for example 146K at Class Monster School).
  6. AI semi-realistic Steve & Alex "baby mob plus food" (small sample).
  7. Shorts, parkour/background, lore, building, analog horror, nursery and movie-related content: **not measured**.
- **Long videos are what pay** (INFERRED). In our sample the long-video median was 452K and the Shorts median was 0 (OBSERVED). This partly reflects how our sample was collected, so we cannot conclude that Shorts get no traffic.

### Gaps
- No 2025–2026 total-views-per-sub-niche data exists in public sources. YouTube's last public Minecraft breakdown is from 2021.
- Shorts-only Minecraft channels (very high view counts are common) were not sampled. Their RPM was not measured here.
- Minecraft parkour/"satisfying background" gameplay, lore/history, building tutorials, analog horror and nursery Minecraft: no reliable 2025–2026 numbers were found.
- Minecraft-movie content (*A Minecraft Movie*, April 2025): no source with YouTube view figures for creator content was found. Tubefilter's tag page covers only promotion and cameo news from March–April 2025. — [Tubefilter tag](https://www.tubefilter.com/tag/a-minecraft-movie/)

- Ratios over 100× (DL Minecraft about 195×) appear in channels with very few subs and only a few weeks of uploads (INFERRED). That pattern fits suggested-feed testing of a new channel, so a single ratio should not be read as a durable channel median.

### Gaps

- No external tracker (Social Blade, Playboard) was used to verify these channels, because the pages render dynamically or need a login.

## Q3. Which breakouts depend on third-party IP vs. original characters?

### Takeaway
Most observed small-channel breakouts (Cubeee, Mine Block, Морфиз, Ligabka, JJ Cartoons, ZomBo, Mikey Spikey, Teddy and Freddy) use **Maizen's JJ & Mikey characters**. Verity, Poppy Playtime and Sprunki channels (GameToons, MyMy toon, HB Toon) borrow third-party mascots. Only Iron Studio (original axolotl story) and, partly, DL Minecraft (Mojang default Steve/Alex plus baby mobs) are outside that dependency.

### Cited Findings
- In our outlier table, the words that repeat most in outlier titles were animation (38), minecraft (37), **maizen (16), mikey (16), verity (12)**, asmr (12), mukbang (11) and baby (9) (OBSERVED 2026-11-03). — [INT-STORY](/home/claude/cine/reports/research/2026-11-03_minecraft_story.md)
- We flagged HB Toon as a near-copy or reupload channel: its titles are "near verbatim" matches of MyMy toon's, for example "The RISE of LORD VERITY!" and "MISERY Just Wanted a COLOR..." (OBSERVED titles; the copy judgement is INFERRED). — [INT-STORY](/home/claude/cine/reports/research/2026-11-03_minecraft_story.md)
- Verity is a third-party creation by ThatMob (May 2026), and its mods are "officially supported" by the creator (OBSERVED). — [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/minecraft/verity-mod-viral-millions-downloads)
- Our IP scorecard rates "most leading channels use Maizen characters (copyright risk)" at 4/10 for MSToons, against 9/10 for original IP (INFERRED internal score). — [INT-SCORE](/home/claude/cine/reports/research/2026-11-03_level_scorecard.md)
- **Policy context:**
  - YouTube's 2025-07-15 enforcement update renamed "repetitious content" as **"inauthentic content"**. It targets mass-produced, templated and near-identical videos and reposts with little editing.
  - Compilations and reaction videos remain eligible "if you've added significant original commentary, modifications, or educational or entertainment value" (Rene Ritchie, YouTube; OBSERVED, published 2025-07-09, updated 2025-07-11).
  - AI tools are allowed, but realistic synthetic content must be disclosed.
  - Source: [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-monetization-update-spam-not-reaction-channels/550755/)

### Inferences
- **Main risk for third-party-IP channels** (INFERRED): copyright claims or strikes from the IP owner (Maizen, ThatMob, Mob Entertainment for Poppy Playtime). Monetization is a secondary risk under the "inauthentic content" policy if output is templated or near-copied, as with HB Toon.
- **Original-character channels** carry less of this tail risk, but our sample shows lower median traffic for them right now. Examples: Class Monster School median 146K; BigSchool's best videos are about 3 years old.
- The Steve & Alex "default character" route sits between the two (INFERRED). Mojang's characters appear in countless videos under Minecraft's usage guidelines. A semi-realistic AI look may still trigger synthetic-content disclosure and "made for kids" scrutiny (see Q5).

### Gaps
- No public record was found of Maizen enforcing (or not enforcing) copyright against fan-animation channels.
- Mojang's current commercial-usage guidelines were not re-read in this round.

## Q4. What drives breakout in each sub-niche?

### Takeaway
In story animation, breakout is driven by four things. The title names a known character, adds an extreme event and ends with "?!" or "...". The thumbnail shows two characters' faces with exaggerated emotion in saturated red and pink. Single episodes run 8–14 minutes, and 25–60 minute compilations follow. Winners are serialized quickly. Gameplay breakouts use "Minecraft, But…", "Surviving X for 24 Hours" and "Busting Myths" hooks at 12–30 minutes.

### Cited Findings
All OBSERVED 2026-11-03 from [INT-STORY](/home/claude/cine/reports/research/2026-11-03_minecraft_story.md):
- **Title pattern.** Character, then extreme event, then "?!" or "...". Examples: "Buried Under The Water", "EAT", "Became Spider-Man", "NOT a MONSTER...", "SAD ORIGIN STORY". It appears in 8 of the top 10 deep-dive videos.
- **Thumbnail pattern.** Two characters' faces in close-up with exaggerated emotion (heart eyes, panic, sweat), heart or "!?" graphics, and saturated red and pink. It appears in 6 of 10 thumbnails.
- **Length.**
  - MSToons: 30 of 30 recent videos run 9:34–14:19.
  - Maizen: median length 19.3 minutes, 21 long videos per 30 days.
  - GameToons alternates 8-minute episodes with 1-hour compilations.
  - Cubeee's 54:29 compilation reached 2.06M in about 6 days.
  - Sources: [INT-STORY](/home/claude/cine/reports/research/2026-11-03_minecraft_story.md); [INT-SCORE](/home/claude/cine/reports/research/2026-11-03_level_scorecard.md)
- **Upload frequency** (long videos per 30 days, OBSERVED): Maizen 21, GameToons 25, MyMy toon 29, MSToons 21. The small breakout channels post far less: Cubeee 3, Mine Block 4, Морфиз 8. — [INT-SCORE](/home/claude/cine/reports/research/2026-11-03_level_scorecard.md)
- **Serialization.** MSToons followed its 8.2M "JJ was Buried Under The Water" with "JJ BUS EATER was Buried Under The Water", which reached 1.28M in about 2 days.
- **Visual pacing** (OBSERVED, our own frame sampling):

  | Channel | Cuts per minute | Resolution |
  |---|---|---|
  | BigSchool | 39–45 | 1080p |
  | MSToons | 12–13 | 2160p |
  | Maizen | 7–13 | 1080p |

  Source: [INT-SCORE](/home/claude/cine/reports/research/2026-11-03_level_scorecard.md)
- **Gameplay hooks** (OBSERVED titles and lengths): "Minecraft, But You Can Combine Any Mobs!" (29:50), "Surviving in Deadly Pool for 24 Hours…" (12:10), "Busting Hidden SCARY MYTHS…" (13:55), "1,000,000 Villagers Simulate RICH vs POOR" (20:04).
- **Food and baby-mob warmth formula** (DL Minecraft): "[Steve & Alex] rescue or feed a [baby mob] [food] for the First Time!". Thumbnails are semi-realistic, with the food filling the frame.

### Inferences
- **Small channels do not win on volume** (INFERRED). Cubeee posts about 3 long videos a month yet has a 1.93M median. They win on borrowed search demand (the "MAIZEN" prefix in titles) plus emotional romance and family hooks.
- **Compilations are the cheapest watch-time lever** (INFERRED). A compilation of 5–6 proven episodes at 25–55 minutes was a top-velocity video at both large and small channels.
- **Shorts to long-video funnel: not evidenced.** None of the breakout channels in our sample showed a Shorts-led path. We did not check their Shorts tabs (gap).

### Gaps
- No CTR, retention or RPM data for any competitor. These are not public.
- No Shorts-tab analysis of the breakout channels.
- No measured link between upload frequency and breakout for small channels.

## Q5. Signals of saturation or declining traffic

### Takeaway
No Google Trends numbers for "minecraft animation" or "minecraft 100 days" were found in public articles, so trend direction is **not measured**. Indirect signals point to saturation in two places. Older original-IP story channels are fading (BigSchool has been dormant about 1 year; ZomBo and Class Monster School have low medians despite about 3M subs each). The Maizen and Verity wave is crowded with copy channels. Policy pressure on mass-produced, AI-made kids content is also rising.

### Cited Findings
- **Fading original-IP channels** (OBSERVED 2026-11-03; [INT-STORY](/home/claude/cine/reports/research/2026-11-03_minecraft_story.md)):
  - BigSchool (32.4M subs) has posted **no long video in about the last year**.
  - ZomBo (2.9M subs) has a median of 64K. Its outliers all use the "MAIZEN" prefix.
  - Class Monster School (2.96M subs) has a median of 146K.
- **Low barrier to entry and copy channels** (OBSERVED 2026-11-03; [INT-STORY](/home/claude/cine/reports/research/2026-11-03_minecraft_story.md)):
  - JJ Cartoons, 45 days old with 3.55K subs, already reached 281K on a Maizen-fan video.
  - HB Toon copies MyMy toon titles (copy judgement INFERRED).
  - Teddy and Freddy (107K subs, about 14 months old) has a best video of only 136K, so not every fan channel breaks out.
- **Trend dependence:**
  - Verity grew from a May 2026 video to millions of mod downloads by July 2026 (OBSERVED). — [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/minecraft/verity-mod-viral-millions-downloads)
  - By November 2026 it was the dominant mascot in outlier titles: "verity" appears 12 times (OBSERVED). — [INT-STORY](/home/claude/cine/reports/research/2026-11-03_minecraft_story.md)
- **Policy pressure on AI and mass-produced content:**
  - On 2026-04-01, a Fairplay-led coalition asked YouTube to ban AI-generated content from YouTube Kids, stop recommending AI content to under-18s, and remove financial incentives (OBSERVED).
  - YouTube says it limits AI content on YouTube Kids to "a small set of high-quality channels". Neal Mohan names "managing AI slop" a top priority (OBSERVED).
  - Fairplay claims the top AI kids-slop channels earn more than $4.25M a year (OBSERVED claim, unverified).
  - Source: [Fortune](https://www.fortune.com/2026/04/01/ai-slop-200-organizations-letter-youtube-google)
- **Inauthentic-content enforcement** from 2025-07-15 targets templated, near-identical, high-volume uploads (OBSERVED). — [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-monetization-update-spam-not-reaction-channels/550755/)

### Inferences
- **Mascot-crossover traffic** (Verity, Sprunki, Poppy Playtime) follows a hype cycle (INFERRED). Verity has been hot for about 6 months, so expect decay. Entering it now means chasing a peak.
- **Maizen-fan animation looks like a stable but crowded demand pool** (INFERRED). The parent channel still gets 1.6M medians, and new fan channels break out within weeks. It is closed to us because of the original-IP rule, and its legal and monetization tail risk is growing.
- **AI semi-realistic kids content** (the DL Minecraft style) carries the highest policy risk heading into 2027 (INFERRED), because of the April 2026 coalition pressure and YouTube's "AI slop" priority.
- **The original-IP story-animation slot that BigSchool left looks under-supplied** (INFERRED). Its classic videos still hold 7M+ views, and few active original channels fill the gap (Iron Studio's 420K is the visible exception). This is an opportunity signal, not proof of demand.

### Gaps
- Google Trends values (2023–2026) for "minecraft animation", "minecraft 100 days", "maizen" and "verity" were not retrieved. Use Trends directly in a browser at human pace in the next round.
- No public 2025–2026 data on Minecraft-wide YouTube view totals by year after 2021.
- Any decline in "100 Days" gameplay after its 2021 peak is anecdotal only. No source with numbers was found.
