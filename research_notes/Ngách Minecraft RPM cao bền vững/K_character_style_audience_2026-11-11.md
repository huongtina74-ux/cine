# Which character style does our audience watch? (2026-11-11)

**Method (OBSERVED 2026-11-11):** 8 YouTube searches (minecraft animation / story / movie / zombie apocalypse / war /
survival story / anime / family), filter = this month, sort = views, logged out, built-in browser on the owner PC.
95 unique videos. Each thumbnail was looked at (3 grids of 35) and labelled by the main character style.
Thumbnails only - no video viewed, no audio heard. Tally script: `K_character_style_tally.py`.

| Style | Videos | Views (sum) | Median | Share of Minecraft-story views |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A Classic simple Minecraft skin, big expressive face (Maizen, MSToons, Carry Depie, Karry Kraft, Cash, ProBoiz, MrAlpha, Anshu Bisht, AlphaExtra) | 48 | 184.6M | 2.71M | 69% |
| B Cute/anime-styled skin on the standard block body (Dagar, Nacho, Knoxy, Morfiz) | 13 | 46.5M | 3.38M | 17% |
| C Elaborate hero: glowing armour / detailed anime figure (Freshlol x2, Karl, Awita) | 4 | 11.1M | 2.33M | 4% |
| S 2D stick figures (Nobody Gets In, Beanie Survives, Kit vs The End, DoodleSurvivals...) - the zombie-outbreak lane itself | 8 | 13.4M | 1.19M | 5% |
| E Realistic Minecraft-world creature, no hero (LookOut, PaYuDan, NEFF447) | 4 | 11.8M | 3.37M | 4% |
| non-Minecraft / face-cam (excluded) | 18 | 172.4M | - | - |

INFERRED:
- 86% of Minecraft-story views go to the standard Minecraft body (A+B). Elaborate heroes are 4%, and all 4 are
  gameplay creators wearing in-game armour, not story mascots.
- The best median is B: the standard blocky body with a cute, anime-leaning face, hair and colour identity (Dagar 7 of the top 41).
- The zombie-outbreak lane's newest winners use the *simplest* characters of all (2D stick figures) - the story and the
  "Day N" device carry it, not character rendering.
- The owner's 16 AI sheets (anime-voxel heroes) are style C, plus they are 2D AI paintings that cannot be animated
  consistently in Minecraft/Blender without a custom rig, and AI-looking kids content is under public pressure
  ("AI slop" advocacy letters to YouTube, April 2026).

Recommendation (INFERRED, OWNER decides): keep the standard Minecraft 64x64 skin body (style A/B). Upgrade Bin/Bo/Zu's
*faces and identity* - bigger eyes and brows, 2-3 signature colours, one prop each, readable at 120 px - and use the AI
sheets only as mood/colour reference and for the side-quest "transform" moments (armour as an earned costume, like Dagar's
"I became the X king").
