## What a PASS means
Verifying a source crop is an exact subset of the original **does establish identical visible source pixels** (no model-driven drift in those pixels). It DOES NOT establish full-face/figure similarity of a newly generated character image, especially for previously unseen views, lip-sync, new poses or 10-second video. No numerical claim of full new-view identity matching is made.

## Promotion gate for any AI-generated asset
User selection of original art authorizes **SOURCE_IDENTITY_LOCKED** status; it does not authorize marking unrelated AI variants as approved. To mark a generated master or new view `APPROVED_FOR_REFERENCE`, require the actual PNG, SHA256, source ID, approved outfit/angle, human face/body/prop visual checks, date and approver. If any required part drifts, keep `REVIEW_REQUIRED`. CINE must fail closed rather than inventing identity.
