# Research and evidence

Use the selected CineFactory checkout. If required tools, documents or media are unavailable, report the missing dependency and complete only supported analysis; do not invent command results or schema fields. Exercises below are evaluation scenarios, not authorization to render, spend or publish.

Work from the selected CineFactory repository and its current operating model. Separate observation, inference and owner decisions.

## Method
For evidence-bearing production work, read [references/production.md](references/production.md). It maps this specialty to the executable packet checks, source/use limits and evaluation procedure. In CineFactory, available tools are `tools/cf_professional.py` and the existing stage SOPs.

1. State the decision the research should change, the target audience, sampling window and available access. Separate a source's popularity from evidence of usefulness.
2. Sample comparable formats and publication ages, including ordinary performers and failures. Compare outliers to their own channel baseline. Record selection bias when only winners are available.
3. For each claim record URL, author, access date, exact section or timestamp, inspected modality, confidence, contradiction and the decision it supports. A transcript supports spoken content; sparse stills do not establish continuous motion, cut rate or sound quality.
4. Turn a lesson into an original exercise: production problem → supported principle → artifact change → measurable observation → reviewer decision. Keep source teaching, studio inference and proposed experiment distinct.
5. Hand the writer an abstract format brief and evidence map, not competitor dialogue or story expression. Use existing niche research tools; report missing fields instead of filling plausible numbers.

## Deliverables and exercise
Produce a brief plus a claim/evidence table and a sample manifest. For course intake include access status, inspected portion, lesson version, test shot and a condition that would disprove the proposed rule.

Exercise: three high-view videos have unknown publication ages and no retention access. Deliver a provisional sampling plan; leave retention and causal explanations unknown. Pass when every factual claim has a source or an explicit unknown, and no visual observation is asserted from transcript alone.
