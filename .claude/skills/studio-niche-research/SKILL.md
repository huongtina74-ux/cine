# Competitor-First Research
Use CineFactory local MCP `studio_status` and `niche_production_brief`; consult exact published channels/video URLs. Require legal access to full frames AND full audio for a FULL_VIDEO_REVIEW claim. Record timecoded hook, story beats, shot lengths, camera, staging, character, animation, lighting, sound, editing, transitions and copyright risks. Source metadata, thumbnails and transcript alone are PARTIAL. Compare new breakout channels and mature winners plus weaker controls. Label findings OBSERVED, VENDOR-REPORTED, INFERRED or UNKNOWN; never assert production software, retention, CTR, RPM or commercial rights without actual evidence. Produce an original scene mechanics brief and open questions.
