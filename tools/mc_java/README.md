# Minecraft Java (vanilla 26.3, no mods) as the Bin Bo Zu stage

Measured 2026-11-05 on the owner's PC (RTX 3060). Everything here runs in the owner's licensed Java 26.3, with no mods and no downloads.

- `datapack_binbozu/` is copied into `%APPDATA%\.minecraft\saves\BinBoZu_Studio\datapacks\`.
  - pack.mcmeta uses `min_format/max_format 121`.
  - Functions:
    - `build`, `horde`, `sign`: the Block Mart supermarket set.
    - `cast`: Bin, Bo and Zu as `mannequin` entities whose `profile.texture` points at our resource pack skins.
    - `army` + `march` + `tick`: 120 armoured mannequins and 140 zombies marching. The walk animation comes for free from small per-tick teleports.
    - `trailer_build`, `trailer_actors`: a code-generated palace, plaza, houses and Nether field, plus 616 crowd mannequins, 300 zombified piglins and the cast.
    - `trailer_go` + `trailer_cam`: a scripted camera. An invisible marker armor stand is teleported every tick and the player spectates it. The client interpolates the movement, so the camera is smooth. Measured on a 6 s dolly: 0 of 179 frame pairs were static.
- Resource pack `BinBoZu` (format 97): skins at `assets/binbo/textures/entity/cast/{bin,bo,zu}.png`. They are generated by code (64×64 player layout) and must be enabled in Options → Resource Packs.
- Recording: ffmpeg `gdigrab -i title="Minecraft 26.3 - Singleplayer"` captures the game window only, without other windows on top. Encoder: h264_nvenc, 30 fps.
- Post: `trailer_mix.py` builds the score, SFX and Kokoro narration mix at -14 LUFS. ffmpeg handles the cut, crop, grade and titles.

Known issues:
- Command feedback ("Running function…") and the tutorial toast end up in the capture.
  - The capture is cropped to remove the toast.
  - The feedback line is delogo'd, which leaves a visible smear in shot 1.
  - Fix next time: set the tutorial step to none in options.txt, and trigger the camera with a delay.
- Lighting is vanilla. Shaders need Fabric + Iris, which needs owner approval.

## Palace v2 (2026-11-05): detail rules that fixed the "sparse" look
The generator was run inline through the Blender bridge. Its output is `datapack_binbozu/data/binbo/function/palace.mcfunction` on the PC, at about 4,200 commands. It applies standard build-craft rules:
- **Depth layers:** core wall → face plane → pilasters (+1) → sills, lintels and cornices (+1/+2). Windows sit one block back.
- **Trim vocabulary:** stairs (upside-down lintels), slabs (sills, cornices), chiseled caps, a keystone on every bay, a balustrade with lanterns.
- **Limited palette per surface:** quartz bricks, smooth quartz, pillar, chiseled. Roof and dome accents in waxed oxidized copper, highlights in gold.
- **Silhouette:** a central pavilion with a pediment, a drum plus a big dome and lantern, corner towers with domes, and lower wings with end domes.
- **Landscape:** a raised platform with a pillar retaining wall, a grand stair, a parterre with hedges, flower beds and cone trees, two fountains, lamp posts and tree avenues.

Still missing compared with the Unstable SMP builds: terrain shaping (mountains), a city fabric of varied houses, curved walls and interiors.

## City v2 + mountains (2026-11-05)
Run order in game (each is one `/function binbo:<name>`): `palace` → `cv2_0` (clear the ring) → `cv2_1` (ring roads r=72/89, 8 avenues, lamps, fountains) → `cv2_2`, `cv2_3` (166 houses, 5 palettes, hip/dome/tower/flat roofs) → `cv2_4` (wall r=112, 16 copper-dome towers) → `mtn_0..mtn_3` + `mtn_trees` → `cv2_6` (about 200 crowd mannequins). City centre (260, 60), ground y=-60.
- `palace` and `cv2_*` are generator output, copied from the PC. The generator was run inline and its source was not kept.
- `mtn_*` are produced by `gen_mountains.py` (tested in `tests/test_mc_java_mountains.py`). They replace the old `cv2_5`, whose 2×2 single-material columns read as vertical grey stripes.
  - 1×1 value-noise height field: peaks reach 61 blocks; most neighbour steps are ≤2.
  - The ring rises behind the wall and falls away outside, so there is no flat vertical outer face.
  - Surface material by height and slope: grass and dirt, mixed rock patches, calcite and snow on the peaks.
  - Spruce trees on the gentle slopes.
- Reference shots are in `Downloads\Bin_Bo\java_test\city_v3b_*.png`.
- Still weak:
  - Steep cliffs close up show the vertical grain of the stone texture.
  - The south sector is empty.
  - Houses have no interiors.
  - Lighting is vanilla, because there are no shaders.
